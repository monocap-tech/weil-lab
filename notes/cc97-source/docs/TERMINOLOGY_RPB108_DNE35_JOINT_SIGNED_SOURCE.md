# RPB108 — DNE35 terminology: joint signed original source comparison

Definitions are additive. Historical DNE29–DNE34 claims remain unchanged.

**Joint trial packet Z12:** the four exact DNE29 tested columns scaled by DNE31's rational diagonal S, followed by the first eight columns of DNE32's frozen X=hatY U. It has twelve independent retained projections in each parity. Scaling changes coordinates, not the physical trial subspace.

**Joint original native matrix N12:** Q(Z12,Z12). Its tested block is A_s, its remainder block is C_8, and its full mixed native block is (P_s-A_s J)U_[0,8), where J is DNE31's frozen projection and P_s is the authenticated original T4/W52 border. Every mixed entry is retained with an outward interval.

**Joint complete projected source Gram G12:** the matrix <P_F112 L_original Z_i,P_F112 L_original Z_j>, including tested/tested, tested/remainder and remainder/remainder entries. The same analytic source geometry and retained projection subtraction is used for all twelve sources. It includes the unmeasured infinite high tail.

**Signed uniform-high-floor matrix H12:** kappa N12-G12, with the original floor kappa=11/25 on F112. Positive definiteness is a sufficient original all-high Schur certificate. A negative vector of H12 demonstrates that substituting the uniform lower floor for the high inverse is too coarse on this packet; it is not a negative vector of Q or a proof of a null.

**Joint signed failure witness:** an exact rational nonzero trial with a strictly negative paid upper bound for v*H12 v. Floating-point generalized eigenvectors may select the trial but do not certify its sign. The joint original native matrix is separately certified positive as a control.

**Positive joint prefix:** the four tested directions and the first n remainder directions for which the complete principal signed source matrix has a positive rational congruence. This includes actual mixed source entries rather than a separate remainder source allocation below the tested credit c.

**Signed coefficient floor:** if V*H V>=m I is certified and V is rational and invertible, H>=m/||V||_F^2 I. Dividing by kappa bounds the original Schur form in the coefficient mass of the joint packet. This coefficient floor is distinct from the final physical L2 gap.

**Joint all-high physical gap:** a lower bound on Q in the physical norm on span(Z_prefix)+F112, obtained from the signed coefficient floor, exact joint column masses, complete source Gram trace and original high square. It is not whole-aperture positivity unless all 112 retained directions are covered.

**Sharper high-response obligation:** if H12 fails, a proof must avoid that failed uniform-floor substitution on the complete frozen packet. It may retain a matrix or directional high-energy estimate, improve a paid high coercivity bound, evaluate a sharper high inverse response, or change the physical lifted trial frame. An invertible change of coordinates alone cannot change the signed failure.
