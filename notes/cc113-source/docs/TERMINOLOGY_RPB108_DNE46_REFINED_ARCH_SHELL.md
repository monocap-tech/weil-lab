# DNE46 terminology: refined archimedean shells

Registered before DNE46 load-bearing computation. Parent DNE45,
`a9eda40b874163be980da517d3dda87942f83533`.

* **Refined arch shell bound**: the inherited original high-frequency
  multiplier lower b(T)=log(T)-7/(216 T^2), combined with complete F112
  low-frequency mass upper bounds at an increasing rational list of cutoffs.
  Adjacent shell losses telescope; the lowest band retains its 27/5 loss.
* **Fresh positive-region cutoff**: a cutoff satisfying
  (2 a pi T)^2 < 112*113, with a freshly certified positive depth-six
  derivative rate. Passing this condition permits the inherited Bessel
  comparison at that cutoff; the old T<=16 scalar rate is not reused.
* **Transferred positive span**: new positive packet columns whose first
  columns equal DNE44's exact positive embedding. Inclusion is algebraic,
  not a proximity claim between floating-point eigenspaces.

The aperture stays 53/50 and the computed packet stays 44 columns per parity.
New high-floor and retained-rank claims require fresh rational validation.
No original source integration, actual high inverse, whole-aperture
positivity, RH, or Lean closure is asserted by these definitions.
