# RPB108 NF58 terminology

**Even NF57 target** is the first frozen exact rational sign probe, whose
certified lower-matrix enclosure spans zero. It is not a certified negative
witness. Its physical mass and probe provenance are authenticated.

**Odd NF57 target** is an exact rational rejecting witness with certified
negative sufficient lower-bound value and original physical mass.

**Complete target response** uses all P and T53 coordinates through one
shared minorant inverse. The rational approximation selects a high polynomial;
paid signed native/source integrals independently certify its effect.

**Enlarged high family** contains seventeen even or sixteen odd physical
polynomials. The new direction is exactly orthogonal to every inherited
high column on the degree-436/435 shell.

**Resolved target** has a strictly positive independently certified lower-bound
value. This resolves that direction; the complete matrix requires its own
sign proof.

**Correlated gain** cancels the common Xi Gram exactly before evaluating the
positive rank-one inverse-reaction update. Positive gain alone may leave a
target's sign unresolved or negative.

**Complete source custody** preserves historical interval endpoints, physical
errors, endpoint logarithms, signed pole, all 13 prime cells and infinite
remainders. New entries are certified at 800/900-digit precision.

**Whole gate** remains the original shared `D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities.
Its complete sign and whole-aperture status are recorded in the checkpoint
ledger and accompanying note.

**NF58 outcome** clears NF57's odd witness and certifies a positive gain for
NF57's even probe. The even enclosure still spans zero; all 14 newly frozen
even candidates also span zero. A new odd witness rejects the sufficient
lower matrix. These results do not certify an actual negative original
Weil vector. Whole 1.06 remains open, with whole 1.05 as the anchor.

**Gain-to-width ratio** divides the guaranteed correlated gain by the old
target's recorded enclosure width, using exact rational arithmetic. NF58's
even ratio is about 1.016%. It quantifies an enclosure gap, not the actual
form's sign, its sole error source or a convergence rate.

**Recorded target endpoint budget** is an additional guaranteed gain
strictly greater than the negative of a recorded target lower endpoint.
It is sufficient to lift that enclosure, and is neither a necessary gain
for the actual form nor sufficient for positivity of the whole matrix.

**Target normalization** uses a rational upper bound on the original target
norm; a rejecting witness's physical quotient uses its exact original
physical mass squared. Both preserve signs.
