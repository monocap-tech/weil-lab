Write u=x^T G_U_upper x, n=x^T N_upper x, n_low=x^T N_lower x, t_u=x^T U_tail x, t_r=x^T R_tail x, and k=x^T X_upper x, with X_upper the recovered finite source/residual cross upper. Let p be a rigorously rounded-down lower bound for sqrt(t_u t_r).

    W_lower,x >= w_lower=u+n+2k+2p,
    W_upper,x >= w_upper=u+c^2 n+2c k+2c p.

Both w_lower and w_upper are verified nonnegative. Let l_lower<=sqrt(w_lower e) and l_upper<=sqrt(w_upper e) be exact rounded-down square-root bounds.

    theta_lower W_lower,x + (1/(4theta_lower)-1/2)E_x
