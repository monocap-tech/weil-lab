## 1. Two-sided spectral shadow: what follows, and why

Take a provisional first contact a_* after the certified positive interval, Q_(a_*)>=0, dim K=r>0. NF51 implies that any nonnegative earlier window with a kernel would force negativity at every larger window. Such an earlier kernel contradicts the first-contact definition. Thus all a<a_* sufficiently near contact are strictly positive; I+compact upgrades this to canonical coercivity at each named aperture. This strictness uses the retained enlargement theorem, not merely the definition as the first boundary of a negative set.

On a compact neighborhood, retain NF54's uniform shifted coercivity B(a)=A(a)+beta M>=kappa I, M=J*J. Its physical resolvent is

    R(a)=J B(a)^(-1) J*,
    ||R(a)-R(a_*)||<=||J||^2 kappa^(-2)||A(a)-A(a_*)||.

The compact resolvent has exactly r eigenvalues 1/beta at contact; the next is 1/(beta+Delta), Delta=lambda_(r+1)(a_*)>0. Min-max continuity and lambda=1/rho-beta give lambda_j(a)->0 for j<=r and lambda_(r+1)(a)->Delta. Hence on the left 0<lambda_1<=...<=lambda_r and lambda_(r+1)>Delta/2. On the right NF51 and NF54 give precisely r negative modes and the same separated complement. This supplies two-sided continuity, NOT two-sided rate asymptotics. NF53's trial upper bounds are not turned into lower bounds or positive-side rates.

The isolated resolvent projections converge in physical operator norm. The ambient-line statement uses strong dilation continuity on the finite-rank limiting image, not norm continuity of the full dilation group. For cluster mass-one vectors, shifted coercivity bounds the H norm. Weak H compactness, compact J, form continuity and convergence of the positive B(a_*) norm give strong H convergence. Project a fixed basis of K into the cluster and orthonormalize its mass Gram to obtain aligned frames V_a; these frames need not diagonalize L(a). The proof works from either side.

In a fixed larger physical domain D_B, dilation strong continuity and bounded complete actual analysis yield convergence of every original source coordinate in the complete unweighted source norm, with unchanged multiplicities and normalization. The exact source Gram is

    P_a(V_a)*P_a(V_a)-N_a(V_a)*N_a(V_a)=E_a,
    E_a=V_a* L(a) V_a ->0.

E_a is positive definite on the left, zero at contact, and negative definite on the sufficiently close right. No mass term has been replaced by the canonical identity.

The limit basis has the already proved contact derivative flag and endpoint traces. Strong H convergence alone gives neither convergence of global derivatives nor moving endpoint-trace convergence, and supplies no uniform translation/height rate. Smooth canonical approximants of a rough contact vector already disprove such an inference. Bounded fixed-window interpolated trace readouts can converge, but these are not actual endpoint traces of arbitrary approximants. NF49's order-log mixed compensation and NF50's noncommuting height/approximation limits remain intact.

The resolvent/projection/frame conclusions are ordinary compact-resolvent consequences of the existing native architecture. Their actuality rests on the previously attached complete Weil family. They do not constitute a new arithmetic exclusion.

## 2. Explicit form modulus on the registered H=D_1

Let 0<a,b<=B, bounded away from zero. Write w(xi)=log(e+|xi|). Include ALL n with log n<=2B and Lambda(n)!=0, and set c_n=2Lambda(n)/sqrt(n). Define

    d_n(a,b)=|min(log(n)/a,2)-min(log(n)/b,2)|,
    chi(d;R)=max(min(2,2pi R d), 2/log(e+R)), R>0,
    chi_*(d)=inf_(R>0) chi(d;R), chi_*(0)=0.

A reproducible upper bound may choose any explicit R separately for each nonzero d. The clipped displacement is lawful: every compression with shift >=2 is the zero form on supported H, including equality. The exact prime difference is bounded by c_n chi_*(d_n). Indeed below frequency R the cosine difference is <=min(2,2pi R d); above R its absolute value is <=2 and division by w gives the tail bound. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz in both Fourier slots proves the form-operator estimate. This is logarithmic continuity, not operator-norm differentiability of physical translations.

The archimedean bracket is a constant plus Re psi(1/4+i pi xi/a). The pinned actual Euler series permits locally uniform differentiated summation, with derivative tails O(n^-3) on bounded imaginary intervals. For y>0,

    y d/dy Re psi(1/4+iy)
       =sum_(n>=0) 2y^2(n+1/4)/[(n+1/4)^2+y^2]^2 <=5.

For y<=1, the n=0 term is <=2 and the remaining sum is <=2sum_(n>=1)n^-3<=3. For y>=1 the summand as a function of x>0 is unimodal, its integral on (0,infinity) is 1 and its maximum is 9/(8sqrt(3)y)<1. Comparing a shifted unit lattice sum to the integral costs at most twice the maximum, giving <3. These elementary comparisons justify the global bound 5. At xi=0 the difference is zero. Integration in log a therefore bounds the archimedean form difference by 5|log(b/a)|, since physical mass<=H energy. No full-symbol all-derivative assumption is inserted.

The exact pole kernel after mass dilation is 2a cosh(a(t-s)/2), t,s in [-1,1]. Its a derivative is bounded by 2cosh(B)+2B sinh(B). Schur's interval-length bound yields pole difference <=4[cosh(B)+B sinh(B)]|b-a| in physical L2 and hence H form norm. This retains the signed rank-two pole, without asserting pole positivity.

Consequently

    ||A(b)-A(a)|| <= omega(a,b)
      :=5|log(b/a)|
        +4[cosh(B)+B sinh(B)]|b-a|
        +sum_(log n<=2B) c_n chi_*(d_n(a,b)).       (M)

All prime/pole terms, complex slots, support activations and the archimedean correction are retained. Only finitely many actual prime powers occur on this parameter interval. The bound tends to zero, but its explicit constants and radius below go beyond the pre-existing qualitative continuity statement.

## 4. Exact gain dictionary and the near-null projection

In pulled coordinates write c_a||f||_H<=||P_a U_a f||<=C_a||f||_H, and g_a=||N_a P_a^(-1)|| on the ENTIRE closed actual positive range. At a nonnegative aperture,

    (1-g_a^2)c_a^2 <= delta_a <= (1-g_a^2)C_a^2,
    delta_a/C_a^2 <=1-g_a^2<=delta_a/c_a^2.        (G)

These bounds follow by infimizing Q/||f||_H^2 and Q/||P f||^2 respectively; norm attainment of T is not assumed away from contact. For q_a>=kappa||f||_H^2-beta||Jf||^2 and mu_a>0,

    kappa mu_a/(mu_a+beta)<=delta_a<=||J||^2 mu_a.

The upper inequality follows by testing a mass-one lowest eigenvector, whose H norm is >=1/||J||. The lower inequality combines q>=mu mass with the Garding bound. Conversely mu_a>=delta_a/||J||^2. These are inequalities, not equality of canonical and physical spectral levels.

    ||N V_a x||^2/||P V_a x||^2
       =1-(x*E_a x)/(x*S_a x).

Hence all cluster gain ratios tend uniformly to one. More explicitly their defects lie between lambda_1(a)/lambda_max(S_a) and lambda_r(a)/lambda_min(S_a). The restricted gain is

    g_cluster^2=1-lambda_min(S_a^(-1/2) E_a S_a^(-1/2))<=g_a^2.

Thus g_a->1 on the left since g_a<=1. This is a necessary signature and a LOWER bound on g_a, not a contraction upper bound. In source coordinates D_a=I-T_a*T_a=P_a^(-*) A(a) P_a^(-1). Unit gain at contact lies exactly in P_(a_*)K. The physical projector Pi_a and the source metric are distinct; ||V_a x||_2^2=||x||^2 is retained throughout.

There is a local uniform positive-observability bound on any compact positive parameter interval: put all supported vectors in one fixed larger D_B, use its fixed c_B,C_B and dilation weight equivalence. ## 5. Countercontrols, actual arithmetic and the stopping decision

For a generic infinite-dimensional control take H=ell2, compact mass embedding J=diag(d_j^-1/2), d_j=1 for j<=r and d_j growing to infinity afterward. Let s=a_*-a near contact and

    A(a)=diag(s I_r,I), P=I,
    N=diag(sqrt(1-s) I_r,0).

Then A=I+finite-rank, all positive-source observability constants equal one, the physical first r levels equal s, the complement is separated, the projections are constant, the aligned frames and complete channels converge, delta=s and g^2=1-s. It is positive before contact and has exactly r negative levels afterward. This is a rigorous countercontrol to deriving noncontact from spectral continuity, compact resolvent, complete observability, source identity and the approaching-unit signature. It is not the actual Weil family and does not claim its prime/pole or derivative-chain arithmetic.

For the ACTUAL control use the retained lowest positive eigenspace (in particular the rough mass-one mode at a=24/25 with mu>=3e-29). The same boundary/chain, translation hierarchy, sharp/log and mixed-compensation constructions hold for Q_mu=Q-mu mass. Its ORIGINAL source equation is

    ||P h||^2-||N h||^2=mu||h||_2^2,
    shifted negative channel=(N h,sqrt(mu)h).

The shifted gain equals one at that eigenmode, whereas the original ratio is 1-mu||h||_2^2/||P h||^2<1. Every near-cluster or resolvent use keeps this physical mass residual. This control refutes zero-specific conclusions drawn only from the shared endpoint/chain geometry, bounded corrections or height convergence; it does not refute a new inequality using the exact unshifted actual equation.

Full unshifted mixed nullity locates the arithmetic obligation but does not make the exterior source observation vanish. Positive observability prevents denominator collapse and makes the gain signature well-defined; it permits numerator cancellation. The endpoint/derivative structure is inherited at the limit and is shared by shifted positive controls. Quantitative prime/pole compatibility is explicit in (M), but the current margin makes this budget unusable. No actual arithmetic law excluding the approach of the full gain to one has emerged.

One possible genuinely new theorem would bound the cluster source defect below by an actual positive observable that stays bounded below on the limiting kernel, with constants uniform as a approaches contact. That requires an ORIGINAL arithmetic inequality absent here. A separate practical route is a localized Schur perturbation estimate: control the finite near-null block and its coupling while preserving a positive complement gap. Neither is assumed proved merely because it would help.

Outcome: **C for contact exclusion**, plus an effective but practically negligible continuation lemma. The geometry-to-arithmetic route stops here; no indefinite NF-style extension is initiated.
