# RPB108 — DNE38 terminology: joint signed original source comparison

Definitions are additive. Historical DNE29–DNE37 claims remain unchanged.

**Joint trial packet Z28:** the four exact DNE29 tested columns scaled by DNE31's rational diagonal S, followed by the first twenty-four columns of DNE32's frozen X=hatY U. It has twenty-eight independent retained projections in each parity. Scaling changes coordinates, not the physical trial subspace.

**Joint original native matrix N28:** Q(Z28,Z28). Its tested block is A_s, its remainder block is C_24, and its full mixed native block is (P_s-A_s J)U_[0,24), where J is DNE31's frozen projection and P_s is the authenticated original T4/W52 border. Every mixed entry is retained with an outward interval.

**Joint complete projected source Gram G28:** the matrix <P_F112 L_original Z_i,P_F112 L_original Z_j>, including tested/tested, tested/remainder and remainder/remainder entries. The same analytic source geometry and retained projection subtraction is used for all twenty-eight sources. It includes the unmeasured infinite high tail.

**Signed uniform-high-floor matrix H28:** kappa N28-G28, with the original floor kappa=57/100 on F112. Positive definiteness is a sufficient original all-high Schur certificate. A negative vector of H28 demonstrates that substituting the uniform lower floor for the high inverse is too coarse on this packet; it is not a negative vector of Q or a proof of a null.

**Joint signed failure witness:** an exact rational nonzero trial with a strictly negative paid upper bound for v*H28 v. Floating-point generalized eigenvectors may select the trial but do not certify its sign. The joint original native matrix is separately certified positive as a control.

**Positive joint prefix:** the four tested directions and the first n remainder directions for which the complete principal signed source matrix has a positive rational congruence. This includes actual mixed source entries rather than a separate remainder source allocation below the tested credit c.

**Signed coefficient floor:** if V*H V>=m I is certified and V is rational and invertible, H>=m/||V||_F^2 I. Dividing by kappa bounds the original Schur form in the coefficient mass of the joint packet. This coefficient floor is distinct from the final physical L2 gap.

**Joint all-high physical gap:** a lower bound on Q in the physical norm on span(Z_prefix)+F112, obtained from the signed coefficient floor, exact joint column masses, complete source Gram trace and original high square. It is not whole-aperture positivity unless all 112 retained directions are covered.

**Sharper high-response obligation:** if H28 fails, a proof must avoid that failed uniform-floor substitution on the complete frozen packet. It may retain a matrix or directional high-energy estimate, improve a paid high coercivity bound, evaluate a sharper high inverse response, or change the physical lifted trial frame. An invertible change of coordinates alone cannot change the signed failure.

**First failing joint prefix:** the first initial packet beyond the certified positive prefix with a stored rational trial proving a strictly negative paid signed budget. DNE38 stores the exact native, source and signed energies for this trial; a failed numerical congruence by itself does not establish this frontier.

**Incremental coherent source assembly:** the first twenty-column rounded source block and retained coordinates are reused at the same order, precision, physical frame and coefficient grid. Only new columns, new retained coordinates, and all new/new and old/new correlations are integrated. Deterministic reconstruction checks the old rounding ledgers unchanged. Every original-source error payment is recomputed for the assembled matrix.

**Paid required-floor witness:** for a rational trial with positive native energy, G_trial.lower/N_trial.upper is a necessary lower threshold for a positive uniform-floor signed comparison. It is not a proof that every larger floor suffices.

**Unrounded sixth-weight floor control:** the inherited archimedean-minus-pole bound minus the stored maximum Schur-row upper for P^6 1. This stronger paid floor is tested on the same exact trial to distinguish a genuine bound limitation from loss due to choosing the rounded floor 57/100.
