## Exact two-fiber reconstruction

Define

H(t) = f(t),         0 < t < ell,

U(t) = f(a+t),       0 < t < ell.

Boundary propagation gives the complete source decomposition:

1. Head:
   f = H on (0,ell).

2. Zero strip:
   f = 0 on (ell,r).

3. log-3 copy:
   f(r+y) = -beta H(y),  0<y<q.
   Since r+q=a, this fills (r,a).

4. Middle fiber:
   f(a+t) = U(t),  0<t<ell.

5. Opposite copy:
   let w = a+ell = L-a.
   Then
   f(w+y) = beta H(alpha+y),  0<y<q.

6. Opposite zero strip:
   f = 0 on (w+q,c).

7. Tail:
   f(c+t) = -H(t),  0<t<ell.

Thus H and U still determine every source value.

## Why the series-31 scalar recurrence breaks

In series-31 one had u<=k. Therefore the two terms

S(k+t)

and

H(t-k)

never appeared, and the p/j elimination closed on residue chains modulo

h = j-p = log(81/80).

In the present chamber u>k. Both k-shift terms are active.

The new scale is

k = log(16/15).

It is incommensurate with the old recurrence scale h.

Indeed, if k/h were rational, say nk = mh with positive integers m,n, then

(16/15)^n = (81/80)^m.

Comparing the exponent of 5 gives n=m. Comparing the exponent of 2 then gives

4n = -4m,

forcing n=m=0, contradiction.

Hence

k/h is irrational.

Therefore a k-shift moves data between different h-residue chains. Repeated closure under the two shifts no longer produces a finite one-dimensional chain; the additive subgroup generated by h and k is dense in R.

This is the structural transition at L=log(16/3).
