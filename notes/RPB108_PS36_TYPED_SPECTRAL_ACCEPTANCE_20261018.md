## 1. Types and composition are part of the mathematics, not presentation

A positivity object must carry its original operator, physical support, quadratic form domain/norm, parity, signed primes/poles, source elimination and residual coupling, quantifier, and proof/evidence scope. Permitted tiers, *not* automatic implications, are:

1. **COMPONENT:** a local window, one Fourier packet, a pole term, a finite test vector, a source module.

3. **COMPLETE-COMPLEMENT:** the genuine source-eliminated Schur on the entire supported complementary carrier, including all low/high and cross-response couplings.

5. **WHOLE-SIGNED-WEIL:** composed original even + odd form on the original parity-invariant full domain.
6. **NATIVE-TRANSFER/RH:** independent accepted CC27 D-Riesz domain/norm and original explicit-formula/RH implications.

A theorem at tier (1) or (2) cannot be cast to (3) or (4) by shrinking residual errors without a **complete domain-preserving coupling/Schur argument**. A complete parity result must not be called a whole-form theorem absent both parities. Passing GitHub Actions, an exact-rational finite toy, a conditional spectral identity, or local positive windows do not certify an unmeasured infinite-dimensional eigenvalue.

\[

Partial window positivity without this full cross is not admissible evidence.
