## 2. Why foreign realization theory is relevant

Several mature theories treat zeros and poles of a single transfer/Weyl object
as coupled spectral data rather than unrelated divisors. Their conclusions are
powered by additional realization structure such as:

- Herglotz/Nevanlinna positivity;
- spectral-measure representations;
- self-adjoint or Pontryagin-space realizations;
- rank-one perturbation laws;
- interlacing and two-spectra uniqueness;
- canonical-system phase structure;
- rational/infinite-dimensional system realization;
- finite-rank or sparse moment structure.

The working hypothesis of IRT-0 is not that any of these applies to zeta.
It is that one of them may expose the precise *extra hypothesis* required to
turn zero-side data into pole-side weighted control.

That hypothesis can then be:

1. proved for a zeta-derived carrier;
2. shown equivalent to an already known obstruction;
3. shown to be RH-strength and therefore unusable;
4. or used to prove that this realization route also TSTOPs.

All four outcomes are useful.

## 3. First screen families

### A. Weyl (m)-function / Herglotz / two-spectra

Search for exact reconstruction laws in which poles and zeros of one
meromorphic Herglotz/Weyl function encode two spectra or a spectrum plus a
boundary perturbation.

Extract:

- minimal hypotheses;
- whether complete data or local data suffice;
- weighted-functional reconstruction formulas;
- conditioning/interlacing requirements.

### B. Rank-one perturbation / Clark-type spectral measures

Search for settings where Möbius transforms of one analytic object produce
different point spectra/spectral measures with explicit cross-relations.

Extract the exact mechanism coupling the two point sets.

### C. Generalized Nevanlinna (N_kappa) / Pontryagin spaces

Search for realizations allowing finitely many negative squares.

Determine whether finite negative index can encode controlled failure of
ordinary positivity while retaining a usable two-divisor reconstruction
theorem.

Do not assume the zeta carrier has finite negative index.

### D. Canonical systems / de Branges

Search for phase/spectral transfer theorems in which one entire function or
canonical system generates multiple spectra and a Weyl object.

Audit every positivity/real-zero hypothesis for RH-strength circularity.
