## Minimal finite carrier theorem (analytic proof)

Fix \(a>0\). For every finitely supported raw coefficient family u, define its fiber aggregate
\[
b_\rho=\sum_{j<m_\rho}u_{\rho,j}.
\]
Then
\[
Gu=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
b_\rho=0\ \text{for every actual point }\rho.
\]
Here G is the actual whole Green graph synthesis, not a substituted physical-only operator.

Proof. The physical coordinate of Gu is the finite sum \(\sum_\rho b_\rho g_{z_\rho}\), where g_z is the supported endpoint-corrected Green column. Inside \((-a,a)\), each g_z is smooth and satisfies
\[
(-d^2/dx^2+\tfrac14)g_z(x)=e^{-izx}.
\]
The actual open-strip bound certifies every denominator \(1/4+z_\rho^2\ne0\).

If Gu=0, its physical finite sum vanishes in L2. The sum is continuous inside the interval, hence vanishes everywhere there: a nonzero value would give a neighborhood with positive L2 mass. Applying the pointwise differential expression to this finite smooth identity gives
\[
\sum_\rho b_\rho e^{-iz_\rho x}=0\quad(-a<x<a).
\]
Distinct actual points give distinct complex frequencies \(-iz_\rho\). The existing finite exponential independence theorem, now specialized to actual points in Lean, implies each b_\rho=0. No spectral operator-domain premise was used; this is differentiation of an explicit finite smooth formula.

Conversely, identical copies give identical whole graph packets. Grouping the finite graph sum by point shows that vanishing aggregates imply Gu=0, retaining both source coefficient coordinates as well as the physical coordinate.

Therefore finite raw multiplicity differences are the entire finite synthesis kernel. The finite carrier has actual independent Green packet directions indexed by distinct zero points. This does not claim independence of arbitrary infinite coefficient series.

## Exact copy-fiber Hilbert quotient (analytic proof)

Define the weighted point space
\[
H_{\mathrm{pt}}=\left\{b:\sum_\rho |b_\rho|^2/m_\rho<\infty\right\},\qquad
\|b\|_{\mathrm{pt}}^2=\sum_\rho |b_\rho|^2/m_\rho.
\]
Fiber aggregation A maps raw lp2 onto this space. Cauchy–Schwarz in each finite fiber gives
\[
|b_\rho|^2/m_\rho\le\sum_j|u_{\rho,j}|^2.
\]
The canonical minimum-norm lift is \(u_{\rho,j}=b_\rho/m_\rho\); it attains equality in every fiber. Thus
\[
\ell^2(\text{raw copies})/\ker A\ \cong_{\rm unitary}\ H_{\mathrm{pt}}.
\]
The copy-fiber kernel ker A is exactly the closed span of finite same-point differences: approximate any vector in ker A by truncating to finitely many complete fibers; each finite zero-sum fiber is generated by differences of coordinate unit vectors.

The actual bounded graph synthesis annihilates this closed span by the certified copy identities and continuity. It therefore factors through A to a bounded \(\bar G:H_{\mathrm{pt}}\to H_{\mathrm{graph}}\), preserving the unchanged graph packets. Consequently
\[
\ell^2(\text{raw copies})/\ker G
\ \cong_{\rm unitary}\
H_{\mathrm{pt}}/\ker\bar G
\]
with inherited coefficient quotient norms. This does not identify that norm with the graph image norm; their completed observation carrier still uses the already-audited image norm.

The finite theorem proves \(\ker\bar G\cap c_{00}=\{0\}\). It does not prove \(\ker\bar G=\{0\}\). Distinct-point infinite-series cancellation is a separate question.

For logical clarity, even a Hilbert–Schmidt synthesis can have independent finite columns and an infinite relation. In ell2 with coordinates 0,1,2,..., take nonzero d_n=2^-n and alpha_n=2^-n. Set G e_n=d_n e_n for n>=1 and G e_0=sum_n d_n alpha_n e_n. The column norms are square summable, all finite column subsets are independent (the e_0 column has an infinite nonzero tail), yet G(1,-alpha_1,-alpha_2,...)=0. This is an abstract counterexample to a proposed inference, not a claim about actual zeta.

## The counting/completeness obstruction

In a height window, the number of distinct complex ordinate parameters is exactly the number of actual zero points N_pt(T). The raw divisor count is
\[
N_{\rm mult}(T)=\sum_{|\operatorname{Im}\rho|\le T}m_\rho.
\]
Value vanishing at every raw copy is precisely value vanishing once at every point; it does not imply an observation function has order at least m_\rho there.

Accordingly, a Jensen uniqueness argument for a finite-exponential-type observation function must compare its zero count with N_pt(T), unless independent jet vanishing has been established. Multiplicity growth alone does not establish this comparison. The present actual Jensen/divisor growth stack provides upper bounds and summability, not a distinct-point superlinear lower bound or new observation jets.

A concrete sufficient arithmetic input for the counting route would be unbounded N_pt(T)/T. Even after proving that input, one must show that the particular annihilator transform is entire of the required finite type, that its vanishing forces the relevant vector to vanish, and that the resulting topology is the one needed. Physical completeness, logarithmic completeness and full source graph completeness are different claims. No one of them is inferred here.
