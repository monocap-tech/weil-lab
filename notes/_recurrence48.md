## 2. Dense h/k drift

Since h/k is irrational, the additive subgroup

is dense in R.

Therefore for every nonempty open interval I in R and every fixed integer N,

meets I.

Because h,k,p are Q-linearly independent, all triples

have unique integer coordinates (N,a,b).

Thus arbitrarily large p-provenance can occur at distinct physical coordinates inside any fixed open support interval.
