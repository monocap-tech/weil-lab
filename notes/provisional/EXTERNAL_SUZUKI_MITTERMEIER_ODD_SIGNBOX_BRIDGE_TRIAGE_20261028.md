## Verified primary source, rather than a title-level analogy

`<D psi_1, D psi_2>_{G_g,a} = W(psi_1 * tilde(psi_2))`

for smooth compactly supported `psi_i` in `[-a,a]`, with `g=-Psi`, `Psi(0)=0`, `Psi(-t)=Psi(t)`, and **equation (1.4)** `G_g(t,u)=g(t-u)-g(t)-g(-u)+g(0)`. This gives the unconditional algebraic formula

`G_g(t,u)=Psi(t)+Psi(u)-Psi(t-u)`.

Suzuki's kernel is a compact/trace-class operator on its L2 representation (Theorem 1.5), whereas the original Fourier-signed Weil multiplier grows logarithmically; their equality is a **form relation through D**, not an ordinary bounded-L2 unitary equivalence.
