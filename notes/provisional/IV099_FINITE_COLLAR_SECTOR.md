The receiver works with arbitrary finite index sets and complex coefficients. It proves exact physical squared mass and derivative energy sums, coefficient nondegeneracy from positive individual masses, and the full original signed finite Weil lower bound (2/5 - C/(N+1)^2) times physical squared mass under a common derivative-energy/mass bound C. For N with C <= (N+1)^2/5 the margin is 1/5.

Explicit premises: original collars, pairwise pointwise disjoint functions and derivatives, individual energy budgets, arithmetic cutoff; nondegeneracy also requires positive individual masses. No arithmetic or Gamma mixed terms are dropped. This does not construct arbitrary-rank families or certify global positivity. A separate explicit scaled/translated plateau construction must discharge the premises at each target rank, and its C may grow with rank. No adoption or merge.
