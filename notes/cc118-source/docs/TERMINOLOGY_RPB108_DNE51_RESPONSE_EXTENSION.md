# DNE51 response extension

The optimized fixed-trial budget at scalar parameter t is
H_opt(t)=t*N-G+(B-t*M)*(D-t*A)^(-1)*(B-t*M)^*, whenever D-t*A>0.
It is the largest budget obtainable by any coefficient matrix J in this
fixed physical trial space. It is a sufficient comparison, not the original
Schur matrix. A negative exact trial of H_opt excludes all choices of J in
this comparison at t. Hypothetical parameters are not certified high floors.

A packet-tail recycling chart expresses every physical high part of the
56 retained packet columns in the existing three even high trial vectors.
Its exact identities show that taking more high tails from this packet
cannot enlarge the trial space.

The proposed extension adds physical Legendre vectors e112,e114,e116 to
the three existing even trials and builds three odd trials from the high
parts of the nonconstant tested columns. These are exact high vectors only;
their new original source actions, native pairings, and response credits
must be computed and independently paid before use.
