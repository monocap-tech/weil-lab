# RC203 terminology: residual-enriched six-source inverse

The operator is the actual supported canonical M=D^-1 L at aperture B=11/10, in D energy. Response sources remain P0,P2,f0,psi0,w14,v10 in that order. The even raw trial basis is P0,P2,f0,psi0,w14,q185,v10,q202. Definitions of q202 and its input/action errors are in the RC202 terminology registry.

| Term | Definition and scope |
|---|---|
| Eight-trial enrichment | Addition of exact finite q202 to the seven inherited trial directions. It enlarges the inverse trial space without adding a response source or changing the native frame. |
| Source mixed row | Full D pairing of q202 with every trial direction. Since q202 is finite, these entries have no omitted source tail. |
| Canonical mixed row | Actual pairing of q202 and each trial under M. Intersect both self-adjoint orientations after paying their action uncertainties and any signed output tail. |
| Represented-action mixed row | D pairing of the stored complete action carriers, including their infinite output tails. This differs from the actual action pairing, whose error is paid separately. |
| Signed q202 tail | The25 exact endpoint moments retained by RC202. Its leading moment is kept in the residual cancellation; absolute nonleading moments bound the remainder. |
| Eight-term residual tail payment | After separating the common eta leading term, eight remainder terms are bounded by a rational sum-of-squares inequality. Their coefficient is24 times the common eta tail energy, replacing RC199's21 for seven terms. |
| Same-family inverse width | Certified Loewner width relative to RC201's unchanged six-source G_lower. It can be compared directly with RC201's1.823489. |
| Half-Gram inverse gate | Exact PSD test of(1/2)G_lower minus the inverse-enclosure gap. Its ratio upper is twice the certified width; a positive guaranteed margin is1/2 minus that width. |

Norm errors, squared residual/error factors, inverse matrix widths and true inverse uncertainty are distinct quantities. A failed enclosure does not prove that the true inverse gate is impossible. This canonical contract does not identify the original Stage I source range or certify augmented Weil positivity. Stage I,RH,F4 and Lean closure remain open/unclaimed.
