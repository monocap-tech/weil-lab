# RPB108 terminology: pole Weyl invisibility

Introduced 2026-10-07 UTC, global/F4. Historical symbols are unchanged.

- H,A,c,s,Z: physical actual objects of POLE_RESONANCE_COMPATIBILITY.
- B:C^2->L2(I_a), B(u,v)=u c+v s. This is the two actual pole profiles, not complete zeta source analysis Gamma_a or a selected negative-source synthesis.
- M(z)=B^*(H-z)^(-1)B, Im z!=0: unnormalized two-profile compressed resolvent. Parity makes it diagonal with entries E(z),O(z). It is not assumed to be a boundary-triplet Weyl function or a Donoghue deficiency-space m-function.
- V_pole=closed span{(H-z)^(-1)c,(H-z)^(-1)s: Im z!=0}: physical resolvent-cyclic subspace of the two pole profiles.
- d_pole(z)=det(I+diag(2,-2)M(z))=(1+2E(z))(1-2O(z)): rank-two perturbation determinant. It tracks the change from H to A, not automatically the absolute full-native nullity.
- Invisible subspace: V_pole-perpendicular. At negative-base nonnegative contact, Z=ker H lies in it, and P and A also vanish on Z.
- Isoweyl controls: explicitly specified finite weighted spaces with identical M(z) as rational functions and different full kernel dimensions. They are not actual zeta or continuum logarithmic models.
