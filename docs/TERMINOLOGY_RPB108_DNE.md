# Direct Null Exclusion (DNE): terminology and custody

**DNE** is the independent mathematical investigation of whether a nonzero exact zero-energy vector of the complete, original Riemann-Weil quadratic form can occur at a first-contact aperture.

**DNE-0** is the organizational separation checkpoint (branch and charter), NOT a mathematical theorem. The first new mathematical result, if any, is numbered DNE-1.

**Ownership.** DNE owns direct contact/null-equation research. Coupled Continuation (CC) remains the integration authority for the combined global continuation theorem. The Aperture/NF branch independently owns finite-aperture numerical/native positivity certificates. Paused Global/F4 and Pre-Contact Shadow remain read-only dependencies.

**Original contact** means: a finite aperture a with full original native Q_a nonnegative on the complete supported logarithmic canonical domain D_a, and a nonzero h in D_a such that Q_a(h,f)=0 for every f in D_a. Do not infer pointwise boundary values, an unrestricted Fourier-multiplier operator domain, RH, or any target-aperture positivity from this definition.

**Parity pole-free form H_a** is original Q_a minus only its two Hermitian signed pole cross terms; it retains the complete archimedean digamma form and every active prime-power pair with both translation orientations.

**Even/odd contact thresholds.** On the corresponding real parity spaces, Q_even=H_even+2|c|² and Q_odd=H_odd-2|s|², with c(h)=integral h cosh(x/2) and s(h)=integral h sinh(x/2). The extended-real constrained moment-one minima beta_e, beta_o have contact thresholds -2 and +2. The odd susceptibility R_o=2 sup |s(h)|²/H_odd(h) is used ONLY when H_odd is positive coercive; do not assume an inverse when a pole-free kernel might exist. Moment-zero nulls require separate analysis.

**Direct exclusion goal.** Eliminate ALL original first-contact cases without importing the conclusion, assuming positivity beyond contact, or requiring CC19's old-gap-independent shell leakage as a mandatory intermediate. Accept other lawful independent number-theoretic estimates if proved with the actual zeta data.

**Handoff rule.** A DNE result is independently published on its own branch, with precise hypotheses, analytic status, control tests and provenance. CC may cite or incorporate it later by an additive handoff; publishing DNE does not modify, reset, overwrite, cherry-pick or redirect CC/Aperture/Global/Shadow branches. Historical CC40-CC43 result numbers and wording remain immutable.
