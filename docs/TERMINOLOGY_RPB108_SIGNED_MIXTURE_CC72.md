# CC72 terminology: directional source mixtures and inverse-response credit

These definitions precede their use. Historical wording is unchanged.

At a=53/50, E=E112, F=E-perp, and C>=kappa I is the original coercive high form with kappa=207/1000. NF26's trial v has retained component x. NF27's exact rational retained response w lies in E and satisfies physical orthogonality <x,w>=0.

The **mixed source Gram** Gamma has entries <r_i,r_j> for r_v=P_F L v and r_w=P_F L w. It uses the physical L2 metric. It is not the inverse-response Gram T with entries <r_i,C^-1 r_j>.

The **coarse joint score matrix** is U=Q2-Gamma/kappa, where Q2 is the original native energy matrix of (v,w). It is a sufficient lower matrix for the original retained Schur form on their retained components. An indefinite U does not prove an indefinite true Schur form.

A **retained mixture trial** is u_alpha=v-alpha w with retained direction z_alpha=x-alpha w, for fixed rational alpha. Its **directional coarse score** is f(alpha)=U_vv-2alpha U_vw+alpha^2 U_ww. If a paid lower bound is positive, the original Schur value on z_alpha is positive. This changes the retained direction; it is not merely another high correction of x.

A **signed-source-selected weight** is a rational weight frozen from the midpoint optimum of the indefinite coarse quadratic. Its positivity and improvement are proved separately with the full outward intervals. It is not claimed to be the exact optimum of the true inverse-response problem.

The **inverse-response credit** is Delta=Gamma/kappa-T. Coercivity implies Delta>=0. Positivity of the true w Schur diagonal requires Delta_ww>-U_ww. This necessary diagonal credit alone does not certify the mixed matrix or the full retained background.
