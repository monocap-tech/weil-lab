# Terminology registry — RPB-108 source quadratic diagonal

The **source quadratic diagonal** on the retained domain is the real
normalized multiplier energy plus
`2 * Re(conj(Mminus(f)) * Mplus(f))`. This is the Hermitian complex extension
of the real pole quadratic expression. It is not the unconjugated complex
product `2 * Mminus(f) * Mplus(f)`.

The **shifted comparison attachment** supplies the retained lower and upper
logarithmic bounds on the exact normalized symbol plus a real scalar shift.
The absolute-symbol bound needed for mixed convergence is derived from these
bounds with constant `|upperC| + |shift|`. It is not an additional analytic
assumption.

The **source-diagonal comparison** identifies a specified source form with
the constructed multiplier-plus-pole form from equality with this explicit
real quadratic diagonal on the same retained domain. It does not supply an
instance of the historical source domain or a regular residual witness.

Registration: RPB-108 continuation, 2026-10-01 (America/Los_Angeles).
