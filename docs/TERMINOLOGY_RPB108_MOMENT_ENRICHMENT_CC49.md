# Exact moment-enriched split (CC49)

On a fixed parity cap, w is the physical profile cosh(x/2) or sinh(x/2),
zero-extended outside the cap, and m(h)=<w,h>_physical. Starting with
a finite Legendre space E, enrich it to Eplus=E+span{w}. The high form
carrier Fplus is physically orthogonal to Eplus. Z=Eplus intersect ker m
is the retained constrained low space. The compensated tail is the
normalized high physical moment tail minus a multiple of the normalized
low moment profile, chosen so that its EXACT total moment is zero.
Enrichment is a change of certificate coordinates, not a new positivity
theorem, source norm, arithmetic gain or old source defect.
