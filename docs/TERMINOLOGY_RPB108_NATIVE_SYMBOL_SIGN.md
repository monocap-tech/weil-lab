# Actual native scalar-sign terminology

| Term | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| Native scalar symbol | The existing threshold-corrected archimedean-minus-prime multiplier in Mathlib's Fourier frequency coordinate. Pole/evaluation and selected energy are separate. |
| Scalar sign obstruction | Strict negativity of that multiplier at zero, and on a neighborhood, which excludes global pointwise multiplier nonnegativity. |
| Supported background sign | The sign of the integrated multiplier plus cross-pole and selected energy on the unchanged supported Green graph vector. Scalar negativity does not decide it. |
| Pointwise positivity shortcut | Attempt to prove the background inequality solely by global nonnegativity of the scalar multiplier. The actual scalar-sign theorem rules out this route. |
