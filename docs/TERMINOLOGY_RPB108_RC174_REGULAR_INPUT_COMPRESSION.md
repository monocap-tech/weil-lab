The **high-input-mode regular bound** controls R(I-Pi_N) in physical L2, where Pi_N is physical Legendre projection through degree N. Polynomial orthogonality controls low-frequency Fourier content; multiplier decay controls high frequencies. It is a full supported-input operator bound, not a sampled finite matrix.

The **seven-source compressed regular map** is D^-1 Rhat Pi_16382 on the historical seven-source physical family. Its full correlated error relative to D^-1 R is below 0.000128810 times source D norm. Rhat is RC170's paid Poisson operator. The composed action map's self-adjointness is not assumed.

The **f0 finite-input component budget** bounds only D^-1 R applied to the discarded part of the exact finite source f0. Degree 172 meets a 10^-4 absolute D-norm component budget. Poisson evaluation, output projection and other canonical action terms require their separate payments.

Input compression applies to R only. It does not discard the endpoint action, the identity term, or -r D^-1 source tails. Neither the compressed regular map nor its error contract is a completed canonical inverse or an original augmented response.
