# CC86 response innovation terminology

This is a conditional CC integration result at aperture 53/50, attached to NF44. The standing remaining-background hypothesis is A >= (207/1000) I. It is not newly proved.

* **Starting minorant Aj:** the six-high-source lower operator from NF44.
* **Defect D:** A-Aj, a positive operator under the standing hypothesis.
* **Candidate span Y:** a finite family of original high polynomials, on which every native image, signed cross and source error must be paid.
* **Complete denominator B:** Y*DY + (DY)*Aj^-1(DY), positive definite after removing dependent defect columns.
* **Response P:** (DY)*Aj^-1 R, where R is the retained-to-high cross operator.
* **Condensed deficit -s:** the negative scalar left after eliminating the positive leading two-by-two block E of the retained lower certificate K.
* **Response metric M:** B + PE E^-1 PE*, with PE the first two columns of P.
* **Response border t:** p_last - PE E^-1 r, with r the leading-to-last border of K.
* **Span score theta:** t* M^-1 t. Its units and normalization match -s.
* **Innovation:** the extra span score supplied by a candidate after conditioning on already selected candidates in M. Physical L2 orthogonality does not imply orthogonality in M.
* **Finite-span exhaustion:** a proved upper bound theta <= -s, which excludes a passing combination only in that paid candidate span and for this minorant certificate.

Six high-source columns are not the six retained directions. A failed lower certificate is not negativity of the original Weil form.
