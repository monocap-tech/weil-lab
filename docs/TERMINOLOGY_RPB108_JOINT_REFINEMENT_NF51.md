# RPB108 NF51 terminology

**Recovered checkpoint** is the verified NF50 Native Source commit `c576e34`.
Recovery preserves original archive bytes and all historical columns and
records. NF51 adds new work after that checkpoint.

**Full joint response** means the response to `R alpha+G beta` for the unchanged
P and T53 families through one shared high inverse. Its sources retain all
original endpoint, signed pole, prime-cell and infinite-remainder payments.

**Enlarged high family** means ten even or nine odd physical high polynomials.
NF51 adds one exactly orthogonal rational direction to each NF50 family.

**Lifted NF50 witness** means its sufficient lower-bound value is now strictly
positive, independently certified. It does not certify the entire matrix.

**New rejecting witness** means an exact rational vector has strictly negative
value under the sufficient enlarged lower matrix. It is not a negative original
Weil vector or a counterexample to whole positivity.

**Whole gate** remains the original shared high-condensed
`D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities. Whole positivity at 1.06, RH, F4 and Lean
remain open; the highest certified whole-aperture anchor remains 1.05.
