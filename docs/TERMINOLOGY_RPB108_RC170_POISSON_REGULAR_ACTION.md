# RC170 terminology: positive Poisson regular action

The **Poisson regular quadrature** is a finite positive sum of supported Poisson convolution operators approximating the actual regular canonical-metric multiplier R=chi F^-1 log(1+e/abs(xi)) F chi. Its operator error includes small-t and large-t tails, logarithmic midpoint error, and all node, coefficient and step rounding.

The **supported physical action contract** controls every physical L2 input supported on [-11/10,11/10]. It yields a full correlated error-Gram contract after D^-1. It does not require source-by-source diagonal error assembly.

The **represented constant-source action** is the explicit finite arctangent sum Rhat P0. Its entire interval error and three directed sample enclosures are certified. It has not yet been converted into finite/tail D coefficients for subsequent endpoint actions.

R is the bounded regular part of the canonical metric L. It is distinct from the original Weil arithmetic remainder and from the original positive augmentation. Paying this quadrature does not close a canonical inverse iteration or Stage I.
