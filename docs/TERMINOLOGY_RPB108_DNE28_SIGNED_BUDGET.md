# RPB108 — DNE28 terminology: signed matrix source payment

Definitions are additive. Historical documents and certificates are unchanged.

**Original four-column frame T:** the DNE23 frame (low,v,t,u32), separately in each reflection parity. Its retained components span the same eight-dimensional Z8. F112 is the entire original infinite high space. The original high operator C satisfies C>=kappa I, kappa=11/25, by DNE17.

**Native matrix Q4:** the complete original Weil energy matrix of T, including its signed low/lift row. Its entries come from CC62's low diagonal, DNE23's paid original low/lift pairings and NF32's complete three-column native matrix.

**Complete source Gram Gamma4:** the physical Gram of the projected original sources R=(r0,R3) on F112. Gamma3=R3*R3 is authenticated in NF32. The low-source square is bounded by P0 from CC62. The three signed low-source crosses are not evaluated here.

**Coherent source majorant D_tau:** diag((1+1/tau)P0,(1+tau)Gamma3), with a positive rational tau. For every mixed coefficient (a,z), complete source Young gives Gamma4<=D_tau. D_tau has zero low/three-column entries; this does not assert that Gamma4 has zero crosses.

**Signed source credit c:** a positive rational with (kappa-c)Q4-D_tau>0. It implies Gamma4<(kappa-c)Q4. c is a native energy source budget, not a physical L2 gap and not a measured remaining source ratio.

**Congruence scale S:** diag(1,s1,s2,s3), using DNE23's positive exact rational scales. All interval matrix comparisons are made after S congruence and retain signed off-diagonal entries.

**Outward interval LDL payment:** rational interval operations rounded outward after each operation on a decimal grid. All four pivot lower endpoints are strictly positive. This is a sufficient robust matrix certificate, not floating eigenvalue evidence.

**Independent matrix-box vertex payment:** every vertex of a symmetric four-by-four entry box passes exact rational Sylvester tests. Any matrix in the box is a convex combination of those positive definite vertex matrices. This independently proves positivity throughout the box, including matrices with correlated actual errors.

**Physical diagonal lower ell:** a positive four-coordinate diagonal with S(Q4-D_tau/kappa)S>diag(ell). The original coarse source form Q4-Gamma4/kappa is at least this lower form. Physical masses of the inverse-completed columns and every high vector are then paid separately.

**Remaining condition:** DNE25's exact native energy orthogonal remainder Y keeps its definition. The strict complete inequality c B_Y-Gamma_Y>0 would suffice for whole 1.06 positivity. A frozen rational remainder can instead pay r+kappa epsilon<c. Neither remaining inequality is evaluated in DNE28.
