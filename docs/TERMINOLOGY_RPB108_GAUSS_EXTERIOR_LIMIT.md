# RPB-108 exterior Gauss convergence supplement

**Exterior Gauss error bound** means the actual finite physical convolution
differs from the negative of the signed gap function by at most
M exp(-2δ)^N / (1-exp(-2δ)), where δ=a-c>0 and M is the actual compact
carrier L1 mass. The bound is uniform over x outside (-a,a).

**Exterior finite convolution limit** means pointwise convergence at every
such x, derived from that quantitative bound. It does not identify the
archimedean source action: the actual shifted digamma tail still needs its
own support-separated weak/operator estimate and limit.
