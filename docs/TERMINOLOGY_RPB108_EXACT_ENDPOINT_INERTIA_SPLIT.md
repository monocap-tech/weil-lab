# RPB108 terminology: exact endpoint inertia split

- **Old positive complement:** kernel-orthogonal part of the old logarithmic domain, on which the nonnegative endpoint form is strictly coercive.
- **New-test complement:** the logarithmic orthogonal complement of the old domain in the enlarged domain; not literal physical collar support.
- **Kernel coupling range:** range F of the actual enlarged form coupling B from the finite endpoint kernel.
- **Reduced new-test remainder:** compression of C-V* A0^(-1)V to ker B*, after the explicitly nonsingular finite coupling block is removed.
