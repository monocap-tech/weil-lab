# RPB108 native remainder forcing terminology

CC33, 2026-10-08. Complete normalized original native/source form.

- **Canonical leading form:** E_log(h,f)=integral log(e+|xi|)
  conjugate(Fourier h) Fourier f, the D_t inner product.
- **Native remainder symbol:** r_arch(xi)=Re psi(1/4+i*pi*xi)-log pi
  -log(e+|xi|). It is real bounded and tends to zero at infinity.
- **Physical remainder:** R_B is the bounded selfadjoint physical L2
  operator representing Q-E_log on a fixed supported cap, including
  all its prime shifts and both pole terms. The full Q is not claimed
  represented by a bounded physical L2 operator.
- **Canonical remainder:** C_t=i_t* R_B i_t, where i_t:D_t->L2[-B,B]
  is the physical inclusion. Q on D_t is represented by I+C_t.
- **Old canonical projection:** Pi^D_st projects D_t onto its supported
  closed subspace D_s. It is neither a spatial-strip projection nor the
  old positive-source projection in V_t.
- **Native remainder forcing:** q_i=(I-Pi^D_st)C_t h_i for the exact
  source-normalized old critical lift. Its complete positive-inverse
  norm is the scalar arithmetic quantity isolated in CC33.
- **Riesz forced row:** j_i=J_i*1=(I+C_t-delta_i M_t)h_i, with
  M_t=P_t*P_t. Exact old criticality gives Pi^D_st j_i=0.

Compactness of C_t concerns the native remainder on the canonical domain;
it is not compactness of N, does not supply a defect factor, and is not
used to reopen a generic compactness or source-cutoff continuation route.
