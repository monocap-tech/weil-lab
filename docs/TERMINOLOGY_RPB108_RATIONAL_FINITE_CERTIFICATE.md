# RPB108 rational finite certificate terminology

- **Rational enclosure:** rational interval proved to contain a real entry or constant; outward grid rounding preserves containment.
- **Positive pivot certificate:** rational interval Schur elimination whose pivot lower endpoints are all positive. This proves finite matrix positive definiteness, not a smallest-eigenvalue bound equal to the smallest pivot.
- **Certified finite restriction:** positivity on the explicitly listed trial span. It does not certify the entire logarithmic carrier or close WD-T10 globally.

These definitions are additive and do not alter historical terminology.
