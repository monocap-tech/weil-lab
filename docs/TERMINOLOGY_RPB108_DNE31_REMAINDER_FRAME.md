# RPB108 — DNE31 terminology: complete finite native border

Definitions are additive. Prior certificates retain their original scope.

**Optimized tested frame T4:** DNE29's low column and three selected rational polynomial lifts in one reflection parity. Its complete F112 source contraction has credit c=11/100 even and 7/50 odd at the original high floor kappa=11/25.

**Raw remainder W52:** DNE24's exact rational physical orthogonal complement to the four retained tested directions. It has 52 columns in each parity. Its free-coordinate minor is the identity. Its physical mass G_W=W52*W52 is generally not the identity, but G_W>=I in these coordinates.

**Native tested matrix A4 and border P:** A4=Q(T4,T4), P=Q(T4,W52), for the complete original unshifted Weil form. These are finite native energies before any infinite high square completion. They are not the Schur matrix or a retained-only physical source projection.

**Scaled native coordinates:** S is DNE29's positive diagonal column scale; A_s=S A4 S and P_s=S P. The inherited native lower A_s>q I has q=1/25 even and 7/50 odd. Tightening authenticated entry boxes preserves that lower theorem.

**Frozen projection J and K:** J is a rational approximation to A_s^-1 P_s; K=S J. No approximation is treated as an exact inverse. The frozen optimized remainder is hatY=W52-T4 K. Its native border is E_s=P_s-A_s J=S Q(T4,hatY).

**Border residual payment f2:** f2 is the sum of squared absolute maxima of all 208 outward interval entries of E_s. It bounds the squared Frobenius norm and hence the squared operator norm of the complete mixed border, including every remaining coordinate.

**Exact projection error payment:** With J0=A_s^-1 P_s, ||J0-J||^2<=f2/q^2. This bounds a finite native coefficient map in scaled coordinates; it does not evaluate the original infinite high inverse or give a physical form gap.

**Native dual border relative to raw W mass:** Since G_W>=I and A_s>=q I, ||A_s^-1/2 E_s G_W^-1/2||^2<=f2/q. This refers to raw W mass, not to the uncomputed native energy of hatY.

**Conditional required remainder floor:** If Q(hatY)>=b G_W with b>16 kappa^2 f2/(q c^2), then its native mixed dual norm is less than c/(4 kappa). Coupled with the still-unproved complete source estimate Gamma_hatY<=(c/2)Q(hatY), DNE25's rational criterion gives whole-aperture positivity. The displayed required b is a sufficient input threshold, not a proved remainder floor.
