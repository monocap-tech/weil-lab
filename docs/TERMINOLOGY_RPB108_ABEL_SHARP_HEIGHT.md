# RPB108: Abel critical defect and sharp divisor-height head

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Definitions precede load-bearing use in the companion note.

- u_q=|theta_q| and d_q(h)=|p_q(h)|^2-|n_q(h)|^2 use the complete ACTUAL divisor analyses, with multiplicities. These do not add a mass observation.
- S_h(T)=sum_(u_q<=T) u_q d_q(h) is the sharp signed first-height head. It is finite at each finite T. S_K is its trace on a physical orthonormal basis of the ENTIRE actual contact kernel.
- alpha_tau(u)=(1-exp(-tau u))/tau, tau>0, is the bounded Abel ordinate multiplier. A_tau(h)=sum_q alpha_tau(u_q)d_q(h) is a difference of two finite sums. Tau here is a spectral regularization scale, not the aperture lane's Schur margin.
- k_tau(T)=alpha_tau(T)/T, extended by k_tau(0)=1. omega_tau(T)=-k_tau'(T)=[1-(1+tau T)exp(-tau T)]/(tau T^2), a nonnegative probability density on (0,infinity).
- W_tau(h)=(2/pi) integral_0^t0 D_w(t;h)/(t^2+tau^2)dt is the regularized nonnegative native defect. W_(epsilon,1) remains the historical sharp TRANSLATION-scale cutoff; it is not S_h(T).
- Uniform one-sided sharp-head target means sup_(T>=T1) S_K(T)<infinity for some finite T1. A finite liminf along sharp heights is weaker and is NOT the target proved sufficient here.
