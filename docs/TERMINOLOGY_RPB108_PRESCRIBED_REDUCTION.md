# RPB108 prescribed synthesis reduction terminology

Registered 2026-10-06 before first load-bearing use in the companion note.

- `H=D_c`: actual canonical supported logarithmic physical carrier, with its specified Hilbert metric. An abstract historical physical carrier must first be identified with this carrier preserving its physical vector.
- `V,M`: prescribed historical positive coefficient carrier and finite selected negative carrier; neither is silently replaced by an existential actual selection.
- `P:V->H`, `C:M->V`: prescribed bounded synthesis and compensator.
- `R:H->M`: the same prescribed rows after an actual row/normalization attachment. `PC=-R*` is the signed factor gate.
- `A`: actual full native Riesz operator. `G=A+R*R=PP*` is the covariance gate, separate from row custody and from the sign gate.
- `L=(ker P)^perp`, `Pi_L=P*G^-1P`: reduced positive carrier and its orthogonal projection, when G is coercive.
- `F=G^(1/2)`, `U=F^-1 P|L`: explicit unitary from the reduced prescribed carrier to H.
- `C0=-F^-1 R*`, `Z=(I-Pi_L)C`: fresh root compensator and invisible positive padding. `Z` need not vanish on all of M.
- `B_red(t)`: historical closed coefficient filtration intersected with `L` in its positive slot and then mapped by `U` in that slot. It is not asserted to equal the actual physical-support filtration.
- Replacement morphology: an additive record on the reduced coefficient carrier, keeping physical k, negative u, arithmetic witnesses, extension and null interface unchanged. The original historical record/wording remain preserved.
