# RPB108: translated kernel index and one-sided opening bound

Additive definitions, 2026-10-07. For a hypothetical actual nonnegative contact kernel K of dimension r, use its existing chain e_j=D^j h_*, 0<=j<r, and c=kappa_R(e_(r-1))!=0. Define

    C_jk(t)=Q(e_j,tau_t e_k),   t>0.

All pairings are in a larger supported canonical domain and retain the complete actual form. The translated chain consists of different physical vectors; neither original nor translated chain is assumed full-null against the larger domain.

The translated kernel Gram is the 2r by 2r matrix [[0,C(t)],[C(t)^*,0]], in the family (e_0,...,e_(r-1),tau_t e_0,...,tau_t e_(r-1)). Its inertia concerns the finite physical subspace with this form. Its ordinary coefficient eigenvalues are not physical mass-normalized operator eigenvalues.

Lambda_K=sum_i |kappa_R(u_i)|^2 for any PHYSICAL L2 orthonormal basis of K. The one-sided opening bound is an upper bound on lambda_min(a+epsilon), the infimum of Q/physical mass on the enlarged window. It is not an asserted exact domain derivative or a numerical transversality floor.
