# RPB-108 finite Gauss supplement

Registered before first load-bearing use, 2026-10-01.

- **Finite digamma tail split**: the actual digamma symbol equals its shifted
  digamma tail minus a finite sum of real reciprocal coefficients, proved
  from mathlib's digamma recurrence rather than a Gauss premise.
- **Finite Gauss kernel**: the finite geometric exponential sum whose
  off-diagonal limit is the positive half-density of the pinned Gauss kernel.

The scalar tail identity and pointwise kernel limit do not assert Fourier
identification, an operator limit, or central/boundary source cancellation.
