# Pole-free native ground state (CC43)

H_a is the complete original Weil form minus BOTH Hermitian pole terms,
retaining the archimedean digamma form and every prime-power translation.
Its physical selfadjoint realization is A_a+R_0,a, using CC41's supported
logarithmic operator and the bounded pole-free physical remainder.

The ground state means a physical eigenvector at the least eigenvalue of
this semibounded compact-resolvent operator, not a critical source lift.
The positive kernel j(d)=exp(-|d|/2)/(1-exp(-2|d|)) for d!=0 is the
negative of its continuous off-diagonal kernel. Prime atoms also have
negative coefficients. A zero-moment null is an H_a-null whose parity
pole moment vanishes. It is not assumed to be a ground state.
