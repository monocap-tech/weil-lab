# RPB108 NF52 terminology

**Full joint response** means the response to the complete `R alpha+G beta`
through one shared high inverse for unchanged P and T53. Endpoint logarithms,
signed pole, all original prime cells and infinite remainders remain paid.

**Enlarged high family** means eleven even or ten odd physical high polynomials.
NF52 appends one rational direction per parity, exactly orthogonal to every
inherited high column.

**Correlated inverse gain** is the positive rank-one Schur update after the
common Xi source Gram cancels exactly. It certifies improvement without paying
that same Gram uncertainty twice. A positive gain need not clear a witness.

**Extended-shell enclosure failure** means the degree-308 attempt could not
certify the inverse denominator at 500-digit precision. It is not a negative
original source norm. Final NF52 certificates retain the degree-244 shell.

**Lifted witness** means a previously negative sufficient lower-bound value
becomes strictly positive, independently proved. It does not certify the
entire matrix.

**Rejecting witness** means an exact rational vector has strictly negative
value under the sufficient joint lower matrix. It does not establish an
original negative Weil vector.

**Whole gate** remains the original shared high-condensed
`D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities. Whole positivity at 1.06, RH, F4 and Lean
remain open; the certified whole-aperture anchor remains 1.05.
