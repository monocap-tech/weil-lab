# RPB108 rational prime coercivity terminology

- **Physical coercivity on E:** an explicit lower bound Q(f)>=tau||f||_(L2)^2 on the named finite trial span, with tau>0.
- **Shifted pivot certificate:** positive interval Schur pivots for Q_E-tau G_E; the shift uses the actual physical Gram matrix.
- **Finite-range factorization T_E:** the same-vector map S_+f to S_Bf on S_+(E). Its contraction estimate does not imply an estimate on the full positive completion.

Definitions are additive. Historical terminology is unchanged.
