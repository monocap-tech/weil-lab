# RPB108 exterior collar gain terminology

Registered 2026-10-06 before the companion theorem.

- `c`: hypothetical actual nonnegative null support, `h in K_c`: full native weak-null vector with unchanged physical zero extension.
- `q_h=m_c(D)h+p_h`: globally recovered core plus locally defined exact signed physical pole. It vanishes on (-c,c); it is not asserted to vanish on any enlargement.
- `Omega_s=(c,c+s) union (-c-s,-c)`: two exterior collars of total measure 2s.
- `L_s=log(1/s)`, `0<s<=exp(-2)`: collar logarithm; unrelated to a positive coefficient subspace.
- `M_s(h)=||q_h||^2_L2(Omega_s)`: full residual collar mass, retaining the archimedean, prime and pole contributions together before squaring. It is not a single inverse-boundary moment.
- `e_s(h)`: effective inverse energy of the same enlarged full-native residual for any locally coercive actual finite selection.
- `E_p(v)=integral w^p |Fourier v|^2`, `w=log(e+|xi|)`: whole-line logarithmic energy of power p. `E_1` is canonical form energy.
- `chi`: fixed smooth compact cutoff equal to one on a common larger physical support; used to localize q_h without claiming a globally L2 exponential pole.
- Superlogarithmic collar/gain decay means decay faster than every fixed inverse power of L_s. It does not mean O(s^epsilon) or quadratic decay.
