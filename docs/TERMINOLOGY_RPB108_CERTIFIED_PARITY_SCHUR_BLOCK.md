# RPB108 certified parity Schur block terminology

- **Constant-linear corrected block:** exact Schur restriction to span{1,2x}, including the full complement coupling correction.
- **Parity split:** even/odd decomposition under physical reflection, which annihilates actual cross-parity mixed entries.
- **Corrected block coercivity:** a lower energy bound on that named block; it does not certify either full 32-dimensional parity block.

Definitions are additive and preserve historical wording.
