# RPB-108 actual centered digamma step decay supplement

**Centered step decay** means the actual centered symbol's increment from
N to N+1 has norm at most 64(πξ)^2/(N+1)^3. The finite recurrence and exact
real reciprocal deficit prove the estimate without a Gauss representation.

**Absolutely summable centered increments** means that norm series is
summable for each fixed frequency. Increment decay does not determine the
centered symbol's limiting value, prove it is zero, or justify a limit of
Schwartz pairings without appropriate frequency domination.
