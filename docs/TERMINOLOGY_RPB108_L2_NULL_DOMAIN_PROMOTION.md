# RPB108 terminology: L2 null equations and domain promotion

Introduced with REFLECTED_PACKET_BRIDGE_108_L2_NULL_DOMAIN_PROMOTION_20261006.md.

- **Supported L2 solution of the full native null equation:** a global L2 function h supported in [-a,a] for which m_a(D)h+p_h vanishes as a distribution on (-a,a), with the actual frozen prime cutoff, archimedean multiplier, and both pole moments. D_a membership is a conclusion, not part of this definition.
- **Domain promotion:** the proved implication from that supported L2 equation to global L2 multiplier action, logarithmic form membership, and full mixed nullity on D_a.
- **Global H^r regularity:** Sobolev regularity on the whole real line of the zero extension. Interior regularity alone does not provide this premise.
- **Derivative promotion:** if h is full mixed-null and its global derivative is L2, the full distribution equation commutes with differentiation and domain promotion makes that derivative a genuine member of K_a.

These terms apply to the full actual native equation. They do not identify selected-background nullity or a zero diagonal with that equation. The theorem excludes K_a intersect H^r when r=dim K_a>=1, but it does not prove actual H1 regularity or determine r.
