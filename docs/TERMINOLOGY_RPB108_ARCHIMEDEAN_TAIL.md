# Effective archimedean remainder tail (CC38)

Retain CC37's r_arch and complete logarithmic carrier with Fourier
weight w(xi)=log(e+|xi|). For R>=1 the archimedean tail form is the
actual multiplier r_arch(xi) restricted to |xi|>R, compressed to D_t.
Its canonical form operator is denoted C_arch,>R. This frequency
restriction is an error decomposition, not a modified Weil identity.

CC38 proves |r_arch(xi)|<4/|xi| for |xi|>=1 and
||C_arch,>R||<=4/[R log(e+R)]. The complete arithmetic form retains
the tail, all prime translations and both signed pole terms.
