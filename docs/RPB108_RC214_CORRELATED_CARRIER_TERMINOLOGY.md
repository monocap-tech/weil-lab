# RC214 correlated carrier terminology

A native source is unchanged even when its approximate action carrier is rebuilt. RC214 uses δ = z_b6 − v10 with the historical finite centers and combined selected analytic tail.

The rebuilt z action is the prior certified v action plus the certified δ action. Its error shares the v error exactly: e_z = e_v + e_δ. This correlation is an identity between these specific trial carriers. It cannot be assigned to the separately computed RC211 z carrier.

A difference-direction half-Gram error pass bounds approximation error on that direction only. It does not certify the full eight-source mixed/action matrices or the expanded inverse gate. The accepted six-source inverse bound remains 0.436100; no Stage I, RH, F4, or Lean closure follows.
