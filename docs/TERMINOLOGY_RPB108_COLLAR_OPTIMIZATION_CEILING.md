# RPB108 terminology: collar optimization ceiling

- **Collar majorant:** the proved actual near/far upper envelope for M_R, including its logarithmic collar term and separated Gaussian tail.
- **Bound-optimization ceiling:** a lower bound on the infimum of that envelope over collar widths. It is not a lower bound on actual M_R.
- **Uncontrolled smooth regularity:** all-orders smoothness or logarithmic moments with no quantitative growth law for their constants; it does not imply analytic regularity.
