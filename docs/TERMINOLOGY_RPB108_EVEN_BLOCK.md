| Positive odd55 leading block | The entire original retained odd block of degrees1,3,...,109, tested together with all mixed entries, the original complement and the complete source operator error. |
| Terminal odd scalar gate | The remaining one-dimensional retained Schur quotient after eliminating the positive odd55 block and the original positive infinite complement. It is not the native degree111 diagonal. |
| Positive codimension1 slice | All even directions together with the positive odd55 retained block and odd F112. This leaves one odd retained condition; historical codimension107 certification is also preserved. |
