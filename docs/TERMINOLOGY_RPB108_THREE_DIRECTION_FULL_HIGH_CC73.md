# CC73 terminology: retained restriction with the entire high space

These definitions precede their use. Historical wording is unchanged.

At a=53/50, E=E112 and F=E-perp in physical L2. The original high form C on F satisfies C>=kappa I with kappa=207/1000. Inverse responses use this coercive form, not a globally bounded full Weil operator.

Let x_even,x_odd be the unchanged exact rational retained seeds, and w_odd the unchanged NF27 retained response, physically orthogonal to x_odd. The **three-direction retained space** is Z3=span(x_even,x_odd,w_odd). Parity and exact orthogonality make its physical mass matrix diagonal.

The **full-high restricted space** is Z3+F, with the canonical high form domain understood. It contains the entire infinite high space and excludes 109 retained directions. Positivity there is stronger than a finite polynomial restriction, but it is not whole-domain positivity.

The **retained physical Schur gap** mu means S(z)>=mu||z||^2 for every z in Z3, where S minimizes the original form over all F. The **full-high physical gap** g means Q(z+f)>=g||z+f||^2 for every admitted z in Z3 and f in F. The latter pays the high response and the chosen trial lift in physical norm; a coordinate Schur margin alone is not that gap.

For trial columns Tz=z+Hz with complete high sources Rz=P_F L Tz, the **high response graph** is J=H-C^-1 R. Source and lift trace bounds in the retained physical mass give ||J||^2<=2xi^2+2eta^2, where eta^2 bounds ||C^-1R||^2 and xi^2 bounds ||H||^2. This converts a retained Schur gap to a physical gap on Z3+F.
