# RPB108 terminology — native boundary pairing and one-mode reaction (NF18)

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Additive; historical CC20–CC51 and NF10–NF17 terms unchanged.

**Physical Legendre splitting.** At aperture \(a=53/50\), \(E_{112}\) is the span of the first 112 orthonormal supported Legendre test functions, and \(F_{112}\) its physical L2-orthogonal complement in the original canonical supported logarithmic form domain. The original complete mixed form is \(Q_a(x,y)\), not a positivity shift or a finite zero dictionary.

**Native boundary column.** For \(j\ge112\), the set \((Q_a(e_i,e_j))_{i=0}^{111}\) of exact signed mixed native pairings, with the archimedean term, all active original prime powers in both translation orientations, and both Hermitian signed poles. A finite set of such columns is NOT the Riesz representative of the complete physical source operator on all of \(F_{112}\).

**One-mode Schur reaction.** For a specific physical-normalized \(\phi=e_j\in F_{112}\) with \(Q_a(\phi,\phi)>0\) and a specified \(x\in E_{112}\), set
\[
\rho_j(x)=\frac{|Q_a(x,\phi)|^2}{Q_a(\phi,\phi)},\quad
\alpha_j(x)=\rho_j(x)/Q_a(x,x)\quad\text{if }Q_a(x,x)>0.
\]
This is the EXACT amount of old signed Rayleigh energy consumed by minimizing \(Q_a(x+t\phi)\) over the single high trial direction \(t\). It is a LOWER bound on the complete \(F_{112}\) inverse reaction for that \(x\) if the full \(F_{112}\) high response is well-defined and positive; it is NOT an upper bound, does not evaluate the high Riesz response, and cannot by itself certify the full original Schur sign.

**Rational near-critical witness.** A fixed exact rational coefficient vector \(x\) in one parity block of E112, selected numerically then fully verified by exact interval arithmetic. Its approximate construction is not proof evidence; only its published exact coefficients and the complete signed interval matrices enter certified inequalities.

**Absolute-norm sufficient gate limitation.** The elementary sufficient whole-sign test \(\|B\|^2<c\,\lambda\) for positive low lower margin \(\lambda\) and high physical lower \(c\) is not necessary. A certified single mixed native entry exceeding the required norm threshold disproves the *sufficiency test's hypothesis*, not positivity of the original Weil form or RH.
