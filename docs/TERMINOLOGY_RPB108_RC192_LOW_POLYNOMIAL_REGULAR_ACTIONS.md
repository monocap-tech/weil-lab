# RC192 terminology

**Low polynomial regular carrier** means the degree-768 even Legendre polynomial approximating D^-1 R P0 or D^-1 R P2, using RC179's degree-192 analytic kernel, exact beta and differentiated beta moments, directed Decimal coefficient intervals, and a bound on the complete omitted endpoint-log output tail.

**Complete low polynomial canonical action error** is an absolute D-energy norm bound for the represented action of M=D^-1 L on the named unchanged source. It adds the new regular carrier error to the inherited finite endpoint and negative-r coefficient payment. The analytic endpoint tails of RC171 and RC172 remain part of the action representation. This is neither a global operator-norm bound nor an inverse-width certificate.

The source family, norm, operator, and aperture 11/10 remain fixed for the comparisons. Source coverage and the native seven-source frame are unchanged. The five-source inverse acceptance condition remains the RC191 result until all changed mixed rows are reconstructed.
