# RPB108 exact endpoint-log Gram terminology

- **Endpoint-log source component:** the unchanged physical trial vector multiplied by the actual explicit endpoint logarithm.
- **Endpoint-log residual Gram:** its physical Gram after removing Legendre degrees 0..63; only this component is enclosed here.
- **Regularized mixed integral:** subtract a panel endpoint interpolant, integrate its logarithmic part explicitly, and retain the vanishing-endpoint remainder and all mixed projection terms.

Definitions are additive. Historical terminology is unchanged.
