# RC165 terminology additions

Historical definitions and fitted trials remain immutable.

**Direct centered t action trial.** Write the canonical t10=p+r, where p retains its rational center coefficients through even degree 4094 and r contains all remaining finite and analytic source coefficients. Define q_t_new=Q_24(p)+r/2. Q_24 retains direct Cp centers through degree 8190 and 25 positive eta output moments beyond that cutoff. This physical action approximant need not lie in the seven-source span. The source frame itself remains unchanged.

**Finite-fit residual removal.** RC137 approximated Cp by a linear combination of the seven native source vectors and paid the resulting finite distance and fitted-trial tail. RC165 uses the direct finite Cp centers instead. Those old fit-error terms do not enter the new trial's error budget; finite action rounding, output moment rounding, the geometric remainder, and the centered full omitted-source norm do enter it.

**Two tail transitions.** Q_24(p) switches from finite centers to its eta moment polynomial at degree 8192. The canonical t10 source switches from its finite center polynomial to its prescribed RC135 eta moment polynomial at degree 16384. Between those cutoffs, the trial is the captured action eta polynomial plus half the canonical finite t10 coefficient. Above degree 16382, the two eta polynomials retain their separate Lambda scales.

**Three separate action columns.** Replacing the native fitted Ct column leaves four original native action approximants and three independent physical action approximants q_t_new, q_psi, and q_delta_new. Reference inverse reassembly must pay their trial-source and mutual Gram entries before using the new t error norm.
