# RPB108 NF59 terminology

**Even NF58 target** is the first frozen exact rational sign probe, whose
certified lower-matrix enclosure spans zero. It is not a certified negative
witness. Its physical mass and probe provenance are authenticated.

**Odd NF58 target** is an exact rational rejecting witness with certified
negative sufficient lower-bound value and original physical mass.

**Complete target response** uses all P and T53 coordinates through one
shared minorant inverse. The rational approximation selects a high polynomial;
paid signed native/source integrals independently certify its effect.

**Enlarged high family** contains eighteen even or seventeen odd physical
polynomials. The new direction is exactly orthogonal to every inherited
high column on the degree-436/435 shell.

**Resolved target** has a strictly positive independently certified lower-bound
value. This resolves that direction; the complete matrix requires its own
sign proof.

**Correlated gain** cancels the common Xi Gram exactly before evaluating the
positive rank-one inverse-reaction update. Positive gain alone may leave a
target's sign unresolved or negative.

**Complete source custody** preserves historical interval endpoints, physical
errors, endpoint logarithms, signed pole, all 13 prime cells and infinite
remainders. New entries are certified at 800/900-digit precision.

**Whole gate** remains the original shared `D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities.
Its complete sign and whole-aperture status are recorded in the checkpoint
ledger and accompanying note.

**NF59 outcome** certifies positive correlated inverse gains in both
parities without clearing either fixed NF58 target. The even target
remains span-zero; the odd target remains a certified rejection of the
sufficient bound. All 14 frozen even candidates span zero. Whole 1.06
remains open, with whole 1.05 as the anchor.

**Exact component-width audit** decomposes the direct target interval into
native energy, common source ceiling and inverse correction. It checks
the interval identity with exact Fraction coefficients and endpoints,
and verifies coverage by the rounded validator result. Width shares
describe recorded bounds, not independent actual errors.

**Grouped target contraction** computes `w=W* z` before `w* N^-1 w/kappa^2`,
retains every original payment, and intersects valid target enclosures.
NF59 narrows the even enclosure by about 4.5 times. The common source
ceiling then contributes about 99.9053% of its grouped forward width.
This changes the enclosure, not the underlying physical target or minorant,
and does not certify the complete matrix.

**Remaining endpoint budget** is additional guaranteed gain strictly
greater than the negative recorded lower endpoint. NF59's even budget
is about `2.1126651876983884 e-33`. It is sufficient for that enclosure,
not necessary for the actual form or sufficient for the whole gate.

**Bound rejection** never certifies an actual negative original Weil vector.
