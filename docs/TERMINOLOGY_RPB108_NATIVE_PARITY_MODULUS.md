# RPB108 native parity modulus terminology

For a real half-profile f supported in (0,a), its parity extension h_p equals f(x) for x>0 and p f(-x) for x<0, where p=1 is even and p=-1 is odd. Its **half-modulus extension** h_p^abs is the same extension of |f|. In the odd sector it is not the full physical modulus |h_p|.

The **parity modulus defect** is Delta_p(f)=Q_a(h_p)-Q_a(h_p^abs), for the prescribed full actual native form. Both vectors have the same physical mass. A negative defect means that half-modulus increases energy; it does not mean either energy is negative and does not construct a null vector.

Opposite-sign pairs mean f(x)f(y)<0. Write f_+=max(f,0), f_-=max(-f,0), k(s)=exp(-s/2)/(1-exp(-2s)), ell_n=log n, b_n=Lambda(n)/sqrt n. All prime sums below are finite, with ell_n<2a.
