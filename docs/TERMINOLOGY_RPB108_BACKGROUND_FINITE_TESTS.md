# Actual background finite-test terminology

| Term | Exact meaning |
|---|---|
| Finite raw-copy packet | A finite sum of coordinate singles with arbitrary complex coefficients in the existing actual divisor l2 space. Repeated-ordinate copies do not become independent observations. |
| Green background test | The existing unshifted effective-background quadratic evaluated on the unchanged actual Green lift of the named coefficient vector. |
| Finite positivity criterion | Nonnegativity for every finite raw-copy packet, equivalent to nonnegativity on the certified Green graph completion. This is an infinite family of finite tests, not a finite exhaustive test list. |
| Finite negative certificate | One finite packet whose existing unshifted actual background form is strictly negative; equivalent to failure of every unit background factor on the relevant positive completion. |
| Relevant completion | The certified Green graph and its positive-analysis range completion. No full source-graph density is asserted. |
