# RPB108 terminology: strict finite-source Loewner order

Introduced with REFLECTED_PACKET_BRIDGE_108_FINITE_SOURCE_LOEWNER_20261006.md.

- **Selected-source redundancy Z:** the kernel of u->sum_{q in s} u_q eta_q as an entire finite exponential polynomial. It equals ker R_t^* for every positive window, including copy and zero-row dependencies.
- **Observable selected coefficient space:** Z^\perp in l2(s), or its isometric quotient l2(s)/Z. Actual energy weights are retained; redundant rows do not count as independent rank.
- **Finite response L_s(t):** R_t G_t^(-1)R_t^* where G_t= A_t+R_t^*R_t is coercive. The finite native sign matrix is D_s(t)=I-L_s(t).
- **Effective forced solution h_t(u):** G_t^(-1)R_t^*u, characterized by the actual positive mixed form against every canonical test.
- **Strict Loewner order:** for a<b with both effective covariances coercive, L_s(b)-L_s(a) is positive definite on Z^\perp, with kernel exactly Z on the full coefficient space.

The comparison uses unchanged physical support inclusion and an exact variational gap. It is not a uniform inverse estimate, global rank bound, proof of matrix differentiability, historical packet attachment, or endpoint exclusion.
