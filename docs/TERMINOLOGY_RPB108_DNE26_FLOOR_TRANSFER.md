# RPB108 — DNE26 terminology: NF38 fixed-frame floor transfer

Definitions are additive; earlier floor obstructions and historical wording remain unchanged.

**Selected NF38 frame:** three finite original polynomial columns per parity, with retained components (x,w,u32), frozen by NF38's rational joint two-direction correction map. Their complete original native matrix Q and projected F112 source Gram Gamma are the authenticated NF38 selected matrices. All correction polynomials are high, so the retained span is unchanged. This is a particular map, not every map in that family.

**Old scalar floor:** k0=207/1000. NF38's universal obstruction concerns Q-Gamma/k0 on its named two-direction family.

**Available original high floor:** k=11/25, certified by DNE17 on the entire original infinite F112. It may be used with any original finite polynomial frame at the same aperture 53/50; it is not tied to the former frame's selected source shell.

**Transferred coarse matrix:** U_k=Q-Gamma/k. It is a sufficient full-high form bound obtained from C>=k I. It is not the actual inverse Schur matrix. Positivity of U_k on a fixed frame includes every original high correction.

**Scaled coordinate lower bound:** for positive rational scales s_i, D=diag(s_i), D U_k D>=g I. These scaled coordinates are not a physical orthonormal basis.

**Selected physical column bound:** m_i is the frozen base column's physical norm upper bound plus the absolute correction coefficients times the physical norms of both high polynomials. The completed bound b_i=m_i+sqrt(Gamma_ii_upper)/k pays the original inverse-high coordinate change.

**Transferred physical gap:** [sum_i (s_i b_i)^2/g+1/k]^-1, derived by the original high square completion and weighted Cauchy. It is not a finite truncation gap.

**Three-column native source credit c:** g diag(s_i^-2)>=(c/k)Q implies Gamma<=(k-c)Q. DNE26 certifies c=1/100 even and c=3/50 odd for the selected THREE-column frame. These numbers do not replace DNE25's FOUR-column credits or certify its remaining source budget.
