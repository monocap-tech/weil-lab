# RPB108 certified constant Schur terminology

- **Actual Schur diagonal:** Q(e)-Q(z_e), with z_e the exact complement solution. It includes the infinite-complement coupling correction.
- **Endpoint-log separation:** isolate the actual source's explicit endpoint logarithms and bound their physical Legendre tail directly.
- **Certified corrected direction:** positivity of one Schur diagonal; it does not imply positivity of the full matrix.

These are additive definitions. Historical wording remains unchanged.
