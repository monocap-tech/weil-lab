**Correlated nonendpoint variational functional.** In the RC158 conditioned source coordinates, write the inverse trial matrix as Y=S V and Z=I-V. Then S^*Y+Y^*S-Y^*Y=G-Z^*GZ. Certified Gram bounds give G_Lo-Z^*G_Hi Z as a lower and G_Hi-Z^*G_Lo Z as an upper. This keeps the complete Gram uncertainty correlated through cancellation.

**Correlated nominal-residual transport.** Split the approximate residual into one native carrier combination and the two new action trials. Bound the carrier combination by congruence with RC159's complete Gram upper, and pay the cross and two-trial terms with RC156-157's direct pairing intervals. This produces a new nominal-residual Gram upper independently of RC158's previous upper.

**Limiting-direction separate budgets.** Gram-functional width, endpoint-functional width, nominal residual, and action error are each evaluated on RC158's seventh conditioned unit vector and divided by the same certified source Gram lower. These quantities diagnose the checked enclosure. They are not lower bounds on the true inverse's uncertainty.
