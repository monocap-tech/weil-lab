# Coupled continuation terminology (CC1)

Definitions precede their load-bearing use. Historical registries are unchanged.

- Fixed carrier: H=D_1, with norm squared E_log, physical inclusion J, and mass-unitary dilation U_b f(x)=b^(-1/2)f(x/b).
- Canonical margin: delta_b=inf Q_b(U_b f)/||f||_H^2. Physical gap: lambda_min(b)=inf Q_b(h)/||h||_2^2. These are distinct infima.
- Retained space: a finite-dimensional canonical-orthogonal E, with F=E^perp_H. It is not automatically the physical low-eigenspace or the published physical Legendre span.
- Anchor blocks: C=Pi_F A(a)|F, K=Pi_F A(a)|E, S=A_EE-K* C^(-1)K, with C>=c0 I>0.
- Completed Schur lift: W x=x-C^(-1)Kx. This maps E into H, and Pi_F A(a)W=0. Its complement part T=C^(-1)K has norm ell.
- Graph perturbation budgets: alpha bounds ||W*[A(b)-A(a)]W||; eta bounds ||Pi_F[A(b)-A(a)]W||. They include the whole original arithmetic correction.
- Complete original source gain: g_b=||N_b(P_b|D_b)^(-1)|| on the entire closed Ran P_b, with original multiplicities, rows and normalizations. A graph-restricted source ratio is not this norm.
- CC1: a conditional joint update using graph perturbation budgets and a separately protected complement. No new aperture or non-stalling assertion is encoded in this name.
