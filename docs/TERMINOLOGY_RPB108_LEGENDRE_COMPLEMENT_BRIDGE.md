# RPB108 terminology: finite-to-global Legendre complement bridge

Definitions are additive; historical terminology is unchanged.

- **Legendre moment complement**: the subspace of the actual logarithmic carrier whose first \(m\) physical moments vanish. It is the physical \(L^2\)-orthogonal complement of the supported Legendre trial span of degrees \(<m\).
- **Moment-complement leakage**: the explicit bound \(\eta_{m,T}\) controlling physical \(L^2\) norm by logarithmic norm on the Legendre moment complement.
- **Squared-log trial constant**: \(d_m=\sup_{e\in E_m\setminus\{0\}}\|w\widehat e\|_2/\|e\|_2\), the finite constant needed to control the logarithmic identity cross term under the physical orthogonal splitting.
- **Whole-carrier determinant margin**: \(\tau(1-c\eta^2)-(d_m+c)^2\eta^2\). Positivity of this scalar margin promotes the certified finite Legendre coercivity to strict positivity of the entire actual logarithmic carrier.
- **Finite-to-global Legendre bridge**: the theorem that a positive whole-carrier determinant margin closes the uncontrolled complement while preserving the same physical/source vector.
