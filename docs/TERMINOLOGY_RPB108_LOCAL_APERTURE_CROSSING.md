# RPB108 terminology: local aperture crossing

- **Pulled-back endpoint kernel:** ker A(a) on the fixed logarithmic Hilbert domain obtained by physical dilation; distinct from asserting unchanged source coordinates across dilations.
- **Positive-complement gap:** the strictly positive lower bound of the nonnegative endpoint operator on its kernel's orthogonal complement.
- **Local crossing interval:** a right neighborhood of a positive endpoint on which the negative index equals endpoint nullity and the enlarged kernel is zero.
- **Strictly expansive dimension:** maximal dimension of a subspace of the actual positive-analysis range on which full negative analysis strictly exceeds positive energy for every nonzero vector. This is an index, without a claimed singular-vector constructor.

Historical terminology is unchanged. These definitions accompany their first load-bearing use in the local crossing theorem.
