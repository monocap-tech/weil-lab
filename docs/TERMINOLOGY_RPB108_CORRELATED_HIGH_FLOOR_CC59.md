# CC59 terminology: correlated high-floor majorant

Definitions are additive and precede the CC59 report.

Within the ORIGINAL physical complement F112, split the two measured modes H2 from their physical orthogonal complement U. Write the complete high form C_full in blocks [C2,K*;K,D_U]. The **measured-to-unmeasured source map** K maps H2 to U by physical projection of the genuine polynomial sources L phi_j. It is bounded and finite rank; D_U can be unbounded.

The compensated retained source sigma maps E to U, with physical Gram P=sigma* sigma=P2. Define the **tail source Gram** H=K*K and the **tail source cross Gram** Z=K*sigma. These retain all original arithmetic source sectors and their signed cross correlations. H is distinct from the high energy block C2.

For the certified high floor kappa=207/1000 and C2>kappa I, set M=C2^{-1}(C2-kappa I)^{-1} and V=C2(C2-kappa I)+H=M^{-1}+H. The **correlated high-floor majorant** is

    U_corr=(P-Z*V^{-1}Z)/kappa.

It upper-bounds the TRUE unmeasured inverse response T_rest and is no larger than P/kappa. This is an upper reaction estimate, not a lower source frame.

The **least high completion** has D_min=kappa I+K(C2-kappa I)^{-1}K*. It attains U_corr while preserving the known C2,K and floor. Sharpness here means sharp under these specified partial operator data, not sharp after all additional Weil identities are imposed.
