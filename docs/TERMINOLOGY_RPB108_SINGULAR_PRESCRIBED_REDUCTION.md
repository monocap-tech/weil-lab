# RPB108 singular prescribed covariance reduction

Registered 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles); global/F4 lane.

At a nonnegative actual Fredholm window, H is the canonical logarithmic Hilbert carrier, A its full native Riesz operator, and R the prescribed actual finite negative analysis. G=A+R*R is positive Fredholm but need not be coercive.

N=ker G=ker A intersect ker R is the prescribed invisible physical subspace. Invisible refers only to G and the prescribed P,R; complete actual source samples can still see it. H_vis=N perpendicular is a closed physical subspace. i:H_vis->H is inclusion. G_vis=i*G i is coercive. F_vis=G_vis^(1/2) and G^+=i G_vis^(-1)i* is the bounded pseudoinverse, zero on N.

For the named historical synthesis P:V->H, assume PP*=G and PC=-R*. L=(ker P) perpendicular is the reduced positive coefficient carrier. U=F_vis^(-1)i*P restricted to L is a unitary L->H_vis. The reduced synthesis is P_red=i F_vis:H_vis->H, so the physical carrier remains the full H. C_red=-F_vis^(-1)i*R* is the reduced compensator.

k_vis=i i* k and k_inv=-G^+R*u are projected physical vectors. They must not replace the retained unchanged k unless its N-component is zero. Quotienting positive coefficient padding differs from projecting the physical vector.
