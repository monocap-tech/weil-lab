# CC53 terminology — collective native boundary response

Definitions precede load-bearing use in the CC53 report.

* **Original split:** at aperture a=53/50, E112 is the physical L2 span of supported normalized Legendre degrees 0–111; F112 is its physical orthogonal complement in the supported logarithmic form domain. A is the original signed Q restricted to E112 and C is Q restricted to F112. NF16 supplies C>=207/1000 in physical mass. All original archimedean, prime and both signed pole terms remain.
* **Boundary mode:** phi=e112 in even parity and phi=e113 in odd parity. These are physical unit vectors in F112. Define b_i=Q(e_i,phi) and d=Q(phi,phi)>3.
* **Collective first-mode response:** G1=bb*/d, a rank-one low quadratic form. Its relative norm is R1=b*A^{-1}b/d=sup_{x!=0}|Q(x,phi)|^2/[d Q(x,x)]. It optimizes over all 56 retained coordinates of its parity, rather than testing one candidate eigenvector. The inverse is finite A inverse, not an infinite high inverse.
* **First-mode corrected gate:** A-G1. A bound R1<r implies A-G1>(1-r)A. This is a signed correlation statement relative to the actual low energy, without replacing A by its smallest certified scalar floor.
* **Residual response:** after the C-orthogonal Galerkin lift Yx=(Q(phi,x)/d)phi, the residual functional is r_x(z)=Q(x,z)-C(Yx,z). Its full C-dual Gram T is the response beyond this trial lift; the complete inverse response is G=G1+T. Here T is unknown and positive semidefinite.
* **Collective remaining reserve:** A-G1, a matrix, rather than a single witness remainder. To retain fraction theta of A one needs T<=(1-theta)A-G1. This is an equivalent source correlation criterion at this split, not a proved estimate on T.
* **Observation-compatible completion control:** an abstract symmetric matrix retaining an observed response fraction and a positive high floor while varying unobserved coupling. Such a control tests an inference from these observations only. It is not a complete original Weil arithmetic realization, an independence theorem, or an actual negative Weil vector.
* **Whole-mass positive-level control:** subtract mu times the entire physical Gram from the original form, including the high block, to test a positive eigenlevel. A resulting null is a shifted-level null, not an original Q null.
* **Scope:** a first-mode collective bound at a fixed aperture is not an aperture source-shell leakage estimate, an all-cap defect-relative frame bound, or a transfer to CC52's exact moment-zero enriched carrier.
