# RPB108 terminology: stable near-null observation

- **Native residual norm:** logarithmic norm of the bounded native form operator applied to the same vector, or the mixed functional's dual norm.
- **Near-null cone:** vectors whose native residual norm is at most epsilon times their logarithmic norm; not merely vectors with small indefinite diagonal.
- **Joint native/exterior observation:** the native residual together with independent actual exterior point evaluations.
- **Full-source defect operator:** I-T* T for T=N P^(-1) on the actual positive-analysis range.

Definitions accompany first load-bearing use. Stability is fixed-window; no uniform aperture constant or raw synthesis inverse is implied. Historical terminology is unchanged.
