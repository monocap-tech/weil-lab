# CC66 terminology: complete trial source and reduced directional data

These definitions are additive and precede the report.

The **complete trial source** is the entire physical source Lp for ONE
fixed polynomial/direction p, including its retained projection and all
high coordinates. This is stronger than knowing only ||P_F Lp||.
It is weaker than knowing the original operator on all source functions.

The **reduced directional data** consist of Q(p), that complete source,
the measured two-high block C2, the additional observed source row K_J,
and the scalar high floor kappa=207/1000. A stronger abstract control also
fixes the finite form on the retained, measured-H2 and observed-J spaces.
The term does not denote all authenticated E112 records or all original
Weil identities.

The **unmeasured high completion** changes only high source rows not
specified by those reduced data. An **attained coarse response** satisfies
<r,C^-1 r>=||r||²/kappa despite the fixed observed mixed row.

The **corrected correlation** zbar=G*r-kappa H*r and
**correlation credit** zbar*V^-1 zbar, V=G*G-kappa C2, are CC64's complete
high-source quantities. A zero credit is possible without r=0.

A **near-critical scaled control** scales the retained-to-high source by
epsilon and the retained energy by epsilon² while retaining the high
operator. It tests small-energy robustness, not actual Weil eigenvectors.

A **partial-data crossing** is an abstract full-form negative/zero/positive
Schur family with the same specified reduced data. It does not satisfy
or purport to satisfy the complete Weil arithmetic identities.
