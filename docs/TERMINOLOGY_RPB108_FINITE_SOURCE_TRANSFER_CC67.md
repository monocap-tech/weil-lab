# CC67 terminology: finite source transfer and complete-source diagnostic

These definitions are additive and precede the CC67 report.

The **finite source upper bound** bounds the original physical L2 norm
||L_a e_n|| at a=53/50 for normalized Legendre modes n<=115. It is not a
boundedness claim for the complete logarithmic operator on physical L2
and is not a lower frame bound.

A **finite high coefficient perturbation** changes only the two measured
high Legendre coefficients. Its complete original source change is paid
by the finite source upper bound and the coefficient l1 difference.

The **exact H2 Galerkin compensation** of a fixed CC63 trial p is
p_exact=p-H2 C2^-1 u, with u=Q(H2,p). It removes only the actual measured
two-mode residual. It is not the complete infinite high minimizer.

A **source-transfer certificate** is an explicit original source-norm
bound on the difference of those finite polynomials, independent of the
old retained gap. It does not bound either complete source norm itself.

The **complete-source diagnostic** runs the unchanged NF24 90-digit
numerical producer on the EXACT CC65 polynomials, at two inner/outer
integration orders. Its integration remainder is unproved.

The **diagnostic omitted-source ratio** subtracts the three observed
source-coordinate squares from the numerical full-high source square,
then divides by CC65's certified LOWER omitted-source budget.
It is a numerical comparison, not a rigorous gate rejection.

An **authenticated projection comparison** checks a numerical high
projection against an original interval coordinate. Agreement is a
diagnostic consistency check and does not certify the integral remainder.
