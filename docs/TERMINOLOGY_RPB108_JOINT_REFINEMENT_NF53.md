# RPB108 NF53 terminology

**Extended-shell precision barrier discharged** means the previously unresolved
degree-308/307 native/source assembly now certifies its positive inverse
denominator. The producer uses 800 digits and the validator 900 digits, with
larger moment cutoffs and explicit finite analytic tails.

**Exact interval promotion** multiplies stored 500-digit interval integers by
the exact grid ratio before arithmetic changes. Physical functions, rational
endpoints, uncertainties, archive bytes and inherited errors remain unchanged.

**Enlarged high family** contains twelve even or eleven odd physical high
polynomials. NF53 appends one fixed rational direction per parity, exactly
orthogonal to every inherited high column.

**Complete joint inverse** controls all original `(R,G)` sources together
through the same original high operator. Endpoint logarithms, signed pole,
all 13 prime cells, source covariances and infinite remainders remain paid.

**Correlated inverse gain** is the positive rank-one Schur update after the
common Xi Gram cancels exactly. A positive gain alone need not clear a witness.

**Lifted NF52 witness** has a strictly positive independently certified value
under the enlarged sufficient lower matrix. Both inherited NF52 witnesses
are lifted; positivity of the complete matrix still requires a separate proof.

**New rejecting witness** is an exact rational vector with a strictly negative
sufficient lower-bound value and certified original physical mass. It rejects
that bound; it does not establish a negative original Weil vector.

**Whole gate** remains the original shared high-condensed
`D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities. Whole positivity at 1.06, RH, F4 and Lean
remain open. The highest certified whole-aperture anchor remains 1.05.
