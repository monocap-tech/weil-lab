# CC77 — complete-source rejection transfer and shell escape

Definitions are additive. All actual quantities concern the original Weil form at aperture 53/50, with E112, F112, remaining space T and kappa=207/1000 as previously defined.

The NF33 shared map is a rational rounding of the native two-high-shell minimizer on the exact T basis. The CC76 shared map restricts a previously rounded minimizer on the larger seed-orthogonal W basis. Their high coefficients are not assumed identical. For the same exact NF32 retained probe, write u76=u33+d, where d is supported on the selected H2 shell (e112,e114 even; e113,e115 odd).

A paid complete projected-source Gram for the two H2 basis vectors gives ||P_F L e_h||<4. Hence the complete source perturbation has norm at most b=4 sum_h |d_h|. If the NF33 source square is bounded below by gamma_l, the CC76 complete square is at least (sqrt(gamma_l)_lower-b)^2 when the bracket is positive.

The energy perturbation is bounded above in absolute value by 2 sum_h |d_h| |Q(u33,e_h)|_upper + sum_hj |d_h d_j| |Q(e_h,e_j)|_upper. Combined with the preceding complete square lower bound, this yields an upper bound on the CC76 scalar-floor score. A strictly negative upper bound rejects the frozen CC76 collective floor estimator without requiring its entire remaining Gram.

Selected-shell fraction is ||P_H2 P_F L u76||²/||P_F L u76||². Its complement is the source fraction in F112 orthogonal to H2. CC76's measured source operator bound applies to the retained probe norm, so it bounds the numerator independently of any unmeasured source entries. A tiny selected-shell fraction does not imply a tiny complete high source.

All estimator rejections here concern the named fixed maps. They are not actual Schur negativity or rejection of arbitrary H2 retuning, larger shared lifts or sharper inverse-weighted response estimates.
