# RPB108 NF61: retain the direct source, improve the inverse

**Frozen target.** The 56 exact signed joint coordinates are the normalized
NF58 targets retained by NF59 and certified as minorant counterdirections
by NF60. NF61 preserves these coordinates and the original physical frame.

**Response selection.** Use NF60's complete directly combined projected
source to reconstruct the remaining response under the current high
minorant. Restrict selection to the existing even degrees 112 through 436
or odd degrees 113 through 435, round midpoint coordinates downward to
denominator `10^100`, project exactly off every inherited physical high
column, then normalize with a rational norm upper bound. Selection is not
a sign certificate.

**New high direction.** A single exact physical direction is added in each
parity. Every new native pairing and complete projected-source covariance
is paid, including the endpoint logarithm, signed pole, regular infinite
tail, all 13 prime cells, low-projection uncertainty and mixed source terms.
The original `207/1000` F112 floor remains unchanged.

**Correlated gain.** For inverse denominator block
`N+ = [[N,t],[t*,s]]`, let `delta = s - t* N^-1 t` and let the source-cross
residual be `r = w - W N^-1 t`. The target gain is
`|z* r|^2 / (kappa^2 delta)`. The common source Gram cancels exactly.
Positive denominator and signed-residual intervals certify the gain;
subtracting two separately uncertain source Grams is unnecessary.

**Retained direct-source bound.** NF60's certified scalar interval for
`||Xi z||^2` is unchanged. Contract the new high cross vector against the
fixed target before evaluating its inverse quadratic correction. Intersect
this grouped direct-source target enclosure with NF60's old target interval
plus the correlated gain, and with the new full entrywise enclosure.

**Scalar and whole gates.** Lifting one target certifies only that direction
of the sufficient minorant. A negative target rejects the minorant in that
direction and is not a negative original Weil form. Every remaining joint
direction and the complete whole-aperture closure still need certification.
The anchor remains `21/20`; whole `53/50`, RH, F4, Lean and all-aperture
positivity remain open.
