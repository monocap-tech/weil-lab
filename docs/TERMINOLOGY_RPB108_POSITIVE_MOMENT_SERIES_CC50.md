# Positive moment-tail series (CC50)

At B=53/50, phi_n(x)=sqrt((2n+1)/(2B))P_n(x/B) is the supported
physical-orthonormal Legendre mode. The moment-tail series is the
Legendre expansion of the cosh/sinh physical F112 tail. Its coefficients
are positive in the matching parity. Ratios to the leading coefficient
are bounded without subtracting a low polynomial from a nearly equal
moment profile. A form-norm tail bound means the canonical logarithmic
norm, not merely physical L2 error, and does not decide a signed Schur gate.
