# DNE53 even source extension

The extended even source order is the unchanged DNE50 59-column order,
followed by physical Legendre vectors e112,e114,e116. The retained native
map is unchanged; the enlarged high trial map is [44,45,46,59,60,61].

The missing original projected source correlations are DNE52's six
old-trial/e112,e114 pairs and the 62-entry e116 column including its
diagonal. Three freshly integrated e112/e114 self and mutual controls pay
the norms of the new source approximants without replacing the inherited
original high source pairings. Thus 68 missing correlations and three
controls are evaluated in each primary/replay run.

Every fresh projected pairing is obtained from the same complete source
approximants, with all 56 retained coordinates subtracted and separate
analytic and polynomial/log rounding allowances. The new action coordinates
through degree180 permit original native pairings against all retained and
high trial polynomials after whole-source errors are paid. A complete
six-trial response test uses the actual high floor .647 and includes all
mixed entries. Negative sufficient comparisons do not assert original
Weil negativity. The inherited 111-direction packet remains the baseline.

For this enlarged frame, E=G_ZY-k Q_ZY and W=G_YY-k Q_YY.
The paid sufficient budget is k Q_ZZ-G_ZZ+EJ+(EJ)^T-J^T W J.
A rational congruence certificate for W supplies a floor mu relative to
Euclidean trial coefficient mass. For e=E^T u and any exact rational y,
completing the square gives the all-coefficient upper

    e^T W^(-1) e <= 2 e^T y-y^T W y+||e-W y||^2/mu.

This finite six-by-six inverse comparison is a sufficient-budget diagnostic;
it does not evaluate the true infinite high-block inverse. A strictly
negative upper for the base budget plus this quantity rules out every J
inside this six-trial family at the stated high floor. Interval LDL supplies
a separate upper where its outward pivots remain positive.
