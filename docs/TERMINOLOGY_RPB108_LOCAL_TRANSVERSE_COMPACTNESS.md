# RPB108: local transverse mass and actual negative compactness

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Additive definitions.

- B=3/8 is the retained actual transverse strip bound. For integer k, I_k=[k,k+1) is an ORDINATE interval, not a physical aperture.
- For a prescribed actual-copy subset E, b_k(E)=sum_(q in E,theta_q in I_k) beta_q^2 and mu_k(E)=b_k(E)/log(e+|k|). Multiplicities contribute their inherited norm weight; they are not independent observations.
- N_E is the row restriction of COMPLETE actual negative analysis to E, with domain D_a and its canonical logarithmic norm. Compactness here is on D_a, not physical L2(-a,a). The preceding inverse-height weighted L2 operator is different.
- Good bins satisfy mu_k<=1/log log(e^e+|k|); exceptional bins violate that condition. E_good,E_exc retain all actual copies in their respective bins. They introduce no synthetic source or mass channel.
- Local transverse compactness criterion is mu_k(E)->0 as |k|->infinity. It is a uniform unit-window statement, stronger than the previously derived averaged second-count bound.
