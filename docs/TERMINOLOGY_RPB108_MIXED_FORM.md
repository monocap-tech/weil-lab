# Terminology registry — RPB-108 actual mixed form

The **absolute-symbol energy** of a Fourier representative `F` is the
integral of `|m| * ‖F‖²`. The **mixed multiplier pairing** is the genuinely
integrable expression `conj(F) * m * G`, with
`m = rightLimitCompactWeilSymbolMathlib a`. The first slot is antilinear.

The retained logarithmic-energy assumption transfers to absolute-symbol
energy using an explicit upper symbol bound. This is an attachment of the
imported EXT5 bound, not a new proof of digamma asymptotics or a derivation
of logarithmic energy from compact L2 support.

The **domain pole moment** is the integral of an L2 representative against
`exp(s*x)` over the source window. It is independent of representative
choice and complex linear. The **multiplier-plus-pole form** adds its two
cross pole terms to the normalized multiplier pairing on the same retained
domain. Defining that concrete form does not prove its equality with the
historical source form or construct a regular core representative.
