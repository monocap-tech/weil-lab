# RPB108 logarithmic bootstrap terminology

- X_k: whole-line weighted Fourier L2 space with squared weight w^(2k), w=log(exp(1)+|xi|), k a nonnegative integer. X_0 is physical L2; X_1 carries two logarithms. D_a originally requires one logarithm and supported vectors.
- Every finite logarithmic order: membership in each X_k, with a separate order-dependent constant. It does not mean any positive Sobolev exponent, analytic regularity or exponential Fourier decay.
- Support projection P_a: multiplication by 1_[-a,a], bounded on each X_k by the estimates in this promotion. It does not remove the need to prove multiplier-domain membership.
- Actual archimedean commutator: [m0(D),P_a], with the actual quarter-line digamma multiplier m0. Finite prime shifts are kept separately as T_a.
- Full mixed weak-nullity: Q_a(g,h)=0 for every g in D_a. Neither indefinite diagonal zero nor a selected-only retained record qualifies.
- Every inverse logarithmic power Gaussian rate: for each N>0 a bound M_R=O_a,N((log R)^(-N)) times physical squared norm, with order-dependent constants. It is not the exponential estimate required by the active endpoint route.

Entered before or with first load-bearing use. Historical wording remains immutable. F4 and FULL TRANSPORT CLOSED remain open.
