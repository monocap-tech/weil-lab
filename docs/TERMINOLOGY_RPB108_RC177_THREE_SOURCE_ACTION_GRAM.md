# RC177 terminology: correlated three-source canonical action Gram

- **Evaluated source family S:** (P0,P2,f0). This is the family used for the canonical calculation. The native seven-source frame (P0,psi0,f0,w14,v10,z_b6,t10) is unchanged; P2 remains an auxiliary polynomial direction.
- **Source D-Gram G:** S* D S. P2 and f0 overlap: their exact D pairing is 11/30. Their coefficients are not treated as orthogonal coordinates.
- **Canonical first-moment Gram K:** S* L S = S* D M S, where M=D^-1 L and L=D+h+R-rI is the actual supported canonical operator.
- **Canonical action Gram Q:** (M S)* D (M S). All finite and infinite parts of the three action columns are included. This is distinct from a finite compressed operator squared.
- **Nominal action Gram:** the Gram of the paid represented action carriers, before the inherited full-column errors are included. It is not substituted for Q.
- **Correlated action error Gram:** a positive matrix that bounds the error of every real or complex mixture of the three action columns. Mixed errors are paid without an orthogonality assumption.
- **Relative enclosure width:** the certified scalar beta in upper-lower <= beta G_lower. It measures the chosen enclosure, not a lower bound on the uncertainty of the true operator.
- **Canonical inverse response remains next:** RC177 evaluates G,K,Q. It does not extend RC173's two-source inverse certificate to three sources by itself.
- **Stage I remains open:** no original augmented Weil floor or full auxiliary response certificate is supplied by these canonical Gram calculations.
