# CC3 actual complement trial

Additive definitions, before use. CC1 and CC2 terminology is unchanged.

- Target split: physical Legendre span V112 of degrees 0..111 at a=21/20, and its physical orthogonal complement F112. The standing physical complement lower bound remains c112=699/1000.
- Saved witness h: the exact rational coefficient vector from the completed corrected 112-source Schur audit. Its corrected sufficient Schur form is negative; its actual native Weil value is positive.
- Trial z: a rational multiple of shifted Legendre degree 112. It belongs exactly to F112. It is not a new retained vector and does not license substituting the 113-complement lower bound.
- Actual complement trial: U=t z with rational t. Its credit comes from the residual of the original source equation, not from the old residual Gram alone.
- B_h: physical F112 projection of the actual complete interior source of h. Cz: the corresponding physical F112 projection of the actual complete interior source of z. C is the unchanged complement restriction.
- Trial credit: A t-B t^2, with A=2<B_h,Cz>/c112-2 Q(h,z) and B=||Cz||^2/c112-Q(z,z). This reduces the scalar inverse upper bound. It is not a finite-space positivity margin.
- CC3 scope: one witness direction and one actual trial source. Neither a full 113-source Gram nor a full positive target Schur form is implied.
