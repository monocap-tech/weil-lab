# RPB108 actual Legendre complement terminology

- **Physical Legendre complement:** vectors in the logarithmic carrier whose physical L2 moments against the first 64 supported Legendre polynomials vanish.
- **Coupling correction:** the nonnegative form <b_e,C^(-1)b_e> subtracted from the raw finite restriction after eliminating the proved coercive complement.
- **Actual finite Schur obstruction:** the sign of that corrected 64-dimensional form; its entries have not been certified by the earlier raw finite matrix checks.

These definitions are additive and leave historical terminology unchanged.
