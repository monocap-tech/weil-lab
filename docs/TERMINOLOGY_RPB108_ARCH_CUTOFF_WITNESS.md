# RPB108 finite archimedean cutoff witness dictionary

Introduced with NF64, 2026-10-08.

- **Physical cutoff witness**: a specified supported step function whose ENTIRE physical cutoff form Q_N, with original primes and poles, has a proved negative value. It is not a negative vector for the original untruncated form.
- **Cell correlation coefficients**: for equal-width cells and real values w_i, b_k=sum_i w_i w_(i+k). They determine the exact continuous-shift correlation of the step function by linear interpolation. They are physical values, not normalized coefficient Gram entries.
- **Resolution probe**: a floating finite restriction used to discover vectors and assess discretization. Its smallest eigenvalue is a trial upper test for the whole cutoff lowest level when the matrix is exact. Floating positive finite restrictions are not positivity certificates.

The cutoff compact gain and cutoff background keep the NF63 definitions. All original-divisor source identities continue to refer only to the original form.
