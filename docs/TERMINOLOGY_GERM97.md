## Level-indexed source-bit matrices

For j=8,9,10,11, A[j,i] denotes the shorter complete return and B[j,i] the longer, where i is 1 when its initial source is below u. These are distinct labels at each level, NOT identifications with earlier A/B matrices. The verifier stores them in separate level dictionaries under A0,A1,B0,B1.

The return-length pairs are (2,3), (3,4), (10,11), (4,5), respectively. Their formula scopes are u<=psi7, u<=sigma8, u<=c10, u<=c11. In the final twelfth-word formulas only, A_i and B_i abbreviate level j=11.

## Twelfth prefix count

In a twelfth return of length N=10 or 11, s counts the initial consecutive A1 visits before the A0 visits and final B0. Its chronological word is A1 repeated s times, A0 repeated N-s-1 times, then B0. The additional all-internal word ends with B1. This is an increasing-source ordering; it must not be reversed into the descending-source convention used in some earlier passes.

The unchanged GERM-96 chart [[1,1],[-1,3]] applies to these complete twelfth returns, not to arbitrary repetitions of a shorter elliptic map.
