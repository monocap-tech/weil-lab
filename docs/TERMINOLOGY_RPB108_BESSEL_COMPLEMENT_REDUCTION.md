# RPB108 Bessel complement reduction terminology

- **Degree-20 complement F_20:** actual form-domain vectors with physical Legendre moments 0..19 vanishing; now independently coercive.
- **Plane-wave tail bound:** the physical L2 observation bound from the real Poisson integral of spherical Bessel functions and a rational geometric tail estimate.
- **Corrected form S_k:** the exact finite form obtained by minimizing against F_k. Changing k changes the corrected form and requires lawful coupling transport.
- **Successive complement elimination:** the canonical identity identifying S_20 with the Schur complement of the positive degrees-20..63 block in S_64.
- **Remaining twelve-coordinate form S_12:** the Schur complement after eliminating the newly certified E_8 block in S_20, including its mixed coupling to degrees 8..19. Its sign remains to be enclosed.

Physical L2 completeness is proved using compact-interval polynomial approximation; it does not assert full graph density. A failed shifted-margin check does not certify actual negativity. Weak nullity refers to the mixed-form kernel.
