# NF27 terminology — constrained native retained response

Definitions are additive; historical wording is unchanged.

For one parity, E is the 56-dimensional retained E112 space, x the
unchanged retained seed of NF24/NF26, and W=E intersect x-perp in the
original physical L2 mass. v is NF26's fixed corrected trial, whose
retained component is x. F=E-perp is the original full high space.

The **native retained complement** is the restriction of the original
form to W, before eliminating F. Its positive inverse is represented
physically by K=Wmat (Wmat* A Wmat)^-1 Wmat*, where A is the original
native E matrix and Wmat is any exact full-rank basis of W. Wmat need
not be orthonormal.

The **finite retained reaction** is R_W=b* K b, b=P_E L v. It measures
energy removed by minimizing Q(v-w) over w in W with F held at the
trial's fixed high component. It is NOT the true collective reaction
after eliminating the full F space.

The **finite lifted restriction** is the original form on span(v)+W.
Its positivity follows from positive Q|W and Q(v)-R_W>0. Opposite
parities give a 112-dimensional finite restriction inside modes through
180; this restriction does not include the whole F space.

The **exact retained response trial** is a frozen rational w in W,
chosen near K b. Its mixed energy and gain are paid using the native
matrix and NF26's physical Ly source-error ball.

The **arithmetic margin comparison** NF26_score-R_W only compares two
certified numbers. It is not a collective Schur lower bound: full high
elimination also changes the retained background and mixed coupling.
