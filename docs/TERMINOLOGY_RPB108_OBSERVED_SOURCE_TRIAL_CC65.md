# CC65 terminology: observed source trial and omitted-source budget

These definitions are additive and precede the CC65 report.

Use a=53/50, E=E112, F=E-perp, C=C_full>=kappa I and kappa=207/1000.
H is the two measured high modes. The additional observed mode is e118
even or e117 odd. J=H plus that one mode is a THREE-dimensional physical
high observation space, not the full high complement.
W=F intersect J-perp is the **omitted source space**.

The **observed source objective** for a fixed trial p and correction Y is
||P_J L(p-HY)||²-kappa Q(p-HY).
Its **midpoint optimizer** uses midpoint native intervals only to choose
a vector; it is not asserted to be the exact native or complete-source
optimizer. The **frozen correction Y** rounds it onto denominator10^90.

The **combined polynomial v** is the exact original supported polynomial
p-HY, with the same retained NF18 seed as CC63. Its58 coefficient integers
and all56 retained source projection intervals are published.

The **omitted-source budget** is
B_Y=kappa Q(v)-||P_J Lv||².
The certificate publishes a strict LOWER bound. It is a permissible upper
threshold for a future omitted-source square, not a bound already obtained.

The **complete directional tail test** is ||P_W Lv||²<B_Y.
It is exactly equivalent to the scalar sufficient estimate
||P_F Lv||²<kappa Q(v), by physical orthogonal decomposition.
Its success certifies positivity on the retained seed line plus ALL F.
Its failure rejects only this sufficient estimate and this chosen correction.

A **paid omitted-source approximant** subtracts every E and J source
coordinate before squaring, using all original arch/prime/pole sectors.
Arch reconstruction and projection errors are paid before comparison to
the LOWER omitted-source budget. An unevaluated tail is not zero.
