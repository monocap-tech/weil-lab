# Shifted pole test (CC36)

Retain CC35's native parity pole decomposition. For physical level
mu>=0, Q_mu=Q-mu||h||_2^2 and B_t=i_t*i_t on the canonical domain.
The positive source P and metric M_t=P_t*P_t stay fixed. The complete
negative source becomes N_mu=(N,sqrt(mu)i_t); the mass source is the
whole physical L2 channel, not a scalar moment.

A shifted critical lift is normalized by ||Ph||=1 and satisfies
Q_mu(h,k)=delta_mu<Ph,Pk> for all old k. Define
w=(I-Pi)B_t h and q_mu=q_0+sigma a v-mu w. Its original Rayleigh
defect is Q(h)=delta_mu+mu||h||_2^2; it need not be an original source
eigenlift. All pole projections and norms use the complete dual metric.
