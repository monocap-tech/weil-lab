# RPB-108 integral-growth Gaussian supplement

Registered before first load-bearing use, 2026-10-01.

- **Integral-growth residual**: a locally integrable function with finite
  norm mass against exp(-rate |x|), rate nonnegative, and a.e. central
  cancellation on a strict enlarged interval. No pointwise bound is implicit.
- **Weighted mass**: the genuine integral of that weighted norm.
- **Radius-only envelope**: Gaussian geometry depending on the carrier support
  and enlarged radius, independently of residual representation or growth.

This is an additive interface. Actual source weak realization and the
archimedean representative remain separate obligations. Prior carriers and
historical statements are unchanged.
