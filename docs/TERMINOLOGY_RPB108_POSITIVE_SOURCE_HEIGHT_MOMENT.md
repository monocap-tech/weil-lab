# RPB108 positive-source height moment

Registered 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles), before load-bearing use.

- z_q=theta_q+i beta_q is the actual divisor ordinate, with |beta_q|<=1/2 and theta_q the actual zero height. Raw multiplicity copies stay in the sums.
- F_h(z)=integral h(x) exp(i z x) dx. Use the existing normalized raw-coordinate convention p_q=(F_h(conj z_q)+F_h(z_q))/2, n_q=(F_h(conj z_q)-F_h(z_q))/2.
- M_plus(h)=sum_q theta_q^2 |p_q(h)|^2, possibly infinite. This is a positive squared-height source moment, not a signed Weil energy or rank-one inverse-boundary observable.
- Derivative-log membership means the global distributional derivative of the unchanged physical zero extension lies in the canonical supported logarithmic domain.
- E_plus,K(T)=sum_(|theta_q|>T) sum_j |p_q(e_j)|^2 for a physical orthonormal full-kernel basis.
- Height-moment closure gate is integral_0^infinity 2T E_plus,K(T) dT<infinity.

A change to adjoint-evaluation convention flips the negative slot; its beta-coupling signs change accordingly. Energies and the moment criterion do not change. Ordinary l2 tail decay is not a squared-height moment bound.
