Definitions are additive. Historical wording is unchanged.

* **Seven-source minorant A4:** NF45's positive high operator built from seven original high polynomials per parity. It lies below the original remaining-high operator A under the explicit hypothesis A >= (207/1000) I and inherited paid attachments.
* **Joined packet:** the fixed three retained original directions in one parity, coupled to the complete remaining high space through the original retained-to-high source R.
* **Conditional odd joined pass:** positive definiteness of the complete odd three-by-three lower matrix K=Q-R*A4^-1R under that hypothesis. It closes this packet's matrix sign, not the background-floor hypothesis or the entire retained space.
* **Condensed margin s:** c-r*E^-1r for K=[[E,r],[r*,c]], with E the positive leading two-by-two block. Positive E and positive s certify the full three-coordinate sign.
* **Retained coordinate coercivity:** a rational epsilon>0 such that x*Kx >= epsilon ||x||_Euclidean^2. It is a bound in the frozen coordinate system, not a physical whole-domain gap.
* **Even response uncertainty:** the paid interval for the seventh-column gain at the NF44 even witness straddles zero. Exact minorant monotonicity remains nonnegative, but this interval certifies no positive gain.

Seven high-source polynomials are distinct from six retained directions across both parities. Whole-aperture positivity, remaining-background transport, all-cap continuation, RH, F4 and Lean remain open.
