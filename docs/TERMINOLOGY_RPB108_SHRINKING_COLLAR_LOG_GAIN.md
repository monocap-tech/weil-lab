# RPB108 terminology: shrinking-collar logarithmic gain

- **Shrinking exterior collar:** a<|x|<a+delta, adjacent to the true endpoint with no positive source gap.
- **Local concentration bound:** short-set physical L2 mass control obtained by Fourier truncation and the derived squared-logarithmic Fourier norm.
- **Shrinking-collar logarithmic gain:** ||q||_L2(collar)<=C_a||h||_2/log(1/delta). With delta=R^(-1/4), it improves M_R to O((log R)^(-4)) plus a separated stretched-exponential error.
