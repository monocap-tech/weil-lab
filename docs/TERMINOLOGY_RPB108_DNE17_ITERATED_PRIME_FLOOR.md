Positivity-preserving refers to P taking nonnegative functions to nonnegative functions. It does not mean its quadratic form is nonnegative. The original prime contribution to Q is -<f,Pf>, and a bound on the FULL operator norm controls either sign.

The weighted Schur criterion is P w<=lambda w almost everywhere, with w and 1/w bounded. It yields ||P||<=lambda on the full physical L2 carrier, not merely on a finite polynomial space. Symbolic cuts and finite rational interval values are a representation of the exact step function, not a spatial grid approximation.

The new high floor gives the complete response bound S_Q(x)>=S2(x)-P2(x)/kappa_new. A positive directional S_Q fixes a particular retained x and allows every infinite high correction. It does not certify the whole retained matrix, all other low directions, or the aperture's whole-domain sign.
