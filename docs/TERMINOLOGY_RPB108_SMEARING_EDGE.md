# Smearing edge and fixed-width stop (RPB108 NF60)

Use delta>0 fixed and alpha_delta=sinh(delta/2)/(delta/2), as in NF59. The **frozen pole-matched form** averages only original active primes log n<=2a and scales the ENTIRE original signed pole by alpha_delta.

The **prime halo** is the extra prime dictionary 2a<log n<=2a+delta needed to evaluate all nonzero averaged correlations of vectors in D_a. Adding it defines a comparison form; these primes have zero ORIGINAL unsmeared correlations and do not alter the original actual form.

The **halo pole-matched form** keeps E0 unchanged, includes all averaged primes log n<=2a+delta, and scales the entire pole by alpha_delta. It has no asserted actual source graph. Its signed multiplier identity under RH is used only as a conditional control.
