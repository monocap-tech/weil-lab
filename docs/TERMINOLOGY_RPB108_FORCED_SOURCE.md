| Term | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- |
| M_a | P_a*P_a represented in the canonical D_a inner product; the full POSITIVE source Gram, not the original signed Weil operator. |

| Lambda, G | Diagonal critical matrices with entries lambda_i and delta_i; lambda_i>0. |
| Arithmetic estimate | J M_t^-1 J*<=q Lambda G, plus an independently protected low-output cost ell with ell+q<1. |
| Pair difference kernel | At a finite divisor cutoff, exp(i theta(y-x))*cosh(beta(x-y)); the complete limit is taken after legal mixed pairing. |
| Pair sum kernel | At that cutoff, exp(i theta(y-x))*cosh(beta(x+y)); it does not disappear under partner reflection. |
