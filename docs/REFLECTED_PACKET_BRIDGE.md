\textbf{RPB-36 — STANDARD LOGARITHMIC UCP DOES NOT EXTEND TO THE WEIL COLLAR BY A LOWER-ORDER PERTURBATION ARGUMENT.}
}
\`\`\`

The pure logarithmic weak-UCP trigger requires

\`\`\`math
h=0,
\qquad
L_\Delta h=0
\`\`\`

on the same open set. For the actual Weil collar, \(h=0\) but
\(L_\Delta h\) is driven by inward prime translations and global lower-order
terms, so the second Cauchy datum is absent.

Reflection symmetry means the two collars become duplicates after parity
reduction. Even a one-delay regime does not restore the pure logarithmic UCP
hypothesis.

The remaining issue is therefore arithmetic rather than perturbative: exploit
the actual delay set \(\{\log p^m\}\), its coefficients, the interior null
equation, parity, and compact support.
