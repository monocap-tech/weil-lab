# RPB108 fractional positive-source moments

Registered 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles), before load-bearing use.

For 0<s<1 and unchanged h in D_c:
- M_plus,s(h)=sum_q |theta_q|^(2s)|p_q(h)|^2.
- V_s(h)=integral |xi|^(2s)log(e+|xi|)|hhat(xi)|^2 dxi.
- I_s(h)=integral_0^t0 ||tau_t h-h||_log^2 t^(-1-2s) dt.
- Source order r means the height exponent r=2s, not the Sobolev exponent s.
- The critical first-height source moment has r=1, s=1/2. The endpoint derivative criterion has r=2, s=1.

Complete actual pair normalization, raw multiplicity and the +iz transform convention are those of the positive-source height moment registry. Finite trace and uniform tails refer to the whole actual kernel, not a selected witness. This fractional criterion does not provide an arithmetic bound at the critical or derivative endpoint.
