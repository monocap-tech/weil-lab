# RPB108: critical absolute collar product

Definitions registered 2026-10-07 before the corresponding analytic argument.

- h is the unchanged zero extension of an actual full-native null vector in K_a intersect Xcrit; Xcrit has squared Fourier weight (1+|xi|)log(e+|xi|).
- Choose 0<delta<exp(-4) within the recovered right-edge decomposition. Put L(u)=log(1/u), f(v)=h(a-v), and r(u)=r_h(a+u)=A_h(u)-(1/2)Cf(u). Here Cf(u)=integral_0^(2a) f(v)/(u+v)dv and A_h is the actual combined prime/pole/regular-Euler drive. No summand is substituted for r.
- H_f^2=integral_0^delta |f(v)|^2 L(v)/v dv and H_r^2=integral_0^delta |r(u)|^2/[u L(u)]du. Both are finite by the pinned critical joint matching theorem.
- B_abs(t)=integral_0^t |r(u) conjugate(f(t-u))|du. Its signed counterpart is the established F_h(t)=Re integral_0^t r(u) conjugate(f(t-u))du.
- The critical absolute collar product is integral_0^t0 B_abs(t)/t^2 dt, with 0<t0<=delta. This is stronger than integral |F_h(t)|/t^2 dt, since absolute value is taken before the collar integral. It is not an absolute inverse moment integral |f(v)|/v.
- The Schur weight is p(u)=u^(-1/2). It is a weight for a positive integral-kernel estimate, not a physical vector or a packet coefficient.
- A comparison drive is an independently assigned function A^cmp(u). It has no actual native source custody and imposes no full-native interior equation. The two comparison drives in the note use the same supported physical cusp and the same Carleman profile; neither is renamed A_h.

Scope: conditional analytic consequence under actual critical membership, plus a boundary-profile control. No new whole-kernel source bound, endpoint exclusion, derivative promotion, packet attachment, enlarged same-vector null transport, Lean certificate or F4 closure.
