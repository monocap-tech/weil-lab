# NF43 terminology — fifth high polynomial from the inverse witness

These definitions are additive. Earlier definitions and historical reports remain unchanged.

- **Original remaining high background A:** the same original Weil background and physical retained projection used in NF38–NF42, at aperture a=53/50. The hypothesis A >= (207/1000)I is explicit and is not established by this packet.
- **Four high columns H4:** the NF38 original two high polynomials and the two NF40 boundary polynomials, with the first two divided by recorded rational upper physical norms. This invertible coordinate rescaling preserves their physical span and the NF42 minorant.
- **Four high source minorant A1:** kappa I + U4 C4^-1 U4*, with U4=(A-kappa I)H4 and C4=H4*U4. This is NF42's same operator, in rescaled coordinates.
- **Inverse-coupled witness q:** A1^-1 R h, where R consists of the three unchanged NF38 joined source columns and h is the unchanged NF38 rational witness. Its coordinates are enclosed using the original signed NF42 packet and the Woodbury formula.
- **Fifth high polynomial y:** restrict q to physical Legendre degrees 116..180 even or 117..179 odd; down-round coordinate midpoints to denominator 10^100; project exactly off the two restricted old high columns; divide by a rational upper physical norm. Its boundary coordinates vanish, so it is physically orthogonal to all four old high columns. Midpoints choose y only; they do not prove an original form sign or exact equality y=q.
- **Five high source minorant A2:** kappa I + U5 C5^-1 U5*, where H5=(H4,y), U5=(A-kappa I)H5, C5=H5*U5. Under the original floor hypothesis, A >= A2 >= A1. This ordering requires the certified positive C5.
- **Conditional extra inverse response:** R*(A1^-1-A2^-1)R. It is a positive semidefinite lower contribution to R*(A1^-1-A^-1)R under that same floor hypothesis. Its full signed matrix is computed from original native and complete source moments, with physical source error payments.
- **Five high joined lower certificate:** Q-R*A2^-1 R. A negative value of this lower bound is a failure of this certificate, not a negative original Weil-form value.

All Gram matrices keep the original same physical vectors. A2 is a lower bound for the actual operator, not a claim that the actual remaining high background is finite rank. Full remaining background transport, whole-domain positivity at 53/50, RH, F4 and Lean closure remain separate obligations.
