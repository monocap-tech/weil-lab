# RC179 terminology: finite focus residual direction and canonical action

- **Actual focus residual rho:** (S-M S Y)x from RC178's selected inverse trial, with x=(-1/1200,-1/6,1). It is the residual for f0-P2/6-P0/1200, not that source itself.
- **Nominal focus residual:** (S-VY)x, using RC177's complete represented action carriers. Its distance to rho remains paid by RC178's correlated action error.
- **Finite focus direction q:** the rational-center even Legendre prefix through degree 256 of the nominal focus residual. Once selected, q is an exact finite polynomial. It is not an exact representation of rho.
- **Input approximation error:** the full D-norm distance from rho to q, including retained constant/rounding uncertainty, omitted finite coefficients, the native analytic tail, and the prior correlated action error.
- **Canonical action on q:** Mq=q+D^-1 hq-rD^-1 q+D^-1 Rq, for the actual supported canonical M=D^-1 L. The endpoint and regular output tails are paid in full.
- **Action on rho by transfer:** the stored Mq trial approximates M rho with an additional 2||rho-q||_D error, since ||M||_D<=2. This larger transferred error is distinct from the action error on the exact chosen polynomial q.
- **New inverse trial direction:** q is available for an enlarged inverse trial space. Its mixed Gram/action rows and the resulting inverse enclosure are not evaluated in RC179.
- **Native frame unchanged:** the seven native sources retain their meanings. q is an auxiliary trial vector, not a replacement source frame. The three-source half-Gram gate and Stage I remain open.
