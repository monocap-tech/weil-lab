# RC184 terminology: five-source canonical inverse enclosure

- **Response sources:** (P0,P2,f0,psi0,w14), in the even sector. This expands the previously accepted four-source family.
- **Selected trial basis:** the same five directions. The earlier auxiliary q is not retained in this trial basis because its near overlap with w14 broadens raw interval congruences. Its historical certificate remains unchanged.
- **Actual inverse enclosure:** bounds on S* D M^-1 S for the supported canonical M=D^-1 L. All infinite source/action tails and correlated errors are paid. Validity of an enclosure does not imply it passes the requested width gate.
- **Structural versus residual bounds:** global M bounds supply G/2 and G/a. Residual-based inverse bounds supply additional valid matrices. The validator evaluates complete lower/upper combinations and selects a width; it does not take entrywise matrix minima or maxima.
- **Adverse first-moment check:** comparing a chosen K lower to a G upper, or a K upper to 2 G lower, is a stronger interval consistency check. A failure does not invalidate the independently proved actual inequalities aG<=K<=2G or the certified entry enclosure.
- **Five-source half-Gram gate:** inverse upper-minus-lower <= G_lower/2. RC184's selected enclosure fails this gate.
- **Failure witness:** an exact rational source mixture with negative quadratic form in the selected gate matrix. It proves failure of this enclosure, not intrinsic inverse uncertainty or impossibility of a better trial.
- **Focus residual:** the full selected inverse-trial residual for that witness mixture. A paid nonzero residual supplies a concrete next enrichment direction.
- **Scope:** four-source gate closure remains intact. Five-source coverage is now enclosed, but five-source precision acceptance, full native coverage, and Stage I remain open.
