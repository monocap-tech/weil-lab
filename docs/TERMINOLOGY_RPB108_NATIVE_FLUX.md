# RPB108 native flux terminology

CC27, 2026-10-08. Use the original normalized form Q=P*P-N*N.

- **Native residual:** J_i(f)=Q(h_i,f)-delta_i<P h_i,P f>, for the
  actual generalized old critical lift h_i from CC20. J_i kills the old
  domain. It is a bounded canonical dual row, not an asserted L2 function.
- **Native pieces:** A is the digamma archimedean form, Pi the positive
  finite prime translation form, and C the Hermitian cross-pole form.
  Q=A-Pi+C. The residual is A_i-Pi_i+C_i-delta_i M_i.
- **Exterior test:** a smooth compact f whose support is positively
  separated from that of h. It is a diagnostic physical test, not a
  replacement for the positive-source shell or its full covariance.
- **Continuous off-diagonal kernel:** for d>0,
  C_off(d)=2 cosh(d/2)-exp(-d/2)/(1-exp(-2d)). This notation does not
  identify the kernel with the pole form C alone. It applies off the
  diagonal; the full archimedean form needs its local renormalization.
- **Prime atom:** coefficient -Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) at each of the two
  displacement locations +/-log n. All active prime powers and threshold
  conventions are retained. Old prime atoms can couple to a new strip.
- **Sign-based mechanism:** infer cancellation or a one-sided localization
  estimate from a fixed sign of the native off-diagonal interactions.
  CC27 tests this premise against actual coefficients on smooth tests.
- **Defect-relative target:** J M_t^-1 J* <= q Lambda G, q<1, together
  with a compatible low-output budget. It concerns all mixed rows and
  the whole incoming domain. The kernel tests do not evaluate it.

No physical coercivity margin is substituted for delta_i. The shifted
positive-level control includes the entire physical mass channel.
