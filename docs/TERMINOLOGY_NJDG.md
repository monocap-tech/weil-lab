## Registry purpose

This file registers every new term used by the NEXTJET derivative-geometry investigation before or with its first load-bearing use.

The canonical Key-C theorem targets remain in `monocap-tech/provenance`:

Nothing in this registry renames, weakens, or supersedes those canonical obligations.

## NJDG

**NJDG** = **NEXTJET Derivative Geometry**.

A lab investigation testing whether actual-zeta derivative-zero geometry, selected-data inverse control, or independently bounded resummed resolvent statistics can imply the canonical NEXTJET/KPH obligations.

NJDG is not itself a theorem family and does not advance the canonical Key-C cursor.

## target-conditioned inverse

An inverse or Moore-Penrose quantity evaluated only on the one selected datum generated by the dangerous packet/channel, rather than a full operator smallest-singular-value bound.

NJDG may test whether such a quantity is weaker than `C-ACTUAL-KPH-FLOOR`.

No weakening is licensed unless an exact implication to the canonical target is proved.

## derivative-geometry transfer

A theorem, if one exists, that uses zeros of (Xi'), (zeta'), or higher derivatives to exclude or quantitatively suppress the selected/exterior collision geometry that makes the weighted NEXTJET functional large.

This is the primary new external-search species opened by NJDG.

## resummed complement resolvent

A non-fixed-order logarithmic-derivative / higher-resolvent representation of the complement response, intended to package many reciprocal-jet terms into one analytic object.

Important custody rule: SOURCE-II-4 already supplies a finite higher-resolvent jet-tower normal form. NJDG must not count a mere representation change as progress unless the new representation brings an independent actual-zeta estimate with the correct worst-packet quantifier.

## complement-oblivious selection order

The required quantifier order inherited from SOURCE-II:

1. select the packet/channel;
2. choose the bounded-depth multiplier using only selected data, completion, and authorized fixed prime bands;
3. freeze that multiplier;
4. only then read or bound the unselected complement.

Any construction choosing the multiplier after observing complement jets violates this order and is not an admissible NEXTJET input.

## worst-packet moat

A projective lower bound on selected/exterior separation, or any equivalent actual-zeta geometric statement, strong enough to keep the weighted NEXTJET functional within the required projective scale for every admitted dangerous packet.

A moat is one possible sufficient input; NJDG does not assume one exists.

## signed-cancellation route

A theorem controlling the final signed weighted NEXTJET sum directly without separately bounding each reciprocal derivative or each selected/exterior gap.

This remains logically distinct from a moat theorem and from a full KPH floor.

## Governance

Historical terminology is immutable. Corrections are additive.

No term in this registry may be promoted to canonical `monocap-tech/provenance` terminology without a separate custody and theorem-interface audit.
