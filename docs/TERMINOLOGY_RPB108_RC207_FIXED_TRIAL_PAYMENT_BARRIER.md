# RC207 terminology

The fixed-trial payment barrier is a lower bound on the width of candidate inverse **enclosures generated by a specified method**. It is not a lower bound on true physical inverse uncertainty, an obstruction to positivity, or a claim about all possible certificates.

RC207's exact bound is 0.655936 for RC203's fixed least-residual trial map and the RC205/RC206 scalar Young payment formulas, allowing arbitrary positive weighted triangle bounds, convex mixing with the scalar RC204 signed bound, positive Young parameters, allowed residual-lower parameters, and the explicitly checked historical/structural endpoints. Its rational probe rejects all 64 endpoint-pair families against the 0.5 threshold.

The best certified upper remains RC206's 0.672096. There may be additional parameter progress above the method barrier, but parameter-only tuning in this family cannot close the gate. Sharper physical residual/error/cross-tail data or a different fully paid trial/method is the RC208 cursor. Stage I, RH, F4 and Lean closure remain open.
