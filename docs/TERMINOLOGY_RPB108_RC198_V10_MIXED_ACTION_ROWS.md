# RC198 terminology

**Expanded six-response family** is P0,P2,f0,psi0,w14,v10. The seven raw trial vectors are P0,P2,f0,psi0,w14,q185,v10. Auxiliary P2 and q185 do not replace native sources or enlarge the definition of the seven-native-source frame.

**Complete v10 mixed rows** are D-source pairings, canonical first-moment pairings and represented/actual action-energy pairings of v10 with all seven raw trial vectors. All retained degrees through65534, omitted infinite tails and full individual action errors are included.

**Self-adjoint mixed intersection** uses both <s_i,Mv10> evaluated with v10's action error and <v10,Ms_i> evaluated with the other source's action error. Their independently paid intervals enclose the same actual pairing and are intersected without assuming the represented actions are exactly self-adjoint.

**Expanded Gram independence certificate** means that a directed finite-prefix Loewner lower is PSD and nonsingular for the seven raw source vectors. The positive full D-tail Gram preserves this lower. It establishes source independence, not expanded inverse acceptance.
