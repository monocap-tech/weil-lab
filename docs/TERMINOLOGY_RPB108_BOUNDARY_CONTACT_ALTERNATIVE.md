# Boundary-contact alternative (RPB108, post-CC39)

**Contact null:** A nonzero h in the canonical supported space D_a with Q_a nonnegative on D_a and Q_a(h,h)=0. By polarization/Cauchy-Schwarz for a nonnegative form, Q_a(h,f)=0 for all f in D_a.

**One-sided support slack:** h is zero almost everywhere on an interval adjacent to at least one endpoint of [-a,a]. **Boundary-saturated:** h has no one-sided support slack at either endpoint; equivalently the essential closed support contains -a and +a.

**Frozen stationary correlation:** CC28's q_h^a(r), defined by the exact finite-cap native Fourier multiplier with every active prime-power shift and both original signed pole cross terms, even when a translate no longer fits D_a. It agrees with Q_a(h,tau_r h) whenever both physical vectors are supported in [-a,a]; and q_h^a(-r)=conj(q_h^a(r)).

**Boundary-contact exclusion target:** An as-yet-unproved arithmetic-specific mechanism excluding a boundary-saturated contact null without requiring the CC19/CC35 old-gap-relative full incoming covariance bound. The target must survive both genuine first-contact and shifted positive-level controls. Boundary saturation by itself is a necessary condition, not an exclusion.
