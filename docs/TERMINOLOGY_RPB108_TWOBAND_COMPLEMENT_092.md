# RPB108 two-band complement terminology

At a=23/25, W_84 is the unchanged physical Legendre moment complement.
For normalized f in W_84, M_f(U)=integral_{|t|<U}|Fourier(f)(t)|^2.
rho(U) is the existing complete depth-six integrated Bessel upper bound
for M_f(U), with degrees 84–131 and all degrees from 132 onward retained.
The two-band cutoffs are S=263/20 and T=72/5. The archimedean lower
function is g(u)=log(u)-7/(216u^2), u>=1. This is a frequency comparison,
not a replacement of the actual archimedean symbol or prime/pole form.
