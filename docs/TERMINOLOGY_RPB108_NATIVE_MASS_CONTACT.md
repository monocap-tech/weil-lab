Registered 2026-10-06 before the companion theorem's first load-bearing use.

- `Q_a`: unchanged full actual native Weil form, including all prescribed primes and the signed physical pole, on canonical `D_a`.
- `lambda_ph(a)`: infimum of `Q_a(h)` over physical-L2-mass-one `h` in `D_a`; distinct from the lower spectral bound with logarithmic norm one.
- `B_a^mu=Q_a-mu ||h||_2^2`: scalar mass-shift comparison form. It is actual `Q_a` exactly when `mu=0`.
- `K_a^mu`: full mixed kernel of `B_a^mu`; for `mu=lambda_ph(a)` it is the lowest physical eigenspace of the actual native form.
- `c=23/25`, `mu_c=lambda_ph(c)`: attained comparison-contact level, positive by the certified actual mass bound. No numerical eigenvalue is claimed.
- `J_a`: physical inclusion from logarithmic domain to supported L2. Added negative mass analysis `sqrt(mu_c) J_a` is artificial and is not an actual divisor selection.
- `R_t^mu`: finite polynomial observations chosen from the added mass analysis to separate the comparison kernel; not the actual negative-coordinate selection of the global F4 construction.
- `e_s^mu`: compensator gain excess for the comparison packet; the quadratic gain proof applies after explicit mass-shift domain and translation checks.
