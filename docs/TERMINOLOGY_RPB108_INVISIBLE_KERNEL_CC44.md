# Pole-moment invisible kernel (CC44)

On a real parity sector, the pole moment is the bounded physical functional
c(h)=integral h(x)cosh(x/2)dx (even), or s(h)=integral h(x)sinh(x/2)dx (odd).
An invisible kernel is ker H intersected with the kernel of that functional,
where H is the complete native pole-free supported operator of CC43.
It concerns physical eigenvectors, not a chosen source normalization.
A susceptibility is a supremum of squared moment divided by a nonnegative
quadratic form. Its extended value is infinity if a zero-energy vector has
nonzero moment. Zero over zero contributes zero only when the moment vanishes.
An invisible spectral floor means a strictly positive lower physical
Rayleigh bound on the moment-zero subspace. It is an additional estimate,
not a consequence of a scalar susceptibility bound.
