# RPB108 — CC105 selected-source reconstruction

Definitions are additive; historical CC104 intervals and claims retain their scopes.

**Combined physical trial** is the exact rational linear combination of the unchanged Native Source P,T53 columns selected by CC104's late midpoint LDL candidate. Its Legendre coefficients are combined before evaluating the original source, then divided by a rational upper bound for its exact physical norm. Candidate selection supplies a vector, not a sign proof.

**Selected source star** consists of the complete projected source square of that combined trial, its signed source crosses with every NF52 high column, and each high-column source square. Missing high/high off-diagonal entries remain inherited from NF52; this is not a new full source Gram.

**Fresh original native trial row** is the original Weil pairing of the combined trial with itself and every NF52 high column, computed from its reconstructed complete original source with physical L2 error payments. It does not reuse an entrywise cancellation of historical native matrices.

**Reconstructed scalar response bound** is q-g/kappa+w N^-1 w*/kappa^2, kappa=289/500, where q is the fresh original native trial energy, g its complete projected source square, w its complete trial/high source row minus kappa times its native trial/high row, and N is the authenticated NF52 high inverse denominator at the common floor. A negative upper endpoint rejects this sufficient bound on the selected physical vector; it does not establish a negative original Weil vector.

**Primary/replay source construction** means two fresh analytic reconstructions with different regular-kernel truncation orders and outward Decimal precisions. These use the same inherited analytic identities, so they are two fresh runs rather than a claim of two independent derivations of those identities. Rational payment and sign audits are separate from the producers.

**Conditional target floor** is kappa=29/50 used only to evaluate the response and plain criteria on the same original-source data. Positive selected response values at this target are conditional on proving that original high floor; they are neither a newly certified floor nor a complete collective positivity certificate.
