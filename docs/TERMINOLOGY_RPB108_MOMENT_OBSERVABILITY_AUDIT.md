# RPB108 inverse-moment observability audit terminology

Base: ef46f5691790d06d40cc521ca4434962f2f1643e. Registered with first use.

- Moment-null profile: a profile f with a well-defined inverse boundary moment integral f(v)dv/v=0. This is not an actual mixed weak-null vector.
- Edge-saturated comparison profile: an explicit profile with positive mass in every collar at both edges; it is used only to audit an implication.
- Actual-kernel moment injectivity: the proposed implication M(h)=0 implies h=0 within the actual full mixed weak-null space, after first proving M is well-defined there. It remains unproved and is not supplied by the averaged kernel identity.
- Moment observability failure on the carrier: nonzero carrier profiles have zero moment, so this single scalar observation has no positive mass lower bound on that carrier or its parity sector.
