# RPB108 NF54 terminology

**Complete NF53 witness response** uses all P and T53 coordinates of the
inherited exact joint lower-bound witness through the shared minorant inverse.
Its rational approximation selects a polynomial; original signed pairings
and interval proofs independently determine its effect.

**Enlarged high family** means thirteen even or twelve odd high physical
polynomials. The new direction is exactly orthogonal to every inherited
high column on the degree-308/307 shell.

**Inherited source custody** preserves historical rational interval endpoints,
errors and norm bounds. The NF53 appended source is reconstructed at the
800/900-digit grids with its frozen retained midpoint projection.

**Complete covariance payment** includes endpoint logarithms, signed pole,
all 13 prime cells and the physical error cross terms
`e_i n_j+e_j n_i+e_i e_j`, including infinite analytic remainders.

**Correlated gain** cancels the common Xi Gram exactly and checks a positive
rank-one inverse-reaction update. A positive gain may leave a witness negative.

**Lifted witness** has a strictly positive independently certified lower-bound
value. This checks that vector; the complete matrix needs its own sign proof.

**Rejecting witness** is an exact rational vector with a certified negative
sufficient lower-matrix value and original physical mass. It does not prove
an original negative Weil vector.

**Whole gate** is the original `D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities, with the
same high inverse for all original sources. Its status is stated in the
checkpoint ledger and accompanying note.

**Unresolved even sign probe** is a frozen exact rational direction whose
enclosure under the certified interval lower matrix spans zero. All 15
selected nonpositive midpoint-pivot directions have this status. A negative
midpoint alone is not a certified rejection.

**NF54 full-bound status** is unresolved even and rejected odd. Both NF53
witnesses are lifted, but whole positivity at 1.06, RH, F4 and Lean remain
open. The certified whole-aperture anchor remains 1.05.
