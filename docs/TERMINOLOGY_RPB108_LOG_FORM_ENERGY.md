# RPB-108 terminology — graph energy attachment

Graph energy attachment means equality of the constructed weighted Fourier L2 coordinate's squared norm with the canonical logarithmic energy, and equality of its L2 inner product with the same-domain mixed logarithmic integral. Genuine convergence is proved from actual L2 coordinates.

It does not mean attachment of the imported WD-T38 source quadratic/null identity, the full Weil symbol or the actual physical carrier. Those identifications remain separate obligations. No spectral-product L2 membership is used.
