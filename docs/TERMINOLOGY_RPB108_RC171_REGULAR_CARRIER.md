# RC171 terminology: regular carrier and canonical first action

The **regular constant-source carrier trial** is the finite even Legendre polynomial approximating D^-1 R P0 through degree 256. Its error pays the represented Poisson action, the pi replacement, far-node Taylor remainder, coefficient and D-constant rounding, and every omitted Legendre coefficient of the actual regular action.

The **weighted second-derivative tail** uses the physical Legendre differential operator A=-d/dx((B^2-x^2)d/dx). Its eigenvalues are n(n+1). A bound for ||A R P0||_2 pays the full D^-1 tail, with the first omitted even degree 258. A is a projection-estimate operator and is distinct from A_Q, the original Weil operator.

The **canonical constant-source first action** is D^-1 L P0. It combines the new regular polynomial with RC126's exact f0+psi0 endpoint closure and a paid constant coefficient. It is one action column, not an inverse iteration or a full-source action matrix.

The **constant-direction canonical metric interval** bounds <P0,L P0> in the actual canonical metric. It is a single-vector result and does not improve a global canonical or original Weil coercivity floor.
