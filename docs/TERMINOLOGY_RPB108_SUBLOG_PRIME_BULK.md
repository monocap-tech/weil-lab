# RPB108 sublogarithmic prime bulk

Registered 2026-10-07 UTC before use. Consume the good-height contour registry unchanged.

- A finite family F satisfies the weak logarithmic translation hypothesis when every h belongs to D_a and D_w(t;h)=O_h(|t|). Actual full-null kernels and the pinned actual positive eigenmode satisfy it by existing proofs.
- Z(s)=zeta'(s)/zeta(s), and B_prime,F is B_F with A replaced by Z. It is an actual absolutely convergent prime-series contribution on c=3/2, not an artificial row sum.
- q_F(t)=sum_h [|H_h(c+it)|^2+|H_h(1-c+it)|^2+|H_h(c-it)|^2+|H_h(1-c-it)|^2], t>0.
- Omega_F(T)=integral_T^infinity log(e+t)q_F(t)dt is a physical Fourier tail; it is distinct from the discrete source tail V_F(T).
- C_gamma,F(T)=(1/(2pi)) integral_(t0)^T t log t Re[W_F(c+it)+W_F(c-it)]dt is the leading gamma bulk. It need not be pointwise positive.
- Sublogarithmic means O(1+log log T), hence o(log T), in this note. No bounded-return assertion follows.
- Primes being subleading in this fixed-vector contour formula does not remove their role in determining whether an actual unshifted null vector exists.
