# DNE20 terminology — response-plane physical gap

These definitions are additive; earlier terminology and claims are immutable.

**Response retained plane Z4.** At a=53/50, let x_even,x_odd be the
authenticated NF24 retained seeds and w_even,w_odd the frozen NF27
rational retained responses. Z4=span(x_even,w_even,x_odd,w_odd). Within
each parity, x and w are exactly physically orthogonal and nonzero.

**Lift family.** Columns T=(v,t) have retained components (x,w). v is
NF26's fixed seed lift. NF29 uses t=w-h on two high modes per parity;
NF30 subtracts a further fixed even correction on modes116 through180.
Both families represent the SAME retained plane after all of F112 is
allowed. The trial spaces before adding F112 differ.

**Complete coarse matrix U.** Q2 is the original native energy of the
lift columns, Gamma is their COMPLETE source Gram into F112, and
U=Q2-Gamma/kappa. It is a lower Hermitian form for the true retained
Schur restriction. Its mixed entry is not an enclosure of the true
inverse-weighted mixed entry.

**Physical retained mass M.** In a parity, M=diag(||x||^2,||w||^2).
The response has a small physical norm; its coordinate must not be
treated as a normalized Legendre vector.

**Physical completion budget.** eta^2 bounds trace(Gamma M^-1)/kappa^2;
xi^2 bounds trace(H*H M^-1), where H=T-retained(T). These bound the
actual inverse-source and chosen-lift operators in physical norm.

**Six-direction retained union.** Adding e0,e1 to Z4 gives six independent
retained directions. DNE18 and DNE20 certify different four-direction
restrictions. Their combined six-direction span requires additional
same-parity mixed control and is not certified here.
