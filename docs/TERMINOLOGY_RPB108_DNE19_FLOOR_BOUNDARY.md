# DNE19 terminology — fixed-budget floor boundary

These definitions are additive. They do not revise the historical DNE17,
DNE18, NF28, CC70 or CC71 terminology.

**Original prime operator P.** On physical L2([-a,a]), a=53/50, extend f
by zero and set P f(x)=sum_n Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) [f(x+log n)+f(x-log n)],
for n=2,3,4,5,7,8. P is bounded, self-adjoint and positivity preserving.
Positivity preserving does not mean its quadratic form is positive.

**Fixed archimedean-minus-pole budget L.** The conservative rational
number L=2772351243732/10^12 inherited from DNE17's NF10-based high
complement estimate. It is a lower-bound budget, not an upper bound on
the actual archimedean form. This checkpoint holds this chosen number fixed.

**Global-prime-norm subtraction family.** High-floor certificates of the
form kappa <= L-U, where U is a valid upper bound on the FULL physical
operator norm ||P||. This definition excludes a new larger L, a
high-compressed or parity-specific prime estimate, signed arch/prime
correlation, and inverse-response estimates. A barrier for this family
is not an upper bound on the actual high operator's spectrum.

**Raw retained response w.** NF27's frozen rational response in the
55-dimensional retained seed-orthogonal complement, without a newly
chosen high lift. NF28 supplies Q(w,w) and ||P_F L_original w||^2, F=E112-perp.

**Necessary scalar-floor threshold rho_w.** The ratio of that source
square to that native energy. The sufficient coarse matrix Q-Gamma/kappa
cannot be positive if kappa <= rho_w. This is a necessary condition for
the estimator to pass, not for actual positivity.

**Rayleigh norm witness.** f=P1, where 1 is the constant physical L2
function. <f,Pf>/||f||^2 <= ||P||. The witness concerns the global prime
operator; it need not lie in F112 or the Weil form domain.
