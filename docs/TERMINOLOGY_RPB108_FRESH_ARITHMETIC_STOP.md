# RPB108 fresh arithmetic and stop-interface terminology

For an actual vector k in D_c, rho(xi)=|Fourier(k)(xi)|^2, M=integral rho=||k||_2^2, and E=integral log(e+|xi|)rho. The actual pole diagonal is p=2 Re(conjugate(M_-(k))M_+(k)), and may be negative.

Given |m_c-w|<=C and |p|<=LM, the **mass reallocation** is mu=m_c-L and pi=p+LM. It preserves the complete form: pi+integral mu rho=p+integral m_c rho. pi is a nonnegative bounded comparison remainder, not the original physical pole. The physical frozen action always retains the original m_c and exponential cross-pole source.

For c<b with no new prime threshold between them, the **bounded weak stop carrier** is Hext=D_b. W_< is the logarithmic Riesz operator of the frozen strict-c multiplier plus original pole on D_b; W_<= is the frozen right-limit-c operator there. Endpoint observation is J*:D_b->D_c for unchanged-physical inclusion J, and enlarged observation is the identity on D_b. J* is a weak-form test compression, not pointwise restriction of an output density.
