# DNE23 terminology — coherent eight-direction union

Definitions are additive and do not amend historical wording.

**Joint retained plane Z8.** At a=53/50,
span(e0,e1,x_even,w_even,u_even,x_odd,w_odd,u_odd), using the same
frozen vectors as DNE21/DNE22. Each parity has four independent vectors.

**Fresh low/probe pairing.** Q(e0,u_even) or Q(e1,u_odd), obtained from
the complete original source of the low Legendre mode paired against
NF32's fixed rational probe, with an outward uniform source-error payment.
It is not inferred from an approximate eigenvector equation.

**Three-column coarse form V.** For T3=(v,t,u), Gamma3 is its complete
physical source Gram into F112 and V=Q(T3,T3)-Gamma3/kappa, kappa=11/25.
DNE22 supplies a positive fixed rational congruence for V.

**Source Gram domination.** The additional certified matrix inequality
Gamma3<=V on these three fixed columns. It is not a uniform source
estimate for the whole retained space or a bound on actual critical vectors.

**Coherent low-source cross bound.** With r0=P_F L e_j and
g0_i=<r0,P_F L T3_i>, the SAME actual source Gram implies
|g0 z|^2<=||r0||^2 z*Gamma3 z<=P0 z*V z. These crosses are constrained
together; they are not unrelated independently chosen scalar intervals.

**Native low row p.** p_i=Q(e_j,T3_i). The first two components are
NF31's paid native coordinates; the third is the fresh low/probe pairing.
Its V-dual square is bounded by the prior rational congruence margin.

**Joint low diagonal alpha.** With A=Q(e_j,e_j)-P0/kappa and
M>=||p||_(V^-1)+sqrt(P0)/kappa, alpha=A-M^2/(1-theta), theta=1/10.
Positive alpha implies the coarse joint lower form is at least
diag(alpha,theta V). This is a joint sufficient bound, not an evaluated
true inverse covariance.
