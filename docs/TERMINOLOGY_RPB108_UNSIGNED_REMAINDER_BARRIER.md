# Unsigned native remainder barrier (CC34)

Use CC33's complete native decomposition Q=E_log+R_B, canonical
C_t=i_t*R_B i_t, positive metric M_t=P_t*P_t, and cap constants
c_B||h||_D<=||Ph||<=U_B||h||_D. A critical lift has ||Ph||=1,
defect delta=1-lambda>0, and Q(h,k)=delta<Ph,Pk> on D_s.

The unsigned remainder envelope V_B(h) is the sum of the absolute
diagonal contributions of the archimedean remainder, EVERY active
prime-power shift pair, and the two cross-paired pole terms. One may
enlarge the archimedean contribution to r_*||h||_2^2. It is a bound
on |<R_Bh,h>|, not an outward source-shell energy.

Pi denotes canonical projection D_t->D_s. The outward remainder
forcing is q=(I-Pi)C_t h. Its relevant norm is ||M_t^-1/2 q||.
Neither V_B nor ||Pi C_t h|| is this norm. Physical mass ||h||_2^2
is distinct from source defect delta and from the certified physical margin.
