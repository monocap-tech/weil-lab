# RPB108 — DNE27 terminology: sharper coherent payment on the unchanged frame

Definitions are additive; all earlier certificates and wording are preserved.

**Unchanged tested frame:** the DNE23 four-column original frame (low,v,t,u) per parity, whose retained components jointly span Z8. It is not the changed NF38 selected frame from DNE26.

**Three-column coarse form V:** the original native matrix Q3 minus its complete F112 source Gram Gamma3 divided by kappa=11/25. DNE22 certifies V>=g diag(s_i^-2)>0.

**Source domination factor rho:** a rational number in (0,1) for which rho V-Gamma3 is positive definite. DNE27 pays this entire signed matrix with outward interval congruence/Gershgorin margins. It is a bound on this particular complete three-column source family, not a uniform aperture estimate.

**Coherent low border norm M:** sqrt(d_native)+sqrt(rho P0)/kappa, where d_native is the inherited native low row's V-dual square bound and P0 bounds the complete low source square on F112. Source Gram Cauchy supplies the same V norm for all three uncomputed low-source crosses together.

**Young split theta:** a rational number in (0,1), giving alpha=A_low-M^2/(1-theta). When alpha>0, the entire four-column coarse form is bounded below by diag(alpha,theta V), hence by L=diag(alpha,theta g/s_i^2).

**Native source contraction credit c:** a positive rational with L>=(c/kappa)Q4, where Q4 is the original four-column native matrix. Then Gamma4<=(kappa-c)Q4. c is an energy-normalized source credit, distinct from a physical L2 gap.

**Paid grid choice:** the smallest successful rho on the denominator-1000 grid and the best sufficient row-comparison credit among denominator-1000 theta choices. Credits are rounded downward to denominator 100000 and their strict margins are checked again. This is finite exact rational selection; no claim of continuous or globally optimal parameters is made.

**Remaining-frame budget:** DNE25's exact native energy orthogonal remainder Y retains its definition. A strict complete inequality c B_Y-Gamma_Y>0 with the new c would suffice for whole 1.06 positivity. For a frozen rational remainder, the corresponding condition is r+kappa epsilon<c. Neither condition is certified on the remaining frame in DNE27.
