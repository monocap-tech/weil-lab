# RPB108: scalar-method gate and 112-vector preflight

Additive definitions, 2026-10-07 UTC. Historical definitions remain unchanged.

- A necessary scalar complement threshold is a lower bound imposed by one exact rational vector on positivity of Q-c^-1 R_actual. Source perturbation is included in the direction most favorable to positivity. It is not a sufficient all-direction threshold.
- A conditional Schur certificate supplies a hypothetical scalar c and proves positivity of the corrected finite Schur matrix. It does not prove that c bounds the actual infinite-dimensional complement.
- A scalar band-method output ceiling bounds the largest lower estimate obtainable from the specified positive Bessel region and a fixed proved prime majorant, after omitting nonnegative frequency-mass and pole penalties. It does not upper-bound the actual complement or the actual Weil form.
- The 112-vector complement acts on the subspace orthogonal to physical Legendre degrees 0 through 111. Its estimate does not replace the 96-vector complement on the larger subspace orthogonal to degrees 0 through 95. A new matched native block, source and Gram must establish the 112-vector coupling sign.
- The degree-111 diagonal preflight audits a truncation budget for that one native entry. It does not certify the remaining entries, native finite positivity or source-map accuracy.
