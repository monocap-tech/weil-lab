# DNE47 terminology: response trial gate

Registered before DNE47 load-bearing computation. Parent DNE46,
`61b896309a4c426a9259290b92dbd039a26f96ef`.

* **Positive-region shell route**: fixed F112, the inherited multiplier
  b(T)=log(T)-7/(216 T^2), positive-region top cutoff
  (2 a pi T)^2<112*113, nonnegative shell/pole payments, and subtraction
  of a valid global prime operator norm upper. This definition does not
  cover all possible arch estimates or changing the retained cutoff.
* **Physical high trial frame Y**: the three nonconstant scaled tested
  even columns projected exactly onto F112, then divided by rational
  upper bounds for their physical norms. This is a trial space for the
  original high inverse, not an evaluated inverse.
* **Residual response upper**: for trial coefficients J and L_F>=kI,
  replace the inverse of the residual f-L_F YJ by its paid bound 1/k.
  This controls the full response matrix, including positive/negative
  packet mixed entries.

The current positivity standing is 87 retained directions and the infinite
high floor 647/1000. DNE47 geometry or algebra alone does not increase it.
New high-trial action correlations must be integrated and validated before
the response criterion can certify the last even packet direction.
