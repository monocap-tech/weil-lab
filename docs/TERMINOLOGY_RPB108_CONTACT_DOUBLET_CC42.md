# RPB108 — CC42 contact doublet and strict interior positivity

**Supported native zero-energy packet:** A nonzero canonical physical vector h in D_a with the complete ORIGINAL signed Weil value Q_a(h,h)=0. This definition does not assume Q_a is nonnegative; a contact null additionally assumes it.

**Native translate correlation at cap b>a:** For |r|<b-a, c_b(h;r)=Q_b(h,tau_r h), tau_r h(x)=h(x-r), both zero extended into D_b. The complete arithmetic native form includes the archimedean digamma multiplier, all active prime-power translations and both Hermitian signed poles. CC28's frozen stationary correlation agrees on this supported neighborhood and is NOT real analytic at r=0 for h nonzero.

**Contact doublet:** The two physical vectors h and tau_r h, each of original zero energy, and their mixed native correlation c_b(h;r). Whenever c_b(h;r)!=0 their signed 2x2 form Gram has eigenvalues +/-|c_b(h;r)| (these are Gram-matrix eigenvalues, not physical normalized operator eigenvalues).

**Immediate outward indefiniteness:** For any finite b>a, every nonzero supported native zero-energy packet at a produces a negative-energy linear combination in D_b. No quantitative uniform step, inverse old-source defect, global positivity, H1 trace, or zero location estimate is included in this assertion.

**Strict interior inherited positivity:** If Q_b>=0 on D_b then for every a<b, Q_a is strictly positive on every nonzero vector. Compact-resolvent attainment on D_a further yields an a-dependent strictly positive coercivity margin, not an effective cap-only numerical bound.
