# RPB-108 finite prime regularity supplement

Registered before first load-bearing use, 2026-10-01.

- **Finite prime physical part**: the actual finite sum of translated carrier
  functions with coefficient Lambda(n)/sqrt(n). This includes cutoff and shell
  sets without changing the closed threshold convention.
- **Integral growth**: integrability of the norm against each specified fixed
  exponential weight. This does not mean a pointwise exponential bound.
- **L1 support-gap pairing**: the bound B times the actual L1 mass, where the
  function vanishes on the central interval and the test norm is at most B on
  its exterior. Gaussian decay may be carried by B.

This supplement is additive. It does not construct the archimedean core,
source-domain identification, or full residual.
