# High pole-moment tail (CC48)

At a=53/50, E112 is the first112 physical-orthonormal Legendre modes,
56 per parity, and F112 its physical-orthogonal supported form complement.
The high moment tail is the physical projection of cosh(x/2) (even)
or sinh(x/2) (odd) onto F112. Beta is the squared norm of its ORIGINAL
Q-high form Riesz response, as in CC47, not its physical norm squared
and not an eigenvalue defect. A normalized moment response is r/sqrt(beta).
Small beta need not imply small coupling to this normalized direction.
