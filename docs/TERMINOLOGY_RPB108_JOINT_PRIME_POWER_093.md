# RPB108 joint prime power comparison

At a=93/100, I=(-a,a). T is the sum of the actual positive compressed
translations for n=2,3,4,5, with amplitudes Lambda(n)/sqrt(n), including
both shift orientations. In particular Lambda(4)=log(2). Its quadratic
contribution to the native form is -<h,T h>.

A four-step word chooses four oriented prime translations, with every
prefix position required to remain in I. r_4(x)=(T^4 1_I)(x) is its
nonnegative row mass. M_4 is a rational upper bound on its essential
supremum. The fourth-power Schur comparison is ||T||^4<=M_4.

The walk offsets are stored as integer triples (u,v,w), representing
u log(2)+v log(3)+w log(5). Prime-4 steps change u by two. These are finite
walk coordinates, not divisor multiplicity or independent observations.
