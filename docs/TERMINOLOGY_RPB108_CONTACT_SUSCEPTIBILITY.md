# RPB108 CC40 — Contact parity susceptibility terminology

**Odd pole susceptibility** on a finite cap where the actual pole-free odd form H_a^- is coercive:

    R_o(a)=2 sup_{h in D_a^-, h != 0} |s_a(h)|²/H_a^-(h),
    s_a(h)=integral h(x) sinh(x/2) dx.

This is the norm squared of the actual odd pole functional multiplied by two, in the complete positive-H metric. It is NOT an old-source inverse-gap/shell norm; H includes the entire original archimedean multiplier and every active prime-power translation with both orientations.

**Odd contact criterion:** if H_a^- is coercive, Q_a^- = H_a^- -2|s_a><s_a| is nonnegative iff R_o(a)<=1; strict canonical positivity iff R_o(a)<1. Equality produces a moment-carrying null proportional to the H-Riesz representative of s_a. If H_a^- has a zero-moment kernel instead, this criterion cannot be invoked with an inverse.

**Even constrained minimum** beta_e(a) was defined in the earlier native contact thresholds registry as the extended-real infimum H_a^+(h) over c_a(h)=1. Its stabilization reserve is beta_e(a)+2. A moment-zero null is a separate alternative and is not ruled out by strictness of the scalar reserve alone.

**Finite Ritz scalar:** beta_e^(2), beta_o^(2), and R_o^(2) mean the corresponding variational values on precisely the two NF12 normalized Legendre degrees (0,2) or (1,3). Determinant formulas (CC40) certify their numerical intervals without assuming any full-space operator inversion, coercivity, or contact.
