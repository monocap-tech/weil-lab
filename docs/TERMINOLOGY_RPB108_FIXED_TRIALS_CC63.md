# CC63 terminology: fixed rational near-critical trials

Definitions are additive and precede the CC63 report.

The **fixed rational trial** is p=x-H2 y, where x is an authenticated NF18 rational retained witness and y is an EXACT rational approximation to C2^{-1}B*x. Rationality refers to coefficients in the physical normalized Legendre basis, not monomial coefficients. The trial belongs to the original finite polynomial source domain.

The **measured trial residual** is Q(p,phi_j), j in H2. It is rigorously small but not asserted to be exactly zero. Thus the fixed trial is distinct from the exact form Galerkin lift used in NF21/CC59.

The **complete trial residual source** is r=P_F112 Lp, removing ALL56 retained physical source coordinates in its parity. It includes the measured trial residual and ALL unmeasured high coordinates. The **trial response gate** is ||r||²<kappa Q(p,p). It suffices for positivity on span{x}+F112 and does not certify the collective retained carrier.

The **retained projection center error** is the L2 error from replacing each enclosed Q(e_i,p) by its rational interval midpoint. It pays only this finite physical projection; it is not an error bound for the complete archimedean/prime/pole source construction.
