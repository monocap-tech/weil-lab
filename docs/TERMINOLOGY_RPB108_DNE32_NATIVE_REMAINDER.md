# RPB108 — DNE32 terminology: complete optimized native remainder

Definitions are additive; historical native and source claims remain unchanged.

**Frozen optimized remainder hatY:** DNE31's exact 52-column polynomial packet in each reflection parity, hatY=W52-T4 K. Neither K nor the polynomial frame is changed in DNE32.

**Complete original remainder native matrix hatB:** Q(hatY,hatY), evaluated before infinite high elimination. With A_s=S Q(T4,T4)S, P_s=S Q(T4,W52) and J=S^-1 K, its exact formula is W52*Q_E*W52-P_s*J-J*P_s+J*A_s*J. Every one of its 52-by-52 entries is included.

**Authenticated original retained native matrix Q_E:** the full unshifted original native pairing on the first 112 normalized Legendre modes at a=53/50. Its archived decoded hash is f69019a895cd675e304989cbb8209c90f033264e0aef589d4b1aa3be09cf4c81. An outward rational extraction retains all 3,192 same-parity upper-triangle entries.

**Rational congruence U:** a nonsingular upper-triangular rational matrix selected using midpoint Decimal LDL diagnostics. Only the outward interval enclosure of U*hatB U and its positive Gershgorin margins prove positivity. The diagnostic arithmetic itself proves no sign.

**Native floor relative to raw W mass:** a number beta satisfying hatB>=beta G_W, where G_W=W52*W52 is DNE24's physical raw-remainder mass. This is not a physical floor relative to the much larger mass of hatY. If U*hatB U>=m I, then beta=m/||W52 U||_F^2 is valid by the physical inverse-trace bound.

**Native mixed-border allocation:** DNE31's paid f2 bounds ||S Q(T4,hatY)||^2. With A_s>=q I and hatB>=beta G_W>=beta I, the full mixed native dual norm squared is at most f2/(q beta). Comparing it to (c/(4 kappa))^2 discharges DNE25's native border allocation while preserving its unproved complete source estimate.

**Full finite lifted native family:** the 112-dimensional polynomial trial space spanned by T4 and hatY in both parities. It projects onto all E112 retained coordinates, but it is not E112 itself and does not include every vector in F112. Positivity on this space is not whole-aperture positivity or actual infinite-high unit-response exclusion.

**Finite native physical gap:** a separate bound in the actual physical mass of the finite polynomial family, obtained from exact column masses and the paid native dual border. It is not an all-high physical gap or a bound on the remaining source Gram.

**Remaining complete source obligation:** enclose Gamma_hatY=(P_F112 L_original hatY)*(P_F112 L_original hatY), including every entry and correlation, and prove Gamma_hatY<=(c/2)hatB or another fully paid sufficient complete comparison. Native positivity cannot supply this bound by itself.
