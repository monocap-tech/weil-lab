# RPB-108 terminology — actual positive-real digamma Euler anchor

- Positive-real normalized shift: actual Re ψ(N+x)-log N, proved to tend
  to zero for every real x>0 from log-convex Gamma bounds.
- Regularized real Euler term: 1/(n+1)-1/(x+n). Its norm is at most
  (|x-1|/min(x,1))/(n+1)², giving independent absolute summability.
- Actual real-axis Euler anchor: Re ψ(x)=-γ+∑' n,[1/(n+1)-1/(x+n)],
  proved from actual recurrence, actual Gamma bounds and the harmonic limit.

This is an identity for the real part at positive real arguments. Complex
analytic identification and cancellation on the source line remain separate.
Historical wording is immutable; no Gauss integral representation is assumed.
