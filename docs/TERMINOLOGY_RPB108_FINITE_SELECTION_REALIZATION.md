# RPB108 terminology: finite-selection effective positive realization

Introduced with REFLECTED_PACKET_BRIDGE_108_FINITE_SELECTION_EFFECTIVE_POSITIVE_REALIZATION_20261006.md.

- **Actual positive carrier H_+:** the closed range of the complete normalized actual positive analysis P_0 on D_a, with its coefficient Hilbert norm.
- **Kernel-separating finite selection:** finite actual negative divisor coordinates s for which R=Pi_s N_0 is injective on the full native kernel K_a. This is a rank condition, not a multiplicity count. Some such selection exists at every fixed nonnegative actual window; agreement with a prescribed packet is separate.
- **Effective background covariance G_s:** A_a+R^*R=P_0^*P_0-B^*B on D_a. It is the native form with the selected negative energy added back.
- **Effective positive synthesis S:** P_0^*(I-T_B^*T_B)^(1/2), with T_B=B P_0^(-1), the actual WD-T10 residual-budget construction.
- **Selected compensator C:** -S^(-1)R^* when selection makes G_s coercive. Its negative synthesis is R^*=-SC; its complete two-channel defect is A_a.

A full native null vector is not a G_s-null vector: for nonzero k in K_a and a separating selection, G_s k=R^*Rk and its effective-background energy is positive. All adjoints and inverses here use D_a and coefficient Hilbert norms; they do not claim an unrestricted physical L2 inverse or raw Green coefficients.
