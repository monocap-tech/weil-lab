# RC182 terminology: materialized psi carrier and four-source inverse

- **Response source family S:** (P0,P2,f0,psi0), in the even sector. This expands RC180's three-source family. Its relative inverse width is not a like-for-like precision history against the old family.
- **Trial basis T:** (P0,P2,f0,q,psi0), where q is RC179's exact rational finite polynomial. The coefficient matrix is five-by-four. The response source family and trial basis remain distinct.
- **Materialized Cpsi carrier:** the historical RC154 finite polynomial through degree 16382, its 25 retained output-tail eta moments, and half the input tail above degree 8190. The finite center/radius digest and analytic moment intervals must reproduce exactly.
- **Complete canonical psi action:** psi0+Cpsi_trial-r D^-1 psi0, with exact analytic negative-r coefficients and RC181's full regular zero-carrier error. Its inherited endpoint and regular errors remain paid.
- **Full residual tail:** the source psi tail and all represented action tails above degree 16382. The negative-r diagonal coefficient varies with n and is bounded by a centered interval; it is not frozen at a midpoint without paying its radius.
- **Four-source inverse response:** the actual S* D M^-1 S for M=D^-1 L. Signed residuals and correlated action errors cover every real and complex mixture of S.
- **Acceptance ratio:** the selected inverse width divided by the local half-Gram threshold 1/2, evaluated on the new four-source Gram. Gate closure remains separate from source coverage and Stage I.
- **Scope:** the native seven-source definitions remain unchanged. Four-source coverage contains the native P0,f0,psi0 plus auxiliary P2; it does not supply the full native seven-source or original augmentation inverse.
