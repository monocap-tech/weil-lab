## Post-fourteenth-all-internal excess

Use z=e-e101=b-35delta13, with b=e-e100 as in GERM-101. Retain d=delta13 and tau=tau14=rho12-35delta13. Here d is a residual length, not GERM-91's parameter.

Define s15=d-tau, t15=2tau-d, rho16=s15-4t15, and cut16=t15-rho16. The exact physical lengths satisfy 0<rho16<t15<s15<tau<d and 4t15<s15<5t15. The new exclusion interval is 0<z<=s15; the new fourteenth/fifteenth formula interval is 0<z<=tau.

## GERM-102-local fourteenth maps

Using GERM-101-local thirteenth H1/J0/J1 inputs, define A=J1 H1^34, D=J0 H1^35, E=J1 H1^35. E is the preceding stopping word. Products act rightmost first.

## GERM-102-local fifteenth maps

The next H/J names are different matrices at a different level:

```math
H_0=AD,\quad H_1=AE,\quad J_0=AD^2,\quad J_1=ADE,\quad J_2=AE^2.
```

They act on K15=(0,s15), whose induced map is negative-t15 translation modulo s15. H maps have two fourteenth steps; J maps have three. H1 is elliptic. J2 is absent from the exclusion interval but is licensed by the larger formula domain.

## Complete sixteenth returns

K16=(0,t15) has positive-rho16 translation modulo t15, with return times four/five. For z<=t15 the four words are H0^(N-1)Ji, i=0,1, N=4,5. For t15<=z<=s15 the nine words are H1^q H0^(N-q-1)J1, N=4,5, 0<=q<N. Two products are shared between these fixed-parameter regimes.

The integer charts are C_low=[[50,50],[-72,-71]] and C_high=[[500,-1000],[-708,1413]]. Their common factor 7 applies per complete sixteenth return, not per original rotation step. A chart is fixed along each orbit.
