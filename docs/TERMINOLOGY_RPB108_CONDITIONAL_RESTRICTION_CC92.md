# CC92 conditional physical restriction terminology

Definitions are additive; historical wording is unchanged.

* **Retained six-direction space E6:** span of the original retained x,w,u32 in each parity. These components are exactly orthogonal and nonzero within each parity.
* **Lifted packet map V:** the three original lifted vectors per parity actually used by NF37–NF46. NF37 subtracts a frozen NF36 high polynomial from the older NF35 lifts; the retained components remain unchanged.
* **Restriction E6+F:** the six retained directions together with the entire original high space F. Replacing a lift by that lift plus a vector of F preserves this restriction.
* **Original high coupling R:** P_F L_original V, with complete physical source Gram Gamma=R*R from the actual NF37 selected family.
* **Conditional physical gap:** a positive rational g for Q_original(h) >= g ||h||_L2^2 on E6+F, conditional on A >= (207/1000)I and the inherited original block/form-domain attachments.
* **Exact parity join:** the even and odd restrictions are orthogonal for the original physical mass and Weil form. Their gap constants join by taking the smaller value.

This restriction omits the other 106 retained dimensions and their mixed coupling. The physical gap is conditional; it does not change the highest unconditional whole-domain aperture 21/20 or discharge background transport.
