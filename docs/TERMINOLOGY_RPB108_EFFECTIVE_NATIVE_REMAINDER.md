# Effective native remainder envelope (CC37)

Retain CC33's normalized Q=E_log+<i_t h,R_B i_t f>, and
r_arch(xi)=Re psi(1/4+i*pi*xi)-log pi-log(e+|xi|).
An effective remainder envelope is a proved numerical upper bound
for ||R_B||, including every active prime-power shift pair and both
Hermitian cross poles. It is not a physical L2 representation of full Q.

CC37 proves |r_arch|<8 globally and ||R_B||<20 for B<=21/20.
These constants bound absolute native components. They do not contain
an old source defect factor and do not certify a larger positive aperture.
