# RPB108 subcritical gain and first-derivative terminology

Registered 2026-10-06 before the companion theorem.

- `s`: physical support enlargement above a hypothetical actual nonnegative contact, not the fractional regularity exponent.
- `alpha in (0,1/2)`: fixed global fractional order in FRACTIONAL_NULL_REGULARITY.
- `L_s=log(1/s)`, `M_s(h)`: full two-collar squared residual mass; `e_s(h)`: actual effective inverse-residual gain.
- `f(s)=trace_E Pi_E[Lambda(c+s)-Lambda(c)]Pi_E`: unnormalized total finite contact gain, with `f(0)=0`; the sequencewise target concerns `f(s)/s^2`.
- First derivative: right derivative of the finite response or f at zero. It is different from a derivative of the compensator in its fixed Hilbert carrier.
- Quadratic compensator control: bounded `||(C_(c+s)-C_c)|E||/s`, which is equivalent to a quadratic matrix gain bound and is stronger than a zero first derivative of f.
- Rate controls below are positive scalar functions, not actual arithmetic response matrices or instantiated null vectors.
