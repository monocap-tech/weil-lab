# RPB108 stationary extension terminology

CC28, 2026-10-08; original normalized Q=P*P-N*N.

- **Simultaneous translation invariance:** Q(tau_r h,tau_r f)=Q(h,f),
  with tau_r h(x)=h(x-r), when both supported vectors are admitted.
  It does not assert that translation stays in an old support domain.
- **Stationary correlation:** q_h(r)=Q(h,tau_r h), on the actual support
  range where the form is defined; it is continuous in r on a fixed cap.
- **Local positive-definite translation kernel:** q_h(v-u) on centers
  u,v in an interval I, when all tau_u h belong to an old positive domain.
  Its positivity follows only for those centers and their combinations.
- **Prescribed extension:** the actual native form on larger supports,
  with its full prime terms and poles. It is already fixed by arithmetic.
- **Positive-definite extension:** any globally positive-definite function
  agreeing with a local continuous correlation. Its existence does not
  identify it with the prescribed native continuation.
- **Frozen native correlation:** q_h^B(r) built from the fixed finite-cap
  scalar symbol b_B and both poles. It agrees with actual q_h(r) wherever
  h and tau_r h lie in cap B. Outside that range it is a diagnostic formula,
  not a claimed full native continuation.
- **Analytic uniqueness mechanism:** attempt to identify a positive
  extension with the prescribed correlation by analytic continuation.
  CC28 proves q_h^B cannot be real analytic at0 for any nonzero compact
  physical vector of finite canonical logarithmic energy.

This is a property of native physical correlation. It is not a theorem
about moments of negative-source critical coordinates at zero ordinates.
A finite-dimensional critical OUTPUT space does not change this distinction.
