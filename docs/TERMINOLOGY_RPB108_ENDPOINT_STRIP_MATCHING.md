# RPB108: lawful strip matching at the critical scale

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Additive definitions.

Use I_a, K_a, q_h, L_epsilon and B_h from ENDPOINT_MASS_PROMOTION.

- chi_(R,epsilon) is the zero-extended indicator of (a-epsilon,a); the left indicator is its reflection. Each is a canonical logarithmic-domain test.
- f_R(v)=h(a-v), 0<v<2a, and J_R(epsilon)=integral_epsilon^(2a) f_R(v)/v dv. J_R is a finite truncated SIGNED integral, not an assumed infinite inverse moment.
- eta_R(epsilon)=sqrt(L_epsilon)/epsilon times integral_0^epsilon |f_R(v)|dv.
- rho_R(epsilon)=1/(epsilon sqrt(L_epsilon)) times integral_0^epsilon |q_h(a+t)|dt. The left versions use reflected coordinates.
- A common vanishing sequence for a finite kernel means epsilon_n->0 with B_K(epsilon_n)->0. It need not initially give a full limit or separate pointwise traces.

The source and physical vectors are not changed. The strip is a lawful same-window TEST; no prescribed finite source packet is treated as physically realizable.
