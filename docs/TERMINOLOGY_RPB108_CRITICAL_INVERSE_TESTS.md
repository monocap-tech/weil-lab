# RPB108 terminology: critical Fredholm inverse tests

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Additive global/F4 registry.

- Write r(xi)=1+|xi| and w(xi)=log(e+|xi|).
- Ecrit is the global Fourier space with norm squared integral r/w |uhat|^2. It is the Hilbert distributional dual of the previously defined Zcrit, whose norm squared is integral w/r |ghat|^2.
- Xcrit uses the inhomogeneous equivalent norm integral r w |uhat|^2. On supported canonical vectors this is the same critical physical space already registered using |xi|w at high frequency.
- Zlow is the distributional space with norm squared integral 1/(r w) |ghat|^2, dual to Xcrit. The actual logarithmic multiplier maps Zcrit to Zlow and Xcrit to Ecrit.
- A critical inverse-test premise at a fixed actual window means: for each f in C_c^infinity(-a,a) physically L2 orthogonal to the full native K_a, there exists a supported canonical solution u in D_a intersect Xcrit of Q_a(u,v)=<f,v>_2 for every v in D_a. No uniform critical inverse bound or prescribed choice of u is included.
- A critical distributional native solution is a compactly supported g in Zcrit satisfying the exact interior equation m_a(D)g+p_g=0. Its pole moments retain the compact-distribution definition. It is not presumed L2.
- Translations, dilation and mollification used to approximate a test change that test vector only; a promoted distribution remains the same physical distribution.
