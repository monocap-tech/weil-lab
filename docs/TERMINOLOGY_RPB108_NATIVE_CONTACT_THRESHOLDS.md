# RPB108 — Native contact null equation and pole-moment thresholds

On a finite physical cap a, D_a is the canonical logarithmic supported form domain. The **pole-free native form** H_a is the complete original archimedean digamma multiplier minus the complete finite set of original signed prime-power translations, with both shift orientations and all active powers. It excludes only the two Hermitian pole cross terms; it is not a different asserted explicit formula.

Let D_a^+ and D_a^- be the real even/odd subspaces. Define moment functionals c_a(h)=int_{-a}^a h(x)cosh(x/2)dx on D_a^+ and s_a(h)=int_{-a}^a h(x)sinh(x/2)dx on D_a^-. The original parity forms are Q_a^+=H_a^++2c_a² and Q_a^-=H_a^--2s_a². Complex Hermitian polarization extends these identities.

The **constrained pole-free minima** are b_+(a)=inf{H_a(h,h):h in D_a^+, c_a(h)=1} and b_-(a)=inf{H_a(h,h):h in D_a^-, s_a(h)=1}. The infima are extended real, possibly -infinity, and no attainment/inverse is presupposed. With the complete support-compatible native form, both b_+(a),b_-(a) are nonincreasing as the aperture grows. The zero-moment channels are contained in these infima, not deleted.

A **contact null** is a nonzero h in D_a with Q_a>=0 on D_a and Q_a(h,h)=0. A **moment-carrying contact** has nonzero appropriate parity moment; a **moment-zero contact** has zero moment and is also null for the pole-free H_a. A **native IMS null identity** is the signed kernel equality for Q_a(phi h) induced by Q_a(h,phi²h)=0 and real bounded smooth multiplier phi.
