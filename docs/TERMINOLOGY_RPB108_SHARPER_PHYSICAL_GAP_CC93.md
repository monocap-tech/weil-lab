# CC93 sharper physical gap terminology

Definitions are additive; earlier certificates and wording remain unchanged.

* **Inverse-trace bound:** for a positive definite packet matrix K, K >= [trace(K^-1)]^-1 I. A paid upper bound for the inverse trace supplies a coordinate coercivity lower bound.
* **Actual lifted-mass trace bound:** sum of squared rational upper norms for the NF37 lifted packet vectors. It bounds the physical Gram operator without requiring its mixed mass entries.
* **Sharpened conditional physical gap:** the CC92 completion-square gap recomputed with the inverse-trace coordinate bound and actual lifted-mass bound, on the same original six-direction-plus-F restriction.

The remaining-high floor, original form/domain attachments and restriction identity are inherited from CC92. A larger gap on this restriction is not certification of additional retained directions or whole aperture 53/50.
