# RPB108 NF62: exterior inverse-response shell

**Exterior shell.** The new selection uses even degrees 438 through 500 or
odd degrees 437 through 499. Every inherited high column is supported at
degrees at most 436. Consequently the exterior direction is exactly
orthogonal to all inherited physical columns by disjoint support.

**Frozen selection.** Reconstruct the remaining inverse response of the
same fixed target using NF60's direct complete projected source and NF61's
high minorant. Down-round exterior midpoint coordinates to denominator
`10^100`, then normalize using a rational norm upper bound. The old-high
projection coefficients are exactly zero. Midpoint selection is not proof
of gain or sign.

**Moment capacity.** With regular N320 and maximum physical degree 500,
the largest source-polynomial square requires moments through degree 1640.
The existing degree-1700 producer and degree-1800 independent moments cover
it. The frozen selection uses 800 digits; its proof uses 1000 digits and
the independent route uses 1100 digits. Endpoint logarithms, log squares,
signed pole and all prime-cell mixed products retain complete paid analytic
enclosures. Historical 800/900-digit enclosures are promoted exactly, with
all physical errors retained. Only the new analytic calculation is rebuilt.

**Correlated gain.** A positive Schur denominator gives a nonnegative
rank-one inverse-reaction gain. It is strictly positive only when its
certified lower endpoint is positive. A signed residual interval containing
zero does not prove that the actual gain is zero. The validator records
the exact gain interval and strictness flag separately.

**Inherited scalar source norm.** Keep NF60's direct complete-source norm
interval unchanged. Intersect the new grouped inverse target enclosure
with NF61's best target interval plus the correlated gain and with the new
full entrywise enclosure. Every old source error and infinite remainder
remains paid.

**Scope.** This is one certified exterior high direction per parity, not
an exhaustive analysis of all exterior or infinite high directions. Scalar
minorant signs and full joint-matrix signs are separate from the original
whole-form gate. The certified whole-aperture anchor remains `21/20`;
whole `53/50`, RH, F4, Lean and all-aperture positivity remain open.
