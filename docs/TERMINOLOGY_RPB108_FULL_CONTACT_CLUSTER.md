# RPB108: full contact spectral cluster

Additive definitions, 2026-10-07. Distinguish the physical self-adjoint native operator L(a), associated to Q_a with physical L2 mass, from the bounded canonical form Riesz operator A(a) used after dilation to D_1. Physical dilation U_a is unitary on mass and transports Q_a to q_a on D_1.

Let J:D_1->L2(-1,1) be the compact mass inclusion, M=J^*J, and choose a fixed positive shift beta large enough that B(a)=A(a)+beta M is uniformly coercive near the fixed aperture. The dilated physical resolvent is

    R(a)=J B(a)^(-1) J^*.

It is the inverse of the dilated physical L(a)+beta, not A(a)+beta I. Kernel cluster rank and negative index always count physical native spectral directions. Convergence of projections on the fixed mass space uses dilation; convergence on the ambient physical line also uses finite-rank strong continuity of dilation.

A cluster frame is a physical orthonormal basis of the small negative spectral SUBSPACE; it need not diagonalize the operator. Its full source energy residual is a finite negative Hermitian matrix, not an asserted common scalar eigenvalue.
