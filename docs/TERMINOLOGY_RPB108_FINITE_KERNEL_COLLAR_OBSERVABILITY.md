# RPB108 terminology: finite-kernel collar observability

- **Kernel collar map:** B_a,b h=q_b(h) restricted to (-b,-a) union (a,b), with unchanged physical h and exact finite prime-shell custody.
- **Physical observation budget:** (sum ||u_i||_L2^2)^(1/2) for the smooth collar tests. Unbounded rescaling is not uniform stability.
- **Fixed-enlargement stability floor:** a positive lower observation bound for fixed a,b on the finite-dimensional endpoint kernel; it may degenerate as b decreases to a.
- **Negative index:** maximal complex dimension of a strictly negative native subspace, used at one fixed window without a global finite-index assumption.
