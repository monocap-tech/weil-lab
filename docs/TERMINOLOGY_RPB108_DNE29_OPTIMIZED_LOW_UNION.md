# RPB108 — DNE29 terminology: optimized low-mode union

All definitions are additive; historical wording and certificates are preserved.

**NF41 base columns B:** the three exact frozen polynomial columns recorded in NF41's boundary/source certificates. They are NF38's joined base columns after the frozen old functional, before its selected two-direction joint correction.

**Optimized selected columns T3:** T3_i=B_i-z0 C_(0,i)-z1 C_(1,i), where z0,z1 are NF38's exact rational high corrections and C is its selected rational joint functional matrix. Both corrections are supported in F112, so all retained components are unchanged.

**Optimized low-mode frame T4:** (e0,T3) in even parity and (e1,T3) in odd parity. Its retained span is the same Z8 as DNE23. Its high lifts differ from DNE23's frame, so native low pairings and native energy orthogonal remainder coordinates must be paid for this frame.

**Fresh low native row p:** the three signed original values Q(low,T3_i). DNE29 encloses them by pairing the reconstructed original low source with each complete selected polynomial, and independently checks symmetry by reconstructing the selected source and pairing with the low mode.

**Source replay:** primary order N=360 at 600 directed Decimal digits; replay order N=400 at 620 digits. The reverse source action also uses N=400 and 620 digits. Degree-64 pole polynomials, exact endpoint logarithmic moments and both orientations of all six clipped prime powers are retained.

**Optimized source credit c:** a rational c>0 with (kappa-c)Q4-D_tau>0 on this optimized frame, where kappa=11/25 and D_tau=diag((1+1/tau)P0,(1+tau)Gamma3). This implies Gamma4<(kappa-c)Q4 without evaluating the three low-source Gram crosses. It is not an invariant of the retained span alone.

**Exact selected-column physical mass:** the sum of squares of its exact rational normalized Legendre coefficients. An outward rational root bounds its norm. This pays the full polynomial, including its changed high tail.

**Optimized native energy orthogonal remainder Y:** W-T4 Q4^-1 Q(T4,W), with W from DNE24 and Q4 the original native energy matrix of the optimized frame. The conditional source test is c B_Y-Gamma_Y>0. The earlier frame's Y is not substituted here, and this new Y and its complete source Gram are not computed in DNE29.

**Matrix-box verification:** the DNE28 outward interval LDL and independent exact vertex/Sylvester methods retain their definitions. All uncertain signed entries are enclosed; positive vertices certify the complete symmetric matrix box by convexity.
