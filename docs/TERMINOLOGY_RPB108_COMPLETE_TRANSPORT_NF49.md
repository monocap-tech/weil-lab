# NF49 terminology — complete sources and the joint high minorant

All physical vectors, native inputs and NF10–NF48 records retain their original meaning.

- **P, T53, F112:** the unchanged three joined physical columns, exact remaining-53 frame, and original physical high complement from NF47–NF48.
- **R, G:** complete original sources `PF L P` and `PF L T53`. Source Grams integrate every high direction through the full physical L2 profiles; they are not finite Legendre projections.
- **Remaining source closure:** the 1,431 symmetric `G*G`, 159 signed `G*R`, and 424 even / 371 odd signed `G*PF L H` entries, with original physical errors and analytic infinite remainders paid. The signed `G*U` data are also assembled using paid `Q(T53,H)`.
- **H, U:** the unchanged eight even / seven odd high physical columns and their original surplus sources `U=(A-kappa)H`, where `A=Q|F112` and `kappa=207/1000`.
- **C, N:** `C=H*(A-kappa)H > 0`, `N=C+U*U/kappa > 0`. These are the existing high-minorant matrices, not the remaining native matrix C53.
- **A0:** the same joint high minorant `kappa I+U C^-1 U* <= A`. Its inverse upper bounds `A^-1`; it is not the exact original inverse.
- **Joint reaction ceiling:** for `Xi=(R,G)`, `Xi* A^-1 Xi <= Xi*Xi/kappa-W N^-1 W*/kappa^2`, with `W=Xi*U`. Every block uses this one high minorant.
- **Joint original Schur lower:** the full 56-by-56 original native Gram of `(P,T53)` minus that joint reaction ceiling. Positivity of this lower matrix in both parities suffices for whole-aperture positivity; completing its input data alone does not.
- **Joint lower-bound rejection:** an exact fixed rational vector has a negative upper endpoint for the lower matrix. This rejects the selected minorant certificate. It does not certify a negative original Weil value.
- **Independent cell-first validation:** a fresh source reconstruction, a different moment cutoff, and integration of each cell's combined polynomial source before its logarithmic cross terms. All source entries, physical error payments, inverse algebra and reported signs are checked independently of the NF49 certifier.

The aggregate NF49 checkpoint records the verified whole-aperture outcome. Individual parity certificates do not assert a two-parity whole-aperture result. RH, F4, all-aperture continuation and Lean require their own closure.
