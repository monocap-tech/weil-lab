# DNE22 terminology — remaining probe plane and loading boundary

Definitions are additive and do not amend historical claims.

**Remaining probe u.** NF32's frozen rational retained vector, obtained
from a native inverse-column selector and then certified independently.
It lies exactly in E112 intersect span(x,w)-perp. It is not asserted to
be an eigenvector, critical vector, or actual high minimizer.

**Probe retained plane Zprobe.**
span(x_even,w_even,u_even,x_odd,w_odd,u_odd). It has dimension six,
distinct from DNE21's six-dimensional plane which contains e0,e1.

**Complete three-column lower form.** With original lift columns
T=(v,t,u), source R=P_F L_original T and complete Gram Gamma=R*R,
U=Q(T,T)-Gamma/kappa is a LOWER FORM for the true retained Schur form.
Its signed mixed entries are not true inverse-response covariances.

**Unchanged remaining loading.** On NF31's entire 54-dimensional raw
remaining background Tremaining, loading is the largest eigenvalue of
A_T^-1/2 G*G A_T^-1/2 divided by the selected scalar high floor kappa,
where G=P_F L_original Tremaining. CC74's single native high row gives
a certified lower bound. Changing kappa rescales this same object.

**Collective estimator rejection.** Loading greater than one implies
that A_T-G*G/kappa cannot be positive definite. This rejects the
specified unlifted scalar-floor estimator, not the actual original
Schur form A_T-G*C^-1 G, and not a new lifted trial family.

**Eight-direction union.** Adding e0,e1 to Zprobe gives dimension eight.
It contains both DNE21 and DNE22 retained planes. Its mixed positivity
is not certified by either separate result.
