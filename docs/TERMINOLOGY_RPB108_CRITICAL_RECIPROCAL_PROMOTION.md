# RPB108: critical reciprocal derivative promotion

Definitions precede the companion theorem.

- Native full kernel K_a: the actual full mixed weak-null space in the canonical supported logarithmic form domain D_a, with unchanged zero-extended physical vector.
- Critical space Xcrit: integral (1+|xi|)log(e+|xi|)|hhat|^2 finite. Critical derivative space Zcrit: squared Fourier weight log(e+|xi|)/(1+|xi|).
- Actual residual q_v=m0(D)v-T_full v+p_v. The complete frozen prime set and actual two-moment pole are retained. Residuals are used locally, where the pole is a smooth function; a compact pole extension may be chosen without changing pairings on the fixed compact supports.
- Pole-free Dirichlet operator B_a: the self-adjoint operator of the closed form integral (m0-t_a)|vhat|^2 on D_a. It is not the full native Riesz operator A, the coercive effective covariance or a prescribed packet.
- Shifted jump symbol psi_a=m0-t_a-mu_a, where tau_a=2 sum Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) and mu_a=m0(0)-tau_a. The exact Euler kernel and actual prime weights make psi_a nonnegative and the exponent of a positive convolution semigroup.
- Inverse test u: any canonical Fredholm solution of Q_a(u,v)=<f,v>_2 with f smooth compact in I_a and physically orthogonal to K_a. It need not belong to Xcrit or H1.
- Critical physical Hardy tail E_h(epsilon): sum over both endpoints of integral_0^epsilon log(1/v)|h(endpoint-inward v)|^2 dv/v. It tends to zero for h in Xcrit.
- Matched residual modulus omega_h(epsilon): essential supremum of |q_h| within exterior distance epsilon of either endpoint. For h in K_a intersect Xcrit, the existing continuous drive and ordinary Carleman matching give omega_h(epsilon)->0; the residual equals zero inside.
- Regularized reciprocal identity: for h_epsilon=phi_epsilon*h, phi smooth compact supported in (-1,1), real/even, the exact full-line identity is <q_(h_epsilon'),u>=<h_epsilon',q_u>. Neither side is silently support-projected.
- Critical promotion conclusion: K_a intersect Xcrit=K_a intersect H1, and for every member its global derivative is the same distribution as an element of K_a. This is an analytic theorem with a pinned external boundary input, not a Lean certificate.
- Remaining endpoint obligation: finiteness of the actual positive source order-one trace on the entire hypothetical contact kernel, equivalently whole-kernel Xcrit membership. A single regular vector is insufficient unless the kernel is one-dimensional.
