# RPB108 averaged native localization

Registered 2026-10-06 (America/Los_Angeles); global/F4 lane.

Choose a real nonnegative chi in C_c^infinity((-a,a)) with ||chi||_2=1. Its real autocorrelation c_chi(s)=integral chi(x+s)chi(x)dx satisfies 0<=c_chi<=1, c_chi(0)=1, and c_chi(s)=0 for |s|>=2a.

For actual supported logarithmic h, h_z(x)=chi(x-z)h(x). L_chi(h)=integral_z Q(h_z)dz is the averaged actual local energy; E_chi(h)=L_chi(h)-Q(h) is the signed full localization defect.

I_s(h)=integral h(x+s) conjugate(h(x))dx. The exact native dictionary uses k(s)=exp(-s/2)/(1-exp(-2s)), ell_n=log n and b_n=Lambda(n)/sqrt(n). E_arch, E_prime and E_pole are the three signed defects in the accompanying note, not independent positivity certificates.

C_arch(chi)=2 integral_0^infinity k(s)(1-c_chi(s))ds is the sharp physical-L2 norm bound for the archimedean defect. The full signed defect need not have the same bound. Long-separation prime/pole cancellation is an independent arithmetic problem.

Translation of a localized test changes its physical vector. The localization construction is not retained same-vector coefficient transport or enlarged nullity.
