# RPB108 terminology: critical interior gain and continuous edge drive

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC. Additive global/F4 registry.

- r(xi)=1+|xi| and w(xi)=log(e+|xi|), as in the critical derivative registry.
- Critical interior logarithmic gain is the conclusion that chi h has finite integral r w^3 |Fourier(chi h)|^2 for every chi in C_c^infinity(-a,a), when h belongs to the actual K_a intersect Xcrit.
- A smooth-cutoff commutator is [m0(D),chi], with chi a smooth compact physical multiplier. It is distinct from the discontinuous support-indicator commutator [m0(D),P_a].
- A localized edge test is v=chi_R h supported in a short interval ending at the original physical endpoint. Only v is changed; h is not transported or dilated.
- Continuous edge drive means the existing combined actual A_h(u) has a continuous right extension to u=0. It does not assert that its Carleman summand has a finite inverse moment or boundary value.
