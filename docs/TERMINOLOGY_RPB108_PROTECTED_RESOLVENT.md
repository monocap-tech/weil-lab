Definitions are additive to CC3, CC4, CC6 and NF65.

- Signed retained comparison Qeven: Q_N plus CC6's retained M_(N,K), with EVEN K, WITHOUT CC6's additional -epsilon mass. NF65 proves Qeven<=Q_original. At N=K=32 its positive defect is bounded by e=|B_66|/[33(129/4)^66]. This is a distinct comparison from CC6's Qminus.
- Actual target source map B: physical F112 projection of the ORIGINAL 112 source columns at a=21/20. Its norm bound imports the saved surrogate bound PLUS the complete source-map allowance; a saved Schur sign failure is not imported as whole negativity.
- Protected comparison complement Ceven: the original F112 restriction of Qeven, with c_even=c-e>0 in physical mass. It is an unbounded logarithmic operator, not the bounded cutoff background F32.
- Shifted complement L: Ceven+c_even I on F112. The shift is only an inverse-construction device in a protected complement; no shift of the ORIGINAL retained Schur or eigenlevel is silently made.
- Resolvent head P_m: sum_(k=0)^m c_even^k L^(-k-1). It is an operator function of the WHOLE shifted complement. Each L inverse must be evaluated or independently enclosed; a finite coefficient matrix is not its definition.
- Resolvent tail r_m: 1/[c_even 2^(m+1)], giving 0<=Ceven^(-1)-P_m<=r_m I. This is an evaluated whole inverse error budget, not a computation of its head.
- Head Schur U_m: Aeven-Beven^* P_m Beven. It is an UPPER bound for the exact completed comparison Schur. Subtracting r_m Beven^*Beven gives a lower bound.
