# RPB108: critical inverse-log native-drive control

Registered 2026-10-07 before first load-bearing use.

- Reuse a=97/100, epsilon=1/1000, delta=1/10000, the actual prime set, the smooth cutoff chi, the reflected bumps b_R,b_L and the genuine boundary compatibility functionals L_R,L_L from ACTUAL_DRIVE_CUSP_20261007. The compatibility matrix B is the same matrix with eigenvalues >230. It is not a null-response matrix or a retained packet.
- L(v)=log(1/v) on the short collar. Replace the prior power cusp by h_R(a-v)=L(v)^(-4)chi(v), v>0, and replace the interior cusp by h_I(x0+u)=L(u)^(-3)chi(u), u>0, with x0=a-log 2. Each cusp is zero on the other side and has continuous value zero at its endpoint.
- Let h0=h_R+h_I, c=B^(-1)(L_R(h0),L_L(h0))^T, and h=h0-c_R b_R-c_L b_L. The actual drive, source coordinates and residual q_h=m_a(D)h+p_h belong to this unchanged constructed physical vector.
- Xcrit has squared Fourier weight r w, r=1+|xi| and w=log(e+|xi|). The stronger log^3 half-order space has squared weight r w^3. These weights are distinct from the earlier pure-log spaces X_k.
- C_R(u)=integral h(a-v)/(u+v)dv, M_R=integral h(a-v)/v dv and r_R=A_R,h-C_R/2. The inverse moments of this control are absolute; this does not assert absolute moments for arbitrary actual null vectors.
- w2=Lambda(2)/sqrt(2)=log(2)/sqrt(2). The critical slow-cusp exterior coefficient is 1/6-w2<0; its interior residual coefficient is 7/6>0. They refer to different physical sides of the boundary.

Scope: a positive actual-native carrier control at the critical Sobolev threshold. It is neither an eigenvector nor a full-null vector, and does not disprove critical promotion on K_a.
