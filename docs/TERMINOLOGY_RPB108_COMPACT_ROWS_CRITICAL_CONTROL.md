# RPB108: compact rows at the critical height

Date: 2026-10-07 UTC.

This terminology applies to the artificial supported-profile control in the linked research note, not to a replacement actual divisor or native form.

- **Triangle f:** the height-one triangle supported on [1/16,3/16], with peak at 1/8. Its integral is 1/16 and the total variation of its distributional second derivative is 64.
- **Sparse heights:** H_j=2^(2^(j+4)), j>=1; H_(j+1)=H_j^2.
- **Physical series:** h=sum_j c_j exp(-i H_j x)f(x), c_j=(j H_j)^(-1/2). The series converges on one fixed physical support.
- **Artificial rows:** one raw half-pair at each (theta,beta)=(H_j,3/8), using exactly p=integral h exp(i theta x)cosh(beta x) and n=integral h exp(i theta x)sinh(beta x). These are not actual zeta zeros. Symmetry partners can be added at constant multiplicity cost.
- **Critical negative moment:** sum_j H_j |n_j(h)|^2. Compactness of the unweighted row map on the logarithmic domain does not imply its finiteness.

The exact full-native equation remains Q_a(h,v)=0 for every canonical v. No row-count control, compactness assertion or artificial physical vector supplies this equation.
