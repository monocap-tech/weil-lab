# RPB108 NF50 terminology

**Joint source** means the complete physical high source `R alpha+G beta`
for the unchanged three joined columns P and all53 remaining columns T53.
All endpoint logarithms, signed pole terms,13 original prime translation
cells and infinite analytic remainders remain in its certification.

**Appended high direction** means a new exact rational physical polynomial
in F112. NF50 appends one per parity, exactly orthogonal to every inherited
high column. Existing P,T53 and high columns are unchanged.

**Lifted NF49 witness** means its value under the sufficient joint lower
matrix is now strictly positive, independently proved. This settles that
particular obstruction to the lower bound; it does not settle the full gate.

**Rejected enlarged lower bound** means a new exact rational vector has
strictly negative value under that sufficient lower matrix. It is not an
original negative Weil vector or a counterexample to whole positivity.

**Remaining whole gate** means the shared original high-condensed condition
`D-B S^-1 B* >= 0` in both parities. Whole positivity at53/50 remains open;
the highest certified whole-aperture anchor remains21/20. RH,F4 and Lean
remain open.
