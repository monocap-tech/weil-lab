# RPB108 pre-prime-3 aperture terminology

- **Pre-prime-3 aperture:** the specific aperture a=27/50. Its support satisfies 2a<log(3), so the actual native prime set is still {2}. This term does not mean the entire interval up to the activation threshold has a certified full-domain sign.
- **Pre-prime-3 complement certificate:** physical coercivity 1/3 and logarithmic coercivity 9/100 for the twenty-moment complement, uniformly for 1/2<=a<=27/50. It supplies inverse factor 3 and the lawful form-domain lift.
- **Pre-prime-3 native/source data:** the newly computed orthonormal native matrix and scaled interior sources at 27/50, on the same twenty physical vectors. Matrix or source data from 51/100 are not substituted.
- **Pre-prime-3 no-lift estimator:** Q_20-3R_20, a lower bound for the exact lift-corrected Schur form. A negative direction of this estimator does not establish a negative direction of the actual form.

These terms extend earlier definitions additively. Historical claims, Lean source and prior CI claims are unchanged. F4 and FULL TRANSPORT CLOSED remain open.
