# CC70 terminology: finite restriction and collective high loading

These definitions precede their load-bearing use. Historical wording is unchanged.

At aperture 53/50, E=E112, F=E-perp in physical L2, and the original high form C satisfies C>=kappa I, kappa=207/1000. In one parity, x is the unchanged rational retained seed, v is the NF26 corrected trial with retained part x, and W=E intersect x-perp. NF27 proves positivity of the original native form A_W on this complete 55-dimensional space. A_W is a form matrix in a rational, nonorthonormal basis; physical mass is never replaced by coefficient mass.

The **finite lifted restriction** is the original form on span(v)+W. Its retained projection covers the full parity retained space, but its high component is restricted to the one frozen trial correction. The two parity restrictions have total dimension 112; they do not include all F.

Write q=Q(v), c=Q(W,v), r=P_F L v and B=P_F L W. The **finite retained reaction** is R=c* A_W^-1 c. The **floor-normalized collective source loading** is theta=sup over nonzero w in W of ||P_F L w||^2/(kappa Q(w)). The **true-response collective loading** is nu=sup over nonzero w in W of <P_F L w,C^-1 P_F L w>/Q(w). Both are basis independent; nu<=theta. A high inverse denotes the coercive form response, not a bounded full Weil operator on physical L2.

The **robust joint gate** is p+t+u+2sqrt(p t u)<1, where p bounds the seed response divided by q, t bounds R/q and u bounds theta or nu consistently with the same high response estimate. It is sufficient and discards signed mixed correlations. The true-response version can use the floor-based upper bound on p, but failure then concerns that unchanged upper estimate. It is not an evaluation of the true seed response.

An **observed native loading lower bound** uses one original unit high mode e_h and all retained directions together. If g=Q(W,e_h), theta>=g* A_W^-1 g/kappa and nu>=g* A_W^-1 g/Q(e_h). Neither lower bound proves that the actual collective Schur form is indefinite.
