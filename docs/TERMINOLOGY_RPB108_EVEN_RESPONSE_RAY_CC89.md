# CC89 even response-ray terminology

Definitions are additive; historical wording is unchanged.

* **Base joined matrix K6:** NF44's six-source even lower certificate on the fixed three retained coordinates.
* **Seventh response ray:** the rank-one matrix Delta=z z*/b reconstructed from NF45's paid moments by CC88, with b>0. The hypothetical family K6+lambda Delta, lambda>=0, tests the orientation of this matrix response.
* **Effective response tau:** z_last-z_pair*E^-1r when K6=[[E,r],[r*,c]]. This is the response in the negative condensed coordinate after eliminating the leading pair.
* **Leading-coordinate penalty a:** z_pair*E^-1 z_pair. It measures the same response's coupling to the positive leading pair, and is not the physical mass of the high polynomial.
* **Arbitrary-strength ceiling:** tau^2/a when a>0. It is the limiting condensed credit of the hypothetical ray as lambda tends to infinity.
* **Orientation deficit:** (-s)a-tau^2, where s=c-r*E^-1r<0. Strict positivity certifies failure of every nonnegative ray strength.

Hypothetical amplification is not an available physical source operation. Scaling a fixed original polynomial scales its response numerator and denominator equally and leaves Delta unchanged. Failure of a lower-certificate ray is not original Weil negativity.
