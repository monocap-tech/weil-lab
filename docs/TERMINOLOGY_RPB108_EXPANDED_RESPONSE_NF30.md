# NF30 — expanded response correction

Definitions are additive; historical reports remain unchanged.

E, F, kappa, x, v, w and w_h retain their NF29 meanings at a=53/50.

**Additional high correction z:** a fixed rational even polynomial supported on normalized physical Legendre modes e116,e118,...,e180. Its coefficients are selected from reconstructed source coordinates of w_h divided by four and downward rounded to denominator 10^100. The number four is a trial-selection scale, not a certified high inverse or an asserted optimal denominator.

**Expanded response trial t:** t=w_h-z. Its retained component remains exactly w. Changing this high lift changes the fixed trial space, unlike a basis change within the old trial space. No actual infinite-high minimizer is asserted.

**Selected-shell fraction:** the paid sum of original source-coordinate squares on the 33 selected high modes divided by NF29's complete response source square. This fraction reports only that finite shell; it is not a bound on omitted source coordinates or on the inverse-weighted reaction.

**Expanded two-direction sufficient matrix:** U=Q((v,t),(v,t))-Gram(P_F Lv,P_F Lt)/kappa. Strict positive diagonal and determinant certify the retained Schur restriction on span(x,w) including all F. A positive t diagonal alone is not a collective certificate. A negative sufficient determinant or diagonal rejects this fixed estimator, not the original form.

**Four-direction retained Schur restriction:** the span of the original even seed, even retained response, odd seed and odd retained response. Exact reflection parity makes the mixed parity block zero. Positive two-direction Schur blocks in both parities therefore prove positivity on this four-dimensional retained space with the entire original high complement F included. This does not cover the other 108 retained dimensions.

Odd NF29's successful block is preserved, not recalculated with a new trial. Neither parity's two-direction restriction replaces the full 55-dimensional retained background or the whole retained Schur matrix.
