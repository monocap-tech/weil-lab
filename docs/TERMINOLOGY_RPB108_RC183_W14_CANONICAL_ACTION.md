# RC183 terminology: native w14 canonical action

- **Native w14:** RC128's unchanged rational finite polynomial through degree 4094 plus its 15 retained analytic eta moments above that cutoff. New action trials do not redefine this vector.
- **Regular input prefix:** the exact native w14 coefficients through degree 256. The actual omitted finite and analytic input is paid by a full D-energy bound and RC174's regular high-mode smoothing theorem.
- **Complete regular carrier:** the stored degree-768 polynomial for D^-1 Rw14, with finite-kernel projection error and the full actual input/output tails paid.
- **Centered endpoint trial:** the historical RC130 finite Cg polynomial plus half the native analytic w14 input tail. The full-domain positive contraction C=D^-1 h pays the remaining half-norm error. No cutoff commutation is assumed.
- **Negative-r correction:** a paid finite polynomial through degree 4094 plus the exact analytic -r D^-1 native tail. The actual D_n dependence is retained on the whole infinite tail.
- **Complete canonical carrier:** native w14 plus the centered endpoint trial, negative-r correction, and regular trial for M=D^-1 L. Component errors may overlap and are added by the triangle inequality.
- **Local regular contract:** 0.001 relative D-norm error on D^-1 Rw14. Its ratio does not stand in for full canonical action accuracy or an inverse-response gate.
- **Coverage:** the canonical w14 action is now available; its mixed inverse rows and five-source inverse response are not evaluated in RC183. Stage I remains separate.
