# CC71 terminology: collective two-high elimination and finite lifts

These definitions precede their use. Historical wording remains unchanged.

At aperture 53/50, E=E112 is the original physical retained space and F=E-perp. In each parity, x is the unchanged rational seed, W=E intersect x-perp, and H2 is the span of the original first two normalized high Legendre modes (e112,e114 even; e113,e115 odd).

The **joint two-high finite restriction** is the original Weil form on W+H2 (dimension 57 per parity) or E+H2 (dimension 58 per parity), as explicitly specified. These spaces include only two high modes per parity; they do not include all F.

In the rational W basis, A_W is its original native form, K=Q(H2,W), and C2=Q(H2,H2). The **collective two-high Schur form** is A_W-K* C2^-1 K. Its positivity tests all retained-background combinations together. The basis is not orthonormal; physical mass is W*W.

The **two-high collective loading** is the largest generalized eigenvalue of K* C2^-1 K relative to A_W. It is the reaction fraction on the worst retained-background combination after minimizing over H2. It is a lower bound for the full infinite-high true-response collective loading, not an upper bound for that loading.

A **frozen rational background lift** has columns U=W-H2 Z, with Z fixed at denominator 10^100 near C2^-1 K. Its native energy and physical mass are recertified using the fixed rational coefficients. No interval inverse is treated as an exact rational polynomial.

The **measured lift residual** is K-C2 Z, the complete original source pairing of U against H2. This controls only the two measured high coordinates. It does not control leakage into the remaining high space.
