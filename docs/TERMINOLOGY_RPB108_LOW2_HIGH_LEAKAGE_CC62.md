# CC62 terminology: original low-two/high restriction

Definitions precede the CC62 report and leave all earlier terminology intact.

The **low-two carrier** E2 is span{e0,e1} in the physical orthonormal supported Legendre basis at a=53/50. The **original retained carrier** E112 contains modes0 through111. F112 is its physical orthogonal complement with the original canonical form domain and certified high floor kappa=207/1000.

The **uncompensated low-two high-source Gram** is P_low=Gram(P_F112 L e0,P_F112 L e1). It is obtained from the complete original low-two source-square Gram by subtracting the square of its ENTIRE E112 source projection. It is not NF21's two-high compensated P2 and not a whole58-column source Gram.

The **two-low infinite restriction** is the original signed form on E2+F112. It includes every canonical high direction, but excludes the110 retained modes e2 through e111. A certificate on this restriction is not whole-domain positivity at53/50.

The **restricted physical gap** delta means Q(h)>=delta||h||² for all canonical form vectors h in this restriction. It excludes a complete null lying entirely there; it does not assert that an arbitrary critical vector is orthogonal to E2.
