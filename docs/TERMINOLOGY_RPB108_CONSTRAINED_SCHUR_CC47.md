# Moment-constrained Schur gate (CC47)

Fix a real reflection parity sector. E is a finite physical-orthonormal
trial space contained in the supported logarithmic form domain; F is
its physical-orthogonal form-domain complement. C denotes the original
full Weil form restricted to F, assumed physically coercive. W:E->F is
its FORM Riesz response, defined by C(Wx,z)=Q(x,z). It is not presumed
to be a bounded full physical source operator. The parity pole moment
m is c in the even sector and s in the odd sector. Its high form Riesz
vector r satisfies C(r,z)=m(z). Beta=C(r,r), and a(x)=m(x)-m(Wx).
The constrained Schur gate is S+a*a/beta, S(x)=Q(x)-C(Wx,Wx), when
beta>0. When beta=0 use S on ker m|E; no division by zero is allowed.
