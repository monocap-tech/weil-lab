# CC69 terminology: seed restriction and mixed assembly

Definitions precede their use in this additive checkpoint. Historical wording is unchanged.

At aperture 53/50, E=E112 is the retained space, F its physical orthogonal complement, and C is the original high form with C>=kappa I, kappa=207/1000. A high response C^-1 means the inverse of this coercive form; no global boundedness of the full logarithmic operator is asserted.

The **retained Schur form** S is the energy after minimizing over F. For any trial v with retained component x and complete source r_v=P_F L v, S(x,x)=Q(v,v)-<r_v,C^-1 r_v>. The **seed restriction** is S on the span of the two unchanged NF18 rational retained directions, one in each parity. It is not a restriction to all critical eigenvectors.

For a low mode e_j of the same parity as x, the **mixed assembly coefficient** is B=S(e_j,x)=Q(e_j,v)-<r_ej,C^-1 r_v>. An **assembly budget** is the product of rigorous positive diagonal lower bounds A_lower D_lower. The enclosure |B|^2<A_lower D_lower suffices for positivity of this two-direction restriction. With exact diagonals, the determinant condition |B|^2<S(e_j,e_j)S(x,x) is necessary and sufficient.

The **norm-only mixed estimator** bounds |B| by |Q(e_j,v)|+sqrt(P_ej P_v)/kappa, using complete high-source norm squares P. Failure of this sufficient estimator neither proves an indefinite actual Schur form nor proves nonimplication from complete Weil identities.
