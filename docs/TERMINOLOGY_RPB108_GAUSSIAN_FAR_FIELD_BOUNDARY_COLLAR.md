# RPB108 terminology: Gaussian far field and boundary collar

- **Separated far-field action:** the genuine fixed-cutoff action against (1-chi_out) conjugate(K_R*h), separated by d/2 from the physical support. Its direct exponential bound is proved.
- **Boundary-collar action:** the action against (chi_out-chi_in) conjugate(K_R*h), compactly localized around both support endpoints. Under endpoint weak-nullity, the whole action equals this collar action plus the exponentially bounded far field.
- **Pole-admissible Gaussian cutoff test:** a Schwartz Gaussian cutoff test with explicitly proved weighted integrability against exp(|x|/2). The exponentially growing pole is not a generic tempered functional on all Schwartz tests.
