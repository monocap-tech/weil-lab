# Nodal energy balance (CC46)

For an actual real even zero-moment null h=u-v, u=h_+, v=h_-,
A=c(u)=c(v)>0. The positive cross-sign cost is C=-H(u,v)=D(h)/4,
where H is the complete native pole-free form. Nodal support measure
m_u means the Lebesgue measure of {x:u(x)!=0}, modulo null sets; it is
not an interval hull, aperture, boundary trace or old source defect.
r_0(B) is a physical L2 operator-norm envelope for the pole-free
remainder H-E_log on a finite cap B. It includes every active prime
power and both translation orientations, and excludes both pole terms.
