## 1. Explicit aggregate blocks
Let e_j(x)=c_j cos(r_j(x+2)) on I=[-2,2], c_0=1/2, c_j=1/sqrt2 for j>0, r_j=j*pi/4, k_n=2n+1/2. For parity sigma=(-1)^j, the literal endpoint moment from IV245 is

This follows by direct integration in t=2-x, or IV243 hyperbolic moments. For j,k of the same parity the aggregate G_m matrix thus has entries
 2 c_j c_k sum_{n>=m} k_n^2(1-sigma exp(-4k_n))^2/[(k_n^2+r_j^2)(k_n^2+r_k^2)].
In Q_agg use factor 1 in even block and 1/(1+epsilon) in odd block as in IV245. Opposite parity entries vanish for this parity-separated operator (not for the raw unseparated endpoint kernel). It is positive Gram, bounded, and no compactness or uniform rank truncation is assumed.

The rational kernel is generated by the single scalar function S_m(z)=sum_{n>=m}1/(k_n^2+z), z>=0. For z!=w,
 sum k_n^2/[(k_n^2+z)(k_n^2+w)]=(z S_m(z)-w S_m(w))/(z-w).
Diagonal is S_m(z)+z S_m'(z). Uniform convergence of the series and its derivative on finite nonnegative z intervals follows from k_n^-2 and k_n^-4. The exp corrections have the same divided difference with scalar sums weighted by exp(-4k_n) and exp(-8k_n). This is an exact entry receiver and O(M) scalar-function data for M modes, not a certified fast evaluation algorithm. For individual scalar sums an omitted tail from n>=T is <=1/[2(k_T-2)] for T>=1 by decreasing-integrand comparison; for derivatives <=1/[6(k_T-2)^3]. Exponential weights only reduce these bounds.
