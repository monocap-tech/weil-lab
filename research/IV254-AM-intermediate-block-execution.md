## 1. A sharper two-sided Gamma bound
For j>0 write t=r_j/2=pi*j/8, a=1/4 and F(x)=x/(x^2+t^2). From the literal digamma gain series and harmonic limit (IV241),
 Re psi(a+it)=lim_{m->infinity}[log m-sum_{n=0}^{m-1} F(a+n)].
The error between the sum and integral on [a,a+m] is bounded by the total variation of F on that interval: on each unit interval integrate the difference from the left endpoint and bound by its variation. F increases until t and decreases thereafter (or is decreasing throughout); max F=1/(2t), so total variation <=1/t. The integral is (1/2)log(((a+m)^2+t^2)/(a^2+t^2)). Taking the limit gives
 |Re psi(a+it)-(1/2)log(a^2+t^2)|<=1/t.
Subtract log pi, use pi>3 and log(1+x)<=x, to obtain
 log(j/8)-8/(3j) <= gamma(r_j) <= log(j/8)+8/(3j)+2/(9j^2).
This is an original-form diagonal estimate, not a change of source normalization.
