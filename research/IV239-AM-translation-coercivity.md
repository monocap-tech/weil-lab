## Obstruction, including removal of the ground state

For the finite ratios s_n/a, partition the unit cube of their fractional parts into Q^d equal boxes and apply pigeonhole to Q^d+1 multiples; get 1<=q<=Q^d with all distances ||q s_n/a||_Z <=1/Q. As Q grows, q has an unbounded subsequence, since log 3/log 2 is irrational (otherwise 3^r=2^p for positive integers). Thus t=2*pi*q/a diverges and E tends to zero while ||f_t||=||w||. Riemann-Lebesgue gives <f_t,w> ->0. Subtract its orthogonal projection onto w; E unchanged since every internal difference of a constant g is zero. Smooth compactly supported functions are dense in L2(J); approximate each projected function in norm, then subtract a suitable multiple of a fixed smooth compactly supported phi with <phi,w> !=0 to restore exact orthogonality. Boundedness of the square root of E (finite norm of the weighted difference map) makes its energy tend to zero; norms stay bounded away from zero. Hence no uniform positive gap on w-perp even on the original supported Schwartz core. This is not a counterexample to Weil positivity.
