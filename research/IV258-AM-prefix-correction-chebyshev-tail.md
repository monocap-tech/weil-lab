## 3. Chebyshev tail bound on j>=3072

For x in [0,1/4] put t=8x-1, D_k=sqrt(1-q_k/4), r_k=(1-D_k)/(1+D_k). The classical Poisson kernel identity gives exactly

Indeed 1-q_k x=1-q_k/8-(q_k/8)t; with r as defined, the identity (1-r^2)/(1-2rt+r^2)=1+2 sum_{m>=1}r^m T_m(t) yields the expression, including q=0 by continuity. D_k>1/2, |r_k|<1/13 (guarded by exact intervals); |T_m(t)|<=1. Sum signed weights for each coefficient before taking absolute value. This is a Chebyshev-series enclosure of the original rational functions, not interpolation or sampling. Keep 160 terms m=0..159. Interval D by integer sqrt and r by directed arithmetic at S0.
