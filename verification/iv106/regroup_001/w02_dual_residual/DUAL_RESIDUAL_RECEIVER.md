## Theorem (abstract analytic proof; actual attachment conditional)
Let K be a closed subspace of the supported weighted-coordinate Hilbert carrier H, J_K its isometric inclusion into ambient complex L2(dxi), A a bounded self-adjoint actual form operator on H, C its orthogonal compression to K. Let E(x)=Re< x,Cx > for x in K, L(x)=||J_K x||^2 and M(x)=||U J_K x||^2=integral |J_K x|^2/w. Assume c>0, D>=0, w>=1 measurable, c M<=E and L<=E+D M for all x in K. Then alpha=c/(c+D)>0. For 0<theta<=alpha let eta=c-(c+D)theta>=0 and q_theta=theta+eta/w. 
The stronger lower energy identity is
 E - theta L - eta M
 = theta(E+D M-L)+(1-theta)(E-c M)>=0.
Consequently E>=integral q_theta |J_K x|^2. This is a family of lower bounds, not merely alpha L. q_theta is positive and bounded above, with inverse bounded by 1/theta.
For any g in ambient L2 with r=J_K^*g, set d=C^{-1}r (inverse exists by coercivity, or use E12 premise). E(d)=Re<d,r>=Re<J_K d,g>. Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 E(d) <= sqrt(integral q_theta |J_K d|^2) sqrt(integral |g|^2/q_theta)
       <= sqrt(E(d)) sqrt(integral |g|^2/q_theta).
If E(d)=0 the assertion is immediate; otherwise divide by sqrt(E(d)) and square:
 E(C^{-1}J_K^*g) <= integral |g|^2/q_theta.
At theta=alpha, eta=0, this recovers the old alpha^{-1} raw norm bound. For theta<alpha, q_theta-alpha=(alpha-theta)((c+D)/w-1). Thus the new bound strictly improves the old one for nonzero residuals supported where w<c+D and may worsen for high frequencies. A fixed theta for an entire coefficient bank gives a valid Hermitian quadratic matrix; optimizing theta for individual entries or taking a pointwise minimum of the integrands is NOT justified. Minimizing the total bound over globally admissible theta for each whole residual is justified. No empirical frequency support claim.
At theta=0 the bound remains valid whenever integral w|g|^2<infinity by the same Cauchy-Schwarz proof with q=c/w (no bounded inverse of the weight asserted). In particular a raw physical residual g=U^*f=w^{-1/2} Fourier(f) gives E(C^{-1}J_K^*g)<=||f||_physical^2/c. This separates physical approximation errors from general weighted-coordinate action defects. It can be applied to actual IV105C response-column errors if the literal physical approximation and adjoint identity are established. For a decomposition g=g0+U^*f, the square root of error energy is at most ||g0||/sqrt(alpha)+||f||/sqrt(c), by triangle inequality for the C-energy norm (C self-adjoint positive); alternatively use a fixed positive theta weighted norm on the full residual.
